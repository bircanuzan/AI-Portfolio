# 03 | Practical workshop and digital-learning storyboard

## 45-minute live practice: moving from product pitch to buyer value
**Setting:** Fictional NovaLink ConnectHub solution, approved product-card substitute supplied by the facilitator. Audience: 6 learners in 2 triads, remote via breakout rooms or on-site.

| Minutes | Facilitator actions | Learner actions | Deliverables |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | Set learning outcomes and safe-claims rule | Complete quick diagnostic poll | Baseline confidence (not skill) |
| 5–10 | Present a buyer's situation | Identify two assumptions needing clarification | Assumption log |
| 10–18 | Demonstrate open discovery | Write five questions; role-play buyer discovery | Discovery worksheet |
| 18–26 | Model value-mapping to approved claims | Pair 2 pain points with confirmed product outcomes | Value map |
| 26–36 | Run two short role plays | Practice response to interoperability/security objection | Scored observation |
| 36–41 | Correct 2 common failures | Redraft misleading claims and propose escalation | Remediation note |
| 41–45 | Explain reinforcement and assessment | Record next action and peer feedback | Learner commitment |

**Facilitator note:** Assign observer, seller and buyer. Swap roles between rounds. Pause on any fabricated claim; coach to say what is known, what is not, and who verifies it. A Product/Compliance SME must approve real customer-facing content before use.

## Practice scenario
A fictional Dubai-based 120-employee professional-services buyer runs hybrid meetings, has intermittent audio issues and asks for guaranteed zero downtime. Product card includes only **hypothetical** approved features: one-touch room join, admin dashboard and support response targets subject to a contract. The seller must avoid promising zero downtime. Observer uses the 25-point rubric in [05](05_Assessment_Rubric_and_Question_Bank.md).

**Sample high-quality exchange (illustrative):**
- Buyer: “We need guaranteed zero downtime. Can you promise that?”
- Seller: “I can't confirm a zero-downtime guarantee. We can review your expected service levels and ask our service team to validate the available support commitment. May I clarify which room failures matter most?”

## Digital microlearning: 15-minute branching decision
Storyboard for M4; planned content only. Not a built interactive module or real SCORM package.

| Screen | Display | Learner interaction | Correct behaviour | QA criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Buyer asks about data storage | Select "assume" / "verify" | Verify documentation first | Keyboard accessible |
| 2 | Claims register with 3 fictional claim tiers | Sort allowed/conditional/prohibited | Route prohibited claims to specialist | Alt text and text alternative |
| 3 | Security compliance objection | Choose draft reply | Refuse universal guarantee, offer escalation | Immediate rationale, no false claims |
| 4 | Product release-note update | Pick most current approved source | Use dated approved version | Version metadata shown |
| 5 | Knowledge check & recap | 3 decisions + confidence | All critical items correct | Passing requires gate |

**Technical handoff:** SCORM packaging, xAPI event tracking, voice-over recording, video creation and LMS publishing are *production tasks*, not completed deliverables. See [Project 32](../32_SCORM_Learning_Design/) for a separate SCORM QA design.

## Accessibility / inclusion checklist
- Text-first, captions and transcripts for audio/video, keyboard operability, meaningful heading order, adequate contrast, readable text, no timed-only assessment.
- Record timezone accommodations and alternatives for learners unable to attend live.
- Request a local-language review for regional adaptations, including technical terminology.
