## Product family expansion: a reusable training design, not invented expertise
The advertised job spans technology product areas including PCs, smart collaboration, security, IoT, healthcare and education solutions. A programme manager can use **one governed curriculum system** with separate product/sector modules. For each category, a qualified product or solutions expert must supply official content and approve every claim before launch.

| Learning vertical | Example buyer problem to explore | Learner exercise | Product SME must verify |
|---|---|---|---|
| PCs / devices | Mixed employee hardware and lifecycle ownership | Compare business needs and device management questions | Device configurations, service levels, warranties |
| Smart collaboration | Hybrid meeting problems across rooms and apps | Discovery call and approved demonstration handoff | Platform compatibility and supported integrations |
| Security | Buyer requests universal risk or compliance guarantee | Recognise prohibited assertions and escalate | Certifications, controls, policies, geographic scope |
| IoT | Distributed sensors create maintenance/visibility friction | Build outcome map and map technical dependencies | Connectivity, management, data retention, uptime |
| Healthcare | Clinical teams need reliable technology workflows | Identify stakeholder roles, privacy review, escalation | Regulatory/clinical safety and approved use cases |
| Education | Hybrid classrooms need accessible learning technology | Map teacher/student/admin needs to approved capabilities | Interoperability, accessibility, student data protections |

### Mini use-case: sales manager enablement (illustrative)
A sales manager asks participants to identify three buyer needs, choose a relevant vertical, propose a product demonstration *only within approved claims*, state two uncertain dependencies, and define the technical expert handoff. A 25-point observation rubric checks discovery, claims, value mapping and next steps. The manager reinforces behaviour after the session; the programme owner captures feedback and maintains an approved-content register.
