# 06 | Stakeholder communications and adoption reinforcement

## Example engagement cadence
| Audience | Channel | Cadence | Action | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Global sales sponsor | Steering note | Weekly during build | Resolve scope, resources, priorities | Programme manager |
| Regional sales leaders | 30-minute review | Biweekly | Validate relevance, reserve cohort time | Regional lead |
| Product/security SMEs | Asynchronous issue log | Twice weekly during build | Approve or challenge claims | Product/Security SME |
| Learners and partners | Email + LMS notices | T-14, T-7, T-1, launch | Explain objectives, links, support and accessibility | L&D operations |
| Sales managers | Coaching guide | Launch + 2-week follow-up | Observe live practice and reinforce | Regional managers |
| Content owner | Change-control log | Release-triggered / quarterly | Maintain and retire content | L&D content lead |

## Sample pre-launch email (illustrative)
**Subject:** ConnectHub learning launch — prepare for buyer discovery and safe demos

Our new learning journey covers buyer discovery, product and service positioning, safe claim handling, practical demonstrations and objection management. Please complete the 20-minute digital foundation before your scheduled workshop. The workshop includes coached role plays; no real customer or confidential data is required. If you need accessibility or time-zone adjustments, contact the programme team. Details and deadlines will be confirmed after the content release gate.

## Manager coaching card
At 2 weeks after learning: observe one 7-minute needs discovery; note questions used, source references, whether risky claims were escalated, and next-step quality. Feed back evidence, not generic ratings. At 30 days: discuss one case where training helped or failed; log changes to content.

## Governance when issues are raised
- A **product accuracy** issue pauses the affected claim and routes to Product.
- A **security/legal** issue blocks reuse until written expert approval.
- An **accessibility** issue receives a priority fix and alternate delivery.
- A **learning design** issue routes to L&D for revised exercise/instruction.
- A **LMS** defect routes to training operations/technology with a documented workaround.

## Stakeholder narrative
The programme manager's job is to keep a consistent master curriculum while allowing language, market examples and scheduling to adapt. Product facts and policy controls remain centrally approved; regional leads contribute relevant buyer scenarios. The core signal of success is **demonstrated behaviour**, not attendance alone.
