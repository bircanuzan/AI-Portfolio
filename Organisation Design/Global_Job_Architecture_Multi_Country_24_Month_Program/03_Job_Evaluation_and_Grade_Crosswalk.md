# 03 | Job Evaluation, Grade Mapping and Equivalence Controls

## Methodological stance
Korn Ferry Hay, Mercer IPE and other recognized evaluation methods provide structured ways to assess role size. **This artifact does not reproduce proprietary factor scoring or assert evaluator certification.** Any actual use would be supervised by authorised, trained job evaluators with access to the employer's selected methodology and licensed materials.

## Internal pre-calibration triage rubric (illustrative, not Hay/IPE)
Assess role content from an approved JD using four original 1–5 descriptive dimensions: technical breadth/depth, problem complexity, decision/accountability scope, and business/operating impact. Values support preparation and anomaly detection only. **No weighted sum is an official grade assignment.**

| Rating | Breadth | Complexity | Accountability | Impact |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Narrow routine work | Structured tasks | Guided execution | Individual task |
| 3 | Multifunction knowledge | Nonroutine operating judgement | Owns team/local outcomes | Site/function |
| 5 | Enterprise strategic expertise | Ambiguous multi-market decisions | Organisation-wide authority | Enterprise and external |

Before formal evaluation:
1. Validate actual accountabilities with role incumbent and manager; do not grade by incumbent salary.
2. Compare peers across markets and evaluate outlier role scope.
3. Document rationale, conflicting evidence and calibration panel sign-off.
4. Conduct job evaluation with selected licensed system / approved evaluators.
5. Link **evaluation result** to local compensation grade through Reward governance, policy, contracts and legal review.
6. Record exception and effective date; communicate appeal/review procedure.

## Grade-mapping discipline
- Global levels are career architecture; local grades are pay/administrative artifacts.
- One global level may map to more than one country grade, depending on role evaluation and labour context.
- Crosswalk contains **proposed** mappings pending verification; no salary change is implied.
- Never auto-convert a role's old local grade into a new grade using a title match.
- Reward owns band governance, job evaluation evidence and internal parity review.

## Calibration controls and fairness
Quarterly role panel with HRBP, OD, Reward, functional expert and local legal on regulated roles; conflict-of-interest declaration; blind-to-incumbent information where feasible; evaluate work not person; publish methodology summary and appeals intake; preserve confidential evaluation records securely.

## Exceptions
Statutory classification, union-specific roles, safety licences, role-critical allowances and local market practice use governed exceptions with business justification, owner, expiry date and next review. A standard architecture accommodates differences rather than masking them.
