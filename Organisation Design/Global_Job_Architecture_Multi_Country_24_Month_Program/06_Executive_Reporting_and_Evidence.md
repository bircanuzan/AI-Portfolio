# 06 | CHRO/Executive Steering-Committee Reporting

## Executive objective
One consistent language of work across international businesses, without erasing important jurisdictional differences or compromising operational safety.

## Steering committee narrative template
1. **Strategic progress:** architecture coverage by wave, critical job family and market.
2. **Business benefits:** visibility into skills, career paths, resource planning and control consistency — benefits are expected, not yet proven.
3. **Controls:** critical mismatches, local legal exceptions, formal evaluation completion and employee-impact risks.
4. **Trade-offs:** standard title library versus legitimate local operational differences.
5. **Decision required:** approve design/pilot/wave, fund remediation or extend calibrated review.
6. **Next 30/60/90 days:** owners, checkpoints, dependencies and sign-off criteria.

## Demonstrable artifact vs experience claim
This repository demonstrates how a Head/Manager of OD would *plan and control* a 24-month multinational program. It must **not** be presented as a completed sector_organization implementation, as a formal Hay/Mercer job-evaluation qualification, or as verified multinational outcomes. Actual use requires employer data, approvals and suitable evaluator credentials.
