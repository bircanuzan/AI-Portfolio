# Agentic AI development

## [Human Capital Intelligence Agent](Human%20Capital%20Intelligence%20Agent/)

A technically implemented, **synthetic** multi-domain Human Capital intelligence agent, developed against an AI-Agent-only enterprise hiring brief. Includes six analytics tools, an executable synthetic Human–AI workforce redesign module (job architecture, capacity reallocation and future organisation), and an executive orchestration plan; guarded FastAPI; role/entity permissions; HMAC-chained audit ledger; optional secure LLM summarisation; read-only external integration contracts; tests; CI and repeatable benchmarks; agent résumé and real-production readiness checklist.

**Status:** independent lab/prototype. **Not production-deployed, no real HRIS/ATS/payroll/ai_enterprise sovereign model connected, and not presently eligible for ai_enterprise production-agent selection.**

Other project areas remain separate, including Workforce Transformation, Organizational Effectiveness, AI in HR & Agentic Automation and Agentic AI Application Testing & Evaluation.
