## Sample year-one improvement model (invented AED)
- Annual HR administrative hours **before**: 600; **after forecast**: 360.
- Annual time difference: **240 hours**; fully loaded hourly rate: **AED 150**.
- **Illustrative annual admin capacity value = 240 × 150 = AED 36,000.**
- Policy-related payroll corrections before: **90**, after forecast: **50**; rework cost: **AED 300 per correction**.
- **Illustrative correction cost avoided = (90 − 50) × 300 = AED 12,000.**
- **Combined annual recurring value = AED 48,000.**
- One-off legal review, implementation, testing and communications budget: **AED 65,000**.
- **Illustrative year-one net = AED −17,000**; simple payback ≈ **16.25 months** if recurring benefits hold.

**Caution:** Reallocated staff time is *capacity*, not automatically financial cash savings; do not count twice. Currency, adoption, affected population, case mix and estimates must be verified by Finance and Payroll before a real business case. Legal compliance and employee protections are mandatory constraints, not cost-optimization levers.

## Measurement design
1. **Baseline** historical ticket data, policy defects, payroll corrections, employee case completion and document versions.
2. **Segment** by market, legal entity, flight/ground/corporate role, policy family and change cohort; check denominator definitions.
3. **Collect** HRIS extracts, case tickets, manager and employee surveys, exception register and counsel change log with privacy review.
4. **Compare** implementation to comparable baseline/counterfactual where reasonable, adjusting for seasonality, workforce size and policy mix.
5. **Audit** data lineage, missing values, employee confidentiality and review bias before publishing.
6. **Act** on threshold breaches and employee feedback; record corrective actions and re-evaluate at 30/60/90 days.

## Proposed decision thresholds (not regulatory standards)
- 100% of live country policy versions have documented required Legal approval.
- 0 unapproved contractual clauses or expired templates used for new employment documents.
- 100% of high-severity legal issues are assigned an accountable reviewer with due date and mitigation.
- HRIS/payroll release blocked if critical UAT fails.
- Review backlog, complaint severity, communication comprehension and employee support need determine remediation priority.

## Management narrative
An integrated policy library creates a control framework, not merely a document catalogue: it should reduce decision ambiguity, stop uncontrolled policy rollouts, establish an auditable evidence trail and improve the quality of employee-facing advice. Operational efficiency is measured **alongside** legality, fairness, aviation safety, employee trust and contractual obligations.
