# 01 | Multinational HR Policy Governance and Release Controls

## Governance charter (proposed)
**Policy owner:** Group Head of HR Policy. **Accountable sponsor:** CHRO or formally delegated approving authority. **Legal interpretive authority:** local qualified employment counsel, with Group Legal coordinating cross-market consistency. **Scope:** permanent/temporary employees, assignments and secondments as legally and contractually applicable; contractors are separately classified by Legal/Procurement.

**Hierarchy check before publication:** applicable mandatory law and regulations; enforceable collective/works-council arrangements; individual contract terms; binding internal commitments; new policy language. The hierarchy and employee-consultation obligations **must be validated market by market**, not assumed from this illustrative order.

## RACI by phase

| Draft global core and local addendum | A/R | C | C | C | I | I | I |
| Assess contract/benefit/pay impact | R | A (legal) | C | R (pay) | C | R (cost) | I |
| Approve policy for publication | R | Mandatory gate | C | Mandatory if pay | Mandatory if configured | C | A |

| Communicate, brief and monitor | A | C | R | C | C | I | I |

A = accountable, R = responsible, C = consulted, I = informed. Approval is not inferred from consultation; evidence of each required approval is retained in the register.

## Lifecycle and document register
**Statuses:** INTAKE → IMPACT_ASSESSMENT → DRAFT → LEGAL_REVIEW → CONTROL_SIGNOFF → APPROVED → SCHEDULED → EFFECTIVE → REVIEW / SUPERSEDED → ARCHIVED.

Minimum metadata: policy_id; title; family; countries/entities; source law/review references; effective and planned release dates; last_review and next_review; owner; legal approver and approval date; payroll/HRIS/ER approvals if applicable; document/template version; supersedes; employee notification evidence; translated version owner; exception logs; retention classification; change ticket and audit links.

**Version rule:** increment major version for a material rights, benefits, contractual or payroll change; minor for non-material editorial changes, only after Legal confirms classification. **Never silently overwrite** the published version. Preserve historical effective-dated copies, redline and approvals.

## Decision / escalation triggers
- **Critical:** credible statutory conflict, unlawful clause, imminent effective date, employee-rights impact or payroll underpayment — halt release; immediate Legal and sponsor escalation.
- **High:** market addendum missing, affected contract population unknown, payroll/HRIS UAT incomplete, country consultation unresolved — hold country go-live.
- **Medium:** unclear employee guidance, high exception volume or ambiguous owner — remediation plan and dated review.
- **Routine:** formatting or clarified examples without substantive change — controlled editorial release after appropriate review.

## Exception process
A line manager cannot override mandatory law or a signed employment term by granting a policy exception. Log request, impacted employee population, jurisdiction, evidence, cost, risk, advice, approvals, effective/expiry dates and review. An exception is **not** effective until an authorized human decision, required consultation and employee documentation are completed.

## Example change-control packet
**Ticket:** HRPOL-CHG-2026-014 (synthetic); **issue:** flexible-working eligibility criteria inconsistent between local handbook and manager guidance.
- Problem statement and employee segments affected; alternatives (maintain, clarify, redesign).
- Country-by-country legal questions and counsel evidence requirements.
- Financial impact across admin time, overtime, travel and change implementation.
- Redlined proposed policy, contract and handbook cross-reference.
- Payroll/HRIS UAT, data-privacy and consultation dependencies.
- Approval record, future effective date, rollback owner, communication matrix.
- Post-go-live 30/60/90-day review and exception dashboard.

## Monthly oversight pack
Open legal updates by severity and ageing; % policies reviewed on time; unapproved templates in circulation; redlines awaiting counsel; country adoption, acknowledgment coverage, payroll/HRIS defects, grievances by policy family, exception frequency, policy service-ticket trends, cost vs approved case and overdue remediation.
