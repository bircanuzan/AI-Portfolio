# Payroll, UAE EOS & WPS — control framework

## Purpose
This is a **pre-payroll assurance and review-routing framework**, not a UAE legal calculator or salary disbursement system. HR owns source-data quality, Payroll/Finance own the payment process, and appropriately qualified reviewers approve statutory entitlement decisions.

## Checkpoints
| Gate | Test / exception | Human owner | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| P01 | Duplicate employee or payroll line for one period | HRMS data steward | Versioned master and batch IDs |
| P02 | Proposed net pay differs from authorised components | Payroll Analyst | Calculation workbook / reconciliation |
| P03 | Missing pay change or payroll batch approval | HR/Finance dual approvers | Named approval chain and timestamps |
| P04 | Missing verified payment reference | Payroll + Treasury | Secure verification record (never public GitHub) |
| P05 | Payroll row for unknown/exited person | HR Operations + Payroll | Status history and exit approval |
| P06 | EOS review pending on exited employee | HR/Payroll specialist + Legal if needed | Expert calculation, contracts, dates, scheme |
| P07 | WPS applicability unverified | HR/Compliance/Legal | Legal entity mapping and signed applicability determination |
| P08 | File / totals do not reconcile with finance ledger | Finance Controller | Batch-to-bank-to-GL reconciliation |
| P09 | Salary or banking record edited without maker-checker | HRIS/IT Security | Immutable change trail |
| P10 | Late payroll or missed service-level cutoff | Payroll Lead | Incident and root-cause record |

## UAE-specific policy checkpoints
1. **WPS is not assumed universal.** UAE Government guidance updated 5 June 2026 references Ministerial Resolution 340/2026 and lists categories of exempt employers, including *banks and financial institutions*. A legal entity's actual eligibility must be checked; do not automatically classify Al Ramz as exempt merely because it operates in financial services. Official source: https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/payment-of-wages
2. **EOS gratuity is not generated by this demo.** For eligible expatriate full-time employees under the federal private-sector regime, guidance generally uses final **basic wage**, 21 days/year for the first five years, 30 thereafter, and a 24-month wage cap, with service and other exclusions or alternative schemes requiring validation. Rules for UAE nationals, part-time contracts, financial free zones and the voluntary Savings Scheme can differ. Official source: https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/jobs/employment-in-the-private-sector/end-of-service-benefits-for-employees-in-the-private-sector
3. Before deployment, capture actual legal entity, jurisdiction (federal/private sector vs special zone), contract terms, gratuity/savings scheme and employment category; send every legal ambiguity to qualified reviewers.
4. Record payroll cut-off, signed approvals, audit trail, secure bank-file handling and evidence retention per applicable company/regulatory policy. Use reconciliation and second-person authorisation before any transfer.

## Release decision
`BLOCKED` whenever any high-priority discrepancy remains. `REVIEW_READY` means **only** that no checks implemented in the demo found an exception; an authorised HR/Finance reviewer must still sign off. AI must never decide an employee's entitlements or initiate the bank payment.

## Example investigation
Employee F002: HRMS authorised components imply AED 15,000 but proposed AED 15,200 and approval is pending. The engine flags a proposed variance and missing sign-off. Payroll does not transmit money; it reconciles supporting evidence and gets dual approval before release.
