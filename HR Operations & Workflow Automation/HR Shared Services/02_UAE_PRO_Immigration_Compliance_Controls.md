# 02 | UAE PRO, Immigration & Compliance Control Framework

**Classification:** Proposed oversight framework. **Not legal advice**; not evidence of direct visa filing experience.

## Case types
New employment, employment permit, residence formalities, renewal, Emirates ID, role/contract amendments, sponsorship changes, Free Zone transitions, cancellations, employee queries, government notices and exceptional cases.

## Five-stage control workflow
1. **Intake:** employee/case ID, entity, case type, sponsor, due date, assigned owner, risk and approved request (no identity-document data in dashboards).
2. **Requirements validation:** specialist PRO / HR Legal verifies latest MOHRE, ICP, GDRFA or applicable Free Zone process, and checks current official requirements.
3. **Authorised execution:** accredited case owner / provider handles submissions through appropriate official channels.
4. **Verification:** receipt/reference evidence is checked; employment and HR system status reconciled; pending government queries are routed to accountable specialist.
5. **Closure and retention:** sign-off, effective dates, employer-approved retention and secure archival. Track next renewal where applicable.

## Proposed control fields
Case ID; anonymised employee ID; employing entity; authority; case type; required-by date; current stage; assigned PRO; HR checker; status; missing items; government reference stored in restricted system; last checked; next action; escalation owner; risk band; closure evidence link (restricted).

## Example escalation framework (illustrative; local SLA to be approved)
| Trigger | Action | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Missing required document | Clarify request and inform employee | HR Ops if unresolved within agreed SLA |
| Process target at risk | Contact PRO owner; unblock dependency | Shared Services supervisor |
| Potential work-authorisation issue | Stop affected onboarding activity; assess with legal specialist | HR Legal / authorised PRO immediately |
| Government rejection or notice | Record case, root cause, decision owner | HR Legal / Government Relations |
| Large batch transfer | Build person-by-person dependency plan with risk controls | Head of HR / Operations / Finance |

## Dashboard and audit
Report cases by type/stage/age, upcoming expiries, on-time renewals, provider cycle times, pending document count, exceptions, rework and unresolved high-risk items. Redact personal data from leadership dashboards.

## Dependency register
Employer entity / Free Zone, employee and contract terms, labour and immigration policy, government portal access, payroll start date, start-date commitments, sponsor transfer sequencing, legal sign-off and vendor SLA.

**Important:** Specific reminder windows, document requirements, rules and current legislation are intentionally not hard-coded. Verify them against applicable official channels and company policy before any real use.
