# CHRO / Chief HR Transformation — Talent and Succession Decision Architecture

**Extends existing Project 08's strategic business-side portfolio, rather than duplicating Project 38’s behavioural scoring.**

## Talent architecture from enterprise need to development
1. Identify enterprise-critical roles using business strategy and approved position architecture; review vacancies, capability scarcity and service continuity.
2. Model capabilities at role/job **not person-attribute** level: expertise, decision rights, adaptability, leadership and development pathways.
3. Distinguish *performance evidence*, *potential hypotheses* and *successor readiness*; no universal numeric score replaces this decision.
4. Apply appropriately qualified, job-relevant assessment methods and candidate/employee consent; track confidence and limits.
5. Separate succession planning from appointment: readiness labels and development actions never become automatic promotion/termination decisions.
6. Link assessment outcomes to development, coaching, internal mobility, opportunity access and observable changes in work behaviour.
7. Monitor talent outcomes, resource allocation, cost and risks with privacy-preserving executive metrics.

## Executive artefacts
| Decision | Required evidence | Accountable signoff | Action if missing |
|---|---|---|---|
| Which roles are business critical? | Approved strategic workforce plan, role dependencies | CHRO + strategy/business | Defer critical-role designation |
| Which capabilities should be built? | Role task/skill gap with verified baseline | Talent/OD leader + business | Validate roles and task measures |
| Which assessment can be used? | Job analysis, purpose, validity, qualified provider | Assessment specialist + HR Legal | Do not interpret as validated |
| What does “ready for succession” mean? | Role-specific judgement, development evidence, uncertainty | Talent Council / CHRO | Record unverified evidence and plans |
| How will impact be measured? | Matched baseline, opportunity fairness, follow-up | Talent Development + Finance | Call benefits hypotheses, not achieved ROI |

## Responsible use of GenAI
An approved assistant could structure redacted evidence, identify missing source citations, or draft a human-reviewed development conversation. It must not autonomously assign potential, produce protected-category inferences, disclose confidential succession records, override independent assessors or generate final employment decisions.
