# CHRO Assessment Centre assurance | Behavioural evidence and assessor calibration

## Project 38’s exclusive purpose
Demonstrate **how independent, role-specific observable behavioural evidence is collected and adjudicated**, while resisting any misleading leap to “validated talent potential”, diagnoses or autonomous hiring.

## Evidence and reliability
The existing synthetic assessor exercise contains 12 evidence items independently rated by two fictional raters (24 ratings), with 10/12 agreement and 2 discrepancies documented for human adjudication. The source code additionally computes Cohen's kappa on the invented small sample. These statistics describe a *toy scoring exercise* only; they are not an assessment-centre validity study, publisher reliability certification or a legitimate employment threshold.

## Executive assurance gate
| Risk | Human control | Evidence required before real use |
|---|---|---|
| Unanchored competency scoring | Competency rubric reviewed against job analysis | Signed job/role analysis and behavioural anchors |
| Rater drift and anchoring | Assessors score independently before calibration | Timestamped independent observation, disagreements and reasoned adjudication |
| Overconfident summary | Distinguish observation and inference | Observable evidence and alternative explanations |
| Accessible participation | Accessible alternative format / reasonable accommodations | Assessor planning, consent and participant feedback |
| Invalid method or role fit | Qualified assessment professional challenges design | Appropriate job-related validity evidence and local requirements |
| Undocumented employment decisions | Separate authorised human committee | Justified decision, appeal/correction and privacy controls |

## Development-to-work integration
The 30/60/90 developmental coaching plan already in Project 38 remains the practical individual feedback loop, not the enterprise leadership operating-model diagnostic in Project 39. Report scoring as **descriptive role-linked behaviour**, not a diagnosis, personality profile, algorithmic fit or substitute for judgement.
