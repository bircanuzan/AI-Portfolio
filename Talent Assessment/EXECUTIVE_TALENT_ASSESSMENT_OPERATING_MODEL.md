# CHRO / Chief HR Transformation Officer / Chief AI Officer — Talent Assessment Operating Model

**Four existing, distinct projects. Independent fictional case work; no actual assessor certification or validated psychometric instrumentation.**

## Executive investment question
How should an enterprise identify, develop and redeploy capability with appropriate validity, transparency, fairness, independent assessment competence and business accountability—without treating AI-generated scores as talent decisions?

## Proposed CHRO / CAIO stage gates
1. **Purpose and rights:** distinguish recruitment, development, succession and organisational diagnostics; record lawful basis, transparency, evidence requirements and individual impact.
2. **Job/role relevance:** qualified specialists validate competency architecture and chosen assessments for specific use/country/population; no recycled universal talent score.
3. **Method/instrument selection:** verify assessor training, vendor permissions, norms, accessibility, published measurement properties, language and administration conditions.
4. **Human evidence collection:** structured and independent observation, documented uncertainty, separate authorised test interpretation, proper candidate feedback and corrections.
5. **Responsible AI:** generative assistants only where approved for **source-grounded drafting**; no individual psychometric diagnosis, talent-ranking agents or unauthorised employment decisions.
6. **Development and value:** assess actual development follow-through, career mobility, succession preparedness and talent-programme adoption with strong denominators and consent.
7. **Independent assurance:** audit data lineage, human decisions, appeals, sample sizes, bias/impact where lawful, incident response and test version changes.

## CHRO scorecard — definitions, not claimed outcomes
- Succession readiness: approved critical positions with reviewed succession plans / all approved critical positions, with confidentiality and small-group protection.
- Assessment validity coverage: assessment uses supported by documented role/job analysis and qualified owner / proposed assessment uses.
- Assessor calibration exceptions: independent divergent ratings reviewed with evidence / divergent ratings; does not alone establish decision validity.
- Development completion and transfer: actions independently verified in follow-up / participant actions due, with appropriate time horizon.
- Participant experience and accessibility: consent, clarity, accommodations and concerns handled by named people.
- Organisational capability improvement: **observed** control/skills evidence after intervention compared with baseline, not a fabricated maturity score.
- Risk: unauthorised access, score leakage, failed norm fit, unresolved candidate challenge or adverse-impact incident.
- Financial value: full vendor/licensing and human-hours cost compared with measurable capability/business outcomes; no assumed ROI.

## Proposed 90-day plan
Days 1–30: Define critical capabilities, role analysis, decision rights, consent, legal/privacy gates, existing assessment inventories and development outcomes.
Days 31–60: Redesign assessor protocols and vendor selection, document organisation-level evidence gaps, assign decision owners and prepare a small risk-bounded nonproduction methodology review.
Days 61–90: Validate materials with qualified professionals, conduct accessibility/fairness and supplier checks, approve limited human-led pilot criteria and build an assurance/measurement plan. **No pilot has been run.**
