# Chief AI Officer / CHRO — Occupational psychometric test-use and vendor assurance

**Project 49 alone owns test-output interpretation scope; this is NOT Project 55's ATS algorithmic screener test and NOT Project 38's behavioural observation lab.**

## Decision problem
Twelve invented cases include style and ability-like indices. They are arbitrary fictional numbers, **not** normed test scores, evidence of psychological constructs, validated selection predictors or appropriate personality diagnoses. Before a real enterprise uses licensed occupational tests, the CHRO and qualified testing practitioner must establish professional competence, authorised test rights and job relevance.

## Vendor and method evaluation gate
| Dimension | Required independent real-world evidence | Action when missing |
|---|---|---|
| Publisher permission and practitioner credentials | Current publisher terms; restricted test administration/interpretation training and qualified test-user verification | NO INTERPRETATION / no restricted score dissemination |
| Construct and job-related validity | Test technical manual, role analysis, validation and limitations appropriate to use | Do not infer aptitude or fit |
| Reliability / score precision | Appropriate reliability, test-retest, standard errors/confidence intervals when meaningful | No precision claims |
| Norms and fairness | Relevant population norms, language, country, measurement invariance/equivalence and protected impact review as law permits | Do not compare people using unsupported norm groups |
| Administration equivalence | Remote vs supervised conditions, accessibility, translations, proctoring and accommodation protocol | Qualified review required |
| Personal data governance | Legitimate purpose, confidentiality, data minimisation, retention/deletion, vendor transfers and correction/appeal rights | Hold processing and report release |
| AI use | Approved data handling, source-grounded report formatting only; qualified human interpretation | No LLM personality/fit diagnosis |
| Employment consequence | Evidence beyond one test, qualified assessor and accountable decision/appeal | No automatic promote/hire/reject |

## Executive decision
The sample [analysis.py](analysis.py) calculates **descriptive aggregates only** on fabricated values. No index or composite is validated for hiring, promotion or succession. Psychometric vendor procurement requires verification and documented independent signoff, and this project is not evidence of that signoff.

## Development lens
Qualified, lawful assessment may inform individual coaching and development with consent, but confidential feedback, advancement decisions and career opportunities must remain human-led, sufficiently contextualised and supported by multiple valid sources.

## RACI
CHRO: assessment strategy; occupational psychologist/qualified authorised tester: test use and interpretation; Talent lead: development process; IT/CAIO: AI/data design assurance; Legal/Privacy: purpose and handling; Procurement: publisher and security contracts; employee/candidate: transparent process and appropriate rights.

## Evidence boundary
No BPS, SHL, Hogan, Saville or other publisher accreditation or test administration is demonstrated; no valid psychometric scores, actual test users, clinical psychological opinion, real fairness claim or hiring recommendation.
