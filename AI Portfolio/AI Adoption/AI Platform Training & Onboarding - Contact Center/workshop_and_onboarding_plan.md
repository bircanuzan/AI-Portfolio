# 90-Minute AI Creative Platform Onboarding Workshop

## Learning objectives
By the end, a participant will be able to:
1. Navigate an approved creative AI platform and identify access/usage restrictions (**Understand**).
2. Build an image prompt using subject, composition, constraints and reference parameters (**Apply**).
3. Plan a 4-shot short video with consistent visual identity and quality checks (**Create**).
4. Diagnose failures and route platform defects or complex requests to the right human owner (**Evaluate**).
5. Document a review decision and satisfy the production-readiness rubric (**Apply/Evaluate**).

## Delivery schedule
| Time | Segment | Participant action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–10 | Context, brand/privacy rules, live onboarding check | Complete 5-question readiness check | Diagnostic |
| 10–20 | Navigation and approved input restrictions | Find settings, history, reference panel | Observation |
| 20–35 | Image generation demonstration | Review example prompt and expected output | Quality checklist |
| 35–50 | Image lab and refinement | Write original synthetic prompt and two revisions | Prompt log |
| 50–65 | Video storyboard + generation workflow | Draft four shots and continuity constraints | Storyboard |
| 65–75 | Troubleshooting clinic | Diagnose three predefined failures | Case sheet |
| 75–85 | Capstone | Submit approved simulated asset package | Rubric |
| 85–90 | Feedback and support | Complete survey and knowledge check | Evaluation |

**Delivery modes:** Classroom with shared screen and individual practice; remote with accessibility-compliant captions/recording only on consent; self-paced job aid alternative.

## Practical exercise: synthetic customer-support visual
Brief: Design a 16:9 fictional self-service help illustration for a generic delivery-tracking article. No real client name, identifiable person, logo, confidential UI or personal data. Participants draft and refine prompts, log quality checks, and explain when a human should reject an output.

## Trainer preparation
Confirm authorised tool access; verify product UI in current release; pre-check network/SSO; prepare synthetic reference images; avoid customer content; create a fallback screenshot-free paper exercise; test captions; prepare a demo of a failed prompt and an escalation ticket.

## Observation checklist
Prompt specificity, constraints, use of only permitted references, awareness of rights/privacy, reproducibility and control of output defects. Trainer records a pass/fail with evidence, not impressions.

## Evaluation and follow-up
Level 1: survey clarity/confidence; Level 2: rubric and quiz; Level 3: observed production-ready behavior after coached practice; Level 4: operational QA trend **only after actual deployment**. No Level 3/4 outcome is claimed.
