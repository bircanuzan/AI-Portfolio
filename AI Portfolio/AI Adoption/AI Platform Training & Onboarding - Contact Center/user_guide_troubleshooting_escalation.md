# Learner Job Aid and Escalation SOP

## Safe five-step workflow
1. **Check scope:** Confirm business use, approval, no personal/customer data and suitable rights for reference media.
2. **Prepare:** Define artifact, target viewer, composition, brand parameters, aspect ratio, negative constraints and review criteria.
3. **Generate:** Use approved account; record settings and version; never paste secrets into public prompts.
4. **Review and refine:** Inspect likeness, artifacts, text, factual accuracy, consistency, accessibility and confidentiality; refine systematically.
5. **Obtain approval:** Record human approval and only then share/export to authorized channel.

## Frequent support issues
| Symptom | First-level response | Escalate when |
|---|---|---|
| Inconsistent visual style | Fix style descriptors, reference provenance, format and repeated constraints | Reproducible after three controlled attempts |
| Nonsense text in graphic | Remove in-image typography; add accessible text manually | Product malfunction persists |
| Inconsistent video subject | Use continuity storyboard; simplify motion and shot length | Severe temporal drift blocks approved use |
| Rejected prompt | Check content/rights guidance, remove prohibited material | Policy ambiguity or erroneous moderation requires review |
| Failed export | Check format, permitted account, browser/network and permissions | Platform issue persists with documented reproduction |
| Access/SSO problem | Check approved sign-in route and assigned permissions | IT/service owner needed |
| Customer personal data in prompt | Stop, do not retry, report via privacy/security policy | **Immediate privacy/security escalation** |

## Triage and owner model
- **L1 — Trainer:** usage/how-to, prompt advice, expected limits and routine corrections.
- **L2 — Product support / Solution Architect:** reproducible product defect, integration, account/architecture or unsupported workflow.
- **Information security/privacy:** accidental client/candidate data exposure; immediate incident process.
- **Operations:** production readiness, coaching assignment, staffing impact.
- **Client owner:** brand, rights, content policy and final approval.

## Training materials version control
After each platform release, validate UI and parameter changes, amend guide/screenshots and assessment cases, record release notes, and communicate change to agents and trainers. Do not teach unreleased features.
