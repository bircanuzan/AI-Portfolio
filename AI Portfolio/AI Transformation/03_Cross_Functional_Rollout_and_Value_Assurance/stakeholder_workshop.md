# Executive workshop and decision record template

## Agenda (90 minutes)
1. Business objective and financial/service baseline (15m)
2. Current workflows, pain points, manual handoffs and competing AI tools (20m)
3. Target processes, human decision points, proposed use cases (20m)
4. Information risk, platform fit, and integration feasibility (15m)
5. Pilot owners, success thresholds, training, dependencies and go/no-go (20m)

## Output to capture
| Field | Prompt |
|---|---|
| Sponsor | What outcome will change, and who accepts the result? |
| Task | Which specific step, volume, complexity and exception profile? |
| User and rights | Who can access input, sources and output? |
| Decision rights | Which human reviews and releases? |
| Tool choice | Existing native function, approved no-code, or scoped API? |
| Work redesign | What roles, handoffs, training and oversight change? |
| Baseline | Measured time, error, backlog, quality, cost and satisfaction? |
| Quality gates | What would make this unsafe or economically unwise? |
| Implementation | Owner, timeframe, risk, cost, UAT and rollback route? |

## Decision log example (synthetic)
DEC-001: Finance and customer operations sponsor a controlled knowledge FAQ pilot only after content ownership, data classification and role-based permission design. Scope excludes HR confidential records and autonomous customer communications. **Status: PROPOSED, NOT APPROVED OR IMPLEMENTED.**
