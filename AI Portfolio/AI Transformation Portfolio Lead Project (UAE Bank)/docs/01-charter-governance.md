# 01 | Change Agenda, Charter and Governance

## Portfolio charter
**Purpose:** Translate Wholesale Banking Group and Treasury/Markets strategic priorities into a sequenced, resourced portfolio with accountable benefits and controlled AI delivery.  
**Business scope:** Corporate Banking, Institutional Banking, Investment & Transaction Banking, Treasury, Asset Management and Global Markets.  
**Sponsor:** Hypothetical Executive Committee sponsor.  
**Portfolio Lead:** Maintains portfolio register, interdependency map, resource plan, risk/issues, proposed changes, status and decision log.  
**Exclusions:** Live financial transactions, client confidential data and autonomous financial decisions.

## Stage gates
| Gate | Decision | Mandatory evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | Accept for discovery | Named business sponsor, problem statement, capability and change impact hypothesis |
| 1 | Portfolio prioritisation | Charter, BRD outline, financial/strategic business case, risks, dependencies, capacity demand |
| 2 | Fund/design | ExCo decision, funded resourcing, architecture and data-governance review, vendor due diligence |
| 3 | UAT/pilot | Test plan, test outcomes, defects, rollback, privacy/security and model-risk reviews, business sign-off |
| 4 | Launch | Release controls, operational SOP, monitoring, human override, training and communications readiness |
| 5 | Value/closure | Benefit-owner validation, adoption KPIs, lessons learned, audit-remediation evidence |

## Stakeholders and decision rights
- **Group Heads / business units:** set strategic outcomes, accept requirements, sign off business cases and benefits.
- **ExCo Steering Committee:** decides portfolio investment, disputed priorities and resource exceptions.
- **Transformation Portfolio Lead:** challenges value/feasibility, manages portfolio changes, escalates issues, facilitates decisions and communicates status.
- **IT / Data / Architecture:** validate integrations, change windows and technical constraints.
- **Finance:** independently validates business-case assumptions and realised savings.
- **Risk / Compliance / Legal / Privacy / InfoSec / Model Risk:** determine applicable control requirements, review exceptions, challenge release readiness.
- **Internal Audit:** independent assurance, findings and closure validation.
- **Vendors:** deliver against approved scope, security terms, SLAs and traceable handover obligations.

## Resource planning
Require quarterly forecast per initiative: product owner, business analyst, project manager, engineering, integration/data SME, UAT users, control reviewer and change lead. Create shared-capability views and critical-path dependencies; do not double-book named SMEs. Escalate resourcing gaps with options: re-sequence, reduce non-critical scope, approved temporary capacity.

## Change-control / conflict resolution
A priority change request must identify sponsor, rationale, strategic effect, impacted charter/BRD, total portfolio cost, resource demand, risk/control changes and alternatives. ExCo meeting minutes record decision, approval authority, effective date, dependency impacts and actions.
