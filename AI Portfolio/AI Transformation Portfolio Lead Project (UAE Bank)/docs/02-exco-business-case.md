# 02 | ExCo Steering Committee Pack / Business Case

**Scenario:** Monthly wholesale and markets portfolio governance meeting. All figures synthetic.

## Executive decisions requested
| Initiative | Business area | Delivery problem | Decision sought |
|---|---|---|---|
| WB-01 | Transaction Banking | Manual trade document triage | Approve limited discovery; require evidence-based extraction tests and reviewer approval |
| WB-02 | Corporate Banking | Fragmented CRM and relationship-manager workflow | Prioritise integration subject to capacity |
| WB-03 | Treasury | High-volume exception monitoring | Hold live AI rollout pending Treasury risk and model-control review |
| WB-04 | Institutional Banking | Onboarding documentation delays | Re-sequence against WB-02 shared integration SMEs |
| WB-05 | Global Markets | Disconnected performance reporting | Shape data-lineage requirements before funding |

## Prioritisation model
Scored 0–5 for strategy, benefits-evidence quality, feasibility, controls and adoption-readiness with weights 25/25/20/20/10. Decision support only. Unresolved severe data, risk or audit concerns override calculated ranks.

## Illustration: business-case cross-check
- Baseline: 40,000 eligible cases annually, verified from an operations system before any real deployment.
- Hypothesis: 6 minutes saved per case at 50% year-one usage = **2,000 gross hours of capacity equivalent**.
- At an illustrative AED 100 per hour, estimated gross equivalent is AED 200,000; **not cash savings** unless Finance confirms released spend and displacement/disaggregation.
- Subtract design, integration, inference, vendor, licence, quality review, security and training costs.
- Compare against non-AI workflow redesign and rule-based automation; quantify risk of false approvals and confidentiality exposure.
