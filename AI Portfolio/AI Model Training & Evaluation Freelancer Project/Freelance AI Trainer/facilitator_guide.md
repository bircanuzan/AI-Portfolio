# Practical Generative AI Workplace Session - Trainer Guide

## Preparation
Agree audience objectives, company tool permissions and safe-data policy. Bring synthetic examples; never solicit participant secrets, customer data, payroll records or unredacted CVs. Prepare sample prompts for a short business memo, a multi-step checklist and a fictional financial table.

## Live demonstration sequence
1. Ask learners for a vague prompt, show how missing context produces untestable output.
2. Add **role, objective, context, constraints, audience, format and acceptance criteria**.
3. Ask the model to distinguish sourced facts, assumptions and unknowns.
4. Validate claims manually, especially citations, numerical answers and process exceptions.
5. Revise the prompt after comparing output against the user's actual objective.
6. Explain tool limitations and when a human expert needs to approve.

## Group exercises
A. Reformat a fictitious policy into a 5-step checklist. Verify no step was invented.
B. Summarize mock sales figures and cross-check every calculated percentage against the source.
C. Build a draft meeting summary from synthetic notes and highlight ambiguous actions.
D. Map a workflow automation idea with human review and exception escalation.

## Troubleshooting
- Hallucination: demand source references and remove claims that cannot be verified.
- Context omission: supply missing inputs and clarify operating assumptions.
- Data privacy: replace with non-identifying synthetic examples.
- Unhelpful verbosity: request a table, length or structure with success criteria.
- Output bias: diversify examples and invite human challenge of assumptions.

## Evaluation / next steps
Use observable rubric dimensions: task framing, context completeness, output specificity, verification plan and privacy/human approval. Reassess with comparable tasks, capture participant feedback, and assign one safe use case with a human reviewer.
