## Calculation rules
- Time saved (hours) = comparable volume × (baseline minutes/case − pilot minutes/case) / 60.
- Reduction percentage = (baseline − pilot)/baseline × 100; reject division by zero.
- Gross labour equivalent = hours × documented labour rate; **not** realised cash savings.
- Net savings = validated cash cost removed − implementation and ongoing operational cost (only if attributable).
- Compare similar case mix and observation periods; calculate confidence intervals where sample size permits.
- Include negative results and unchanged metrics.
