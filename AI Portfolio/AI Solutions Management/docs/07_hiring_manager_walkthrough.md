# 07 — Hiring Manager Walkthrough & Evidence Boundaries

## 90-second project explanation
"I developed an independent AI Solutions Management portfolio blueprint for a fictional multi-entity organisation with overlapping AI subscriptions and no consolidated governance. I designed a product backlog and prioritisation model, a central tool/renewal register, a vendor due-diligence and commercial evaluation scorecard, and a multi-entity gated rollout approach. The control model clarifies which decisions stay with IT Security, Legal, DPO, Procurement and Finance. I also built a small standard-library Python reporting workflow that turns synthetic licence, usage and scenario inputs into an executive view of seat utilisation, spend, illustrative value and approval risks. It demonstrates how I would manage enterprise AI tools from use-case intake through safe adoption and renewal, while keeping simulated results separate from actual client delivery."

## Do not overclaim
- The project is **independent and demonstrative**; it is not an implemented Modon/ADNEC solution.
- Sample vendors, seat counts, spend, rates and productivity benefits are invented and explicitly labelled.
- Naming Claude, Synthesia, Telnyx and Midjourney does not imply hands-on commercial administration or enterprise licences.
- ROI is a scenario calculation; never state it as proven financial impact.
- No production integration, signed contract, real customer information, vendor certification, PMP certification or CSPO certification is evidenced by this repository.
