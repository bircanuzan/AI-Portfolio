# Domain evaluation task specification

Write questions whose answer depends on distinctions in sources, period, scope, accounting definitions, or causal claims. Use a two-stage review: (1) verify primary documents and dates (2) evaluate reasoning from evidence.

**Rubric (100 pts):** verified factual correctness 30; evidence-to-claim alignment 25; nuanced reasoning 20; calibrated uncertainty 15; clear corrective feedback 10.

**Critical defects:** fabricated sources, citation that does not support a claim, misdated report, conclusion stronger than evidence, unverifiable financial inference.

**For real submission:** attach authoritative source URL, page/section, publication date, relevant quotation within licensing limits, model output and why it fails. Examples in this repository are synthetic and are NOT verifications of real organizations.
