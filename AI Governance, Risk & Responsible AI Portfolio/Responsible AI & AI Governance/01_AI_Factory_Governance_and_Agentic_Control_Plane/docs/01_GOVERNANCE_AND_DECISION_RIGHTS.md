# Enterprise AI Governance Charter and Decision Rights

**Engagement:** Independent fictional UAE AI Factory and Agent Control Plane case. No employer/client assignment.

## Executive mandate
Govern all predictive, generative, retrieval-augmented and agentic systems from idea through retirement. Align risk appetite, business value, human rights and worker impacts, data privacy, cyber controls, model evaluation, access, transparency, audit and incident response.

## Governance forum
- CEO / Board Risk Committee: set enterprise risk appetite and approve exceptional risk exposure and funding.
- Chief AI Officer: accountable for AI portfolio, AI Factory control system and capability investments.
- CTO / AI Engineering: design approved model/agent architecture, tool and identity policy, observability, security implementation.
- CRO / Model Risk: independently challenge risk assessments and residual risks, require corrective action.
- CISO: approve security boundaries, penetration and adversarial testing, access constraints, DLP and rollback.
- DPO / Privacy Counsel: approve personal-data legality, retention, vendor and transfer applicability.
- CHRO / Talent Governance: employment impact, fairness and human review of recruitment and people decisions.
- Data Owner / Business Sponsor: lawful purpose, source ownership, KPIs, reliability and business acceptance.
- Internal Audit: independently review control evidence and CAPA closure, not design and then self-certify the same controls.

## RACI
| Activity | Accountable | Responsible | Consulted | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Use-case approval to build | Chief AI Officer | Product owner | Finance, HR, Risk, DPO | Business intake |
| Model and tool inventory | Chief AI Officer | AI engineering | Data owner | Inventory/version records |
| AI impact and privacy assessment | CRO / DPO | Risk and privacy teams | HR, Legal, Model Owner | Risk/DPIA |
| Technical tool controls | CTO / CISO | Platform engineering | AI Risk, Architecture | Allowlist and UAT |
| Human employment decisions | CHRO | Authorized manager | Legal, HR specialists | Human decision and appeal |
| Independent assurance | CRO / Internal Audit | Independent testers | Model Owner | Reproducible tests |
| Release / pause | Authorized steering forum | AI Factory PMO | Risk, privacy, security | Written GO/HOLD/STOP |
| Incident / rollback | COO / CTO | Ops + CISO | Risk, Legal, DPO | Incident and recovery log |

## Stage gates
G0 business purpose & named owner; G1 inventory and source mapping; G2 impact, privacy, fairness and vendor screening; G3 engineering controls and agent delegated rights; G4 independent TEVV + adversarial challenge; G5 human-reviewed pilot authorization; G6 operational monitoring and change management; G7 retirement/periodic recertification.

A missing material approval means HOLD. Unknown or prohibited action means DENY. Synthetic "ALLOW_SIMULATION" is not real security authorization.

**Decision authority cannot be replaced by policy prompts.** An instruction telling an LLM to ask permission is not equivalent to an authenticated, enforced server-side tool approval.
