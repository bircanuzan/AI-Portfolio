# UAE law, policy and standards: decision matrix
**Desk-research baseline: 2 November 2026.** This is a prioritization aid, **not a legal opinion**. Confirm the legal entity, establishment, sector, processing location, regulator and the latest text/implementing regulations with UAE counsel before go-live.

## Binding law vs guidance vs voluntary standard
| Authority or source | Type | Why relevant to an agentic recruiting / HR workflow | What evidence HumanEvoX would need |
|---|---|---|---|
| UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 (Personal Data Protection) | **Federal binding law, subject to scope/exceptions** | Candidate/employee personal-data processing; lawful basis, rights, controls, transfers | Controller/processor analysis, purpose & basis record, data inventory, retention and access controls, privacy notice, transfer review, rights procedure |
| UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 34 of 2021 (Rumours and Cybercrimes) | **Federal law; applicability depends on conduct** | Cyber and information-system misuse; prevention and incident review | Security and prohibited-use controls, legal escalation protocol |
| DIFC Law No. 5 of 2020 and Data Protection Regulations (including autonomous/semi-autonomous system Regulation 10) | **DIFC-specific binding regime; only where applicable** | Special rules for relevant DIFC processing, autonomous systems and potential certification duties | DIFC applicability analysis, Regulation 10 assessment, owner sign-off; check 2026 amendments/status with DIFC |
| ADGM Data Protection Regulations 2021 | **ADGM-specific binding regime; only where applicable** | Personal-data obligations for ADGM-regulated entities/processing | ADGM lawful-basis, controller/processor, data transfers and rights review |
| UAE Charter for Development and Use of AI (2024) | **Government policy/charter, not a blanket private-sector certification law** | Privacy, ethics, human interests and safe development/use | Nonbinding ethics mapping, governance statement, monitoring |
| UAE AI Ethics Principles & Guidelines / official ethics self-assessment | **Nonbinding recommended guidance** | Fairness, transparency, accountability, human-centred safeguards, privacy and security | Ethical impact assessment, bias test plan, independent human review |
| ISO/IEC 42001:2023 | **Voluntary international management-system standard**, unless independently required by contract/regulator | Organizational AIMS: governance, operations, risk assessment, performance and improvement | Context/scope, AI inventory, policy, AI risk and impact assessment, objectives, control applicability, monitoring, audit, management review |
| ISO/IEC 23894 and NIST AI RMF | **Voluntary reference frameworks** | Risk taxonomy, measurement and treatment | Risk record and method mapping |

## **Jurisdiction gate before a live project**
1. Is the organization established in mainland UAE, DIFC, ADGM or another zone? Which regulator and sector rules apply?
2. Is HumanEvoX a controller or processor for each recruitment workflow? Who actually authorizes processing?
3. Which data types are involved (candidate CVs, profiling, salary history, health/sensitive information)?
4. What basis, notice, purpose limitations, retention, transfers and vendor terms are needed?
5. Does the agent merely draft or does it evaluate/rank or take consequential action? Preserve meaningful human judgment.
6. What additional employment, anti-discrimination, cybersecurity, sectoral, automated-decision or Emirate-specific obligations are relevant? Obtain counsel sign-off.

**No single national law named “ISO/IEC 42001 UAE law” exists in these sources.** Do not advertise ISO/IEC 42001 as legally mandatory in all UAE organizations. No company ISO certification is being asserted.
