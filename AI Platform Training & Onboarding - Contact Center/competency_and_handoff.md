# Readiness Scoring, Human Sign-off and Operational Handoff

## Assessment rubric (score 1–4)
| Criterion | Weight | Passing evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Approved tool navigation and access hygiene | 15% | Correct workspace/settings and access control |
| Prompt structure and reproducibility | 20% | Clearly scoped requirements and controlled revisions |
| Image/video creative quality | 20% | Objective QA list, composition and visual consistency |
| Rights, confidentiality and responsible use (**critical**) | 20% | No unapproved media/client data; recorded review |
| Troubleshooting and escalation | 15% | Correct simulated defect triage, reproducible handoff |
| Communication and collaboration | 10% | Clear explanation to learner/client/Operations |

**Ready for supervised production:** weighted score >= 3.0 / 4.0, all critical items >= 3 and named trainer + Operations sign-off.  
**Not ready:** coaching assignment, new practice tasks and re-assessment; no automatic production access.

## Trainer performance metrics
- Learner attendance / completion.
- End-of-workshop knowledge attainment.
- Percentage production-ready on first attempt.
- Percentage reassessed and subsequently cleared.
- Median time to resolve a routine usage problem.
- Open blockers, L2 escalations, SLA age and recurring defect themes.
- Learner satisfaction and post-training QA *once actual operational data exist*.

## Human handoff sequence
Trainer records consented evidence > learner gets actionable feedback > technical and privacy blockers resolved > Operations reviews rubric and work sample > named business owner authorizes release > supervision and follow-up measurement.

## Handoff template
Agent ID (pseudonymous) | course/edition | access validated | prompt lab result | creative quality | critical privacy check | troubleshooting | re-assessment due | reviewer | Ops approval | release date.

## Roles / accountability
Trainer owns instruction and evidence; Solution Architect owns complex technical issues; QA reviewer checks content; Ops lead owns readiness release; Security owns incident path. No autonomous approval by the AI platform.

## What has not been tested
This case has no live tool demonstrations, no named client/agent records, no solution-architect ticket, no actual workplace performance results, and no claim of Trainer Certification Program 101/102.
