# Responsible AI Standards & UAE Regulatory Applicability Crosswalk

**References are conceptual. Not a legal opinion, completed ISO/IEC 42001 assessment, or certified conformity.**

| Standard/law | Relevance | Artifact in this project | Important limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| NIST AI RMF 1.0 | GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE and MANAGE outcomes across AI lifecycle | Governance charter, AI inventory, test/impact package, CAPA | Voluntary; NIST notes RMF revision in progress as of 2026 |
| NIST AI RMF Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1) | Generative AI content, reliability, misuse, hallucination and human factors | Prompt threat cases, quality and source assessment plan | No external GenAI model evaluated by this project |
| ISO/IEC 42001:2023 | AI management system requirements and continual improvement processes | Executive policy/oversight, monitoring plan, corrective actions | ISO conformity requires actual implemented processes and independent evidence |
| ISO/IEC 23894 | Guidance for AI risk identification and treatment | Risk taxonomy, owner, treatment and assessment | Does not validate illustrative risk scores |
| UAE Federal Decree Law 45/2021 (Personal Data Protection Law) | Data protection and confidentiality obligations, data subject rights and transfers | DPO review and no-real-data controls | Jurisdiction, sector, exemptions, implementing law and lawful basis need legal review |
| Applicable financial, public sector, DIFC, ADGM or international regimes | Regulated deployment and sovereign data risk | Proposed sector/sovereign screening | Not assumed to apply to every UAE entity or every use case |

## Due diligence checklist
1. Identify the actual legal entity, regulator, project jurisdiction and data subjects.
2. Determine whether personal or sensitive data will be processed, lawful basis, purpose and retention.
3. Map AI provider, deployer, processor and subcontractor responsibilities.
4. Review cross-border data flows, sovereign residency/availability needs, contractual and regulatory approval.
5. Assess public-sector/financial services-specific supervisory frameworks where relevant.
6. Determine whether EU AI Act obligations are relevant to **specific cross-border activities**; do not assert automatic applicability in UAE.
7. Obtain DPO/legal signoff before any personal data use or automated consequential decision.
