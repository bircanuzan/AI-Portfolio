# Strategic workforce planning — scenario framework

## Enterprise planning problem
Atlas UAE Shared Services faces changing customer/employee support demand and possible AI-assisted workflows. Leadership needs a 3-year *capability* plan, not a claim that technology automatically reduces jobs.

## Planning unit and flow
- Corporate value outcome -> service demand -> task volumes, complexity and exceptions -> human work hours -> role/skill demand.
- Compare current supply and pipeline -> scarcity and mobility -> training, hiring, contracting, technology pilot or process redesign.
- Separate **tasks** from job titles; map learning pathways to recurring human judgment responsibilities.
- Make all scenario assumptions visible, timestamped and owned by named HR and finance sponsors.

## Three scenario definitions
| Scenario | Demand | AI assistance | Human review | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reference | Source-estimated case volumes | None | Current documented controls | Counterfactual baseline |
| Guarded assist | Estimated case volumes | Eligibility and useful assistance bounded by test evidence | Explicit reviewed minutes per assisted case | Conditional pilot hypothesis |
| Demand shock | Demand +20% illustrative | Guarded assist; may face bottlenecks | Review load retained | Stress-test service coverage |

**Do not** interpret synthetic monthly case inputs as actual corporate workload. "AI eligible" does not mean "AI successful" in the real world; the script now models an explicit **synthetic assistance success rate (0.55–0.72)**. Only successful assisted cases receive time-saved credit, while every eligible attempt still incurs review cost. Recalibrate from independent observed tests before any business use.

## Skills and capability strategy
Capability demand dimensions: AI literacy, agent supervision, critical judgment, service empathy, data interpretation, process design, cyber/privacy awareness and work-specific expertise. Score required versus current aggregate proficiency; provide training/redeployment options and human assessment. Do not rank actual employees.

## Example decisions
1. Does HR Service need an additional case manager, changed queue design or more supervised AI support?
2. Where do early-career employees acquire judgment after routine tasks change?
3. Which scarce technology skills should be hired, built internally or borrowed temporarily?
4. How do UAE nationalization commitments, fairness and local labor policy affect scenario feasibility? **Legal applicability must be checked separately.**

## Human governance gates
- **G0:** validated scope, data permissions and baseline.
- **G1:** scenario assumptions signed by operations, finance and HR.
- **G2:** independent impact assessment, accessibility and workforce consultation.
- **G3:** limited supervised pilot with actual intervention, corrections, and quality measures.
- **G4:** steering group approves or rejects any staffing or investment action.
- **G5:** monitor adverse effects, skill pipeline and genuine financial value.

## Draft CEO dashboard
Demand and uncertainty; FTE demand vs available supply; quality/rework/exception metrics; skills coverage heatmap; AI-assisted share *actually validated*; human review burden; source freshness and confidence labels; training velocity; internal mobility; risk incidents; benefit owner. The repository provides **specification**, not a deployed dashboard.
