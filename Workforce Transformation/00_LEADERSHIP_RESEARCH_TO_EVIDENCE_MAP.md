## Chief executive perspectives within the same operating model
- **Chief Workforce Transformation Officer:** task decomposition, demand/capacity, operating model, future workforce and economics.
- **CHRO:** skill and job architecture, fair talent transition, human judgement, junior talent progression, employee safeguards and labor-market alignment.
- **Chief AI Officer/CTO:** AI product architecture, identity and data boundaries, multi-agent routing, failure/exception path, test coverage and runtime cost.
- **CFO:** credible baseline, human-review burden, business case, cost avoidance versus actual cash savings.
- **CISO/Legal:** independent evaluation, data privacy, approval and risk acceptance, suspension and appeal.

## Research-to-evidence standard
The first column reflects a **research dossier about third parties**. The third reflects **independent fictional HumanEvoX work samples**. Neither creates prior job experience at those organizations. Use direct quotes, methods, branded platforms and outcome figures only with accurate attribution and current source verification. Never imply source research proves a real pilot, regulator approval, payment savings, employment outcome or LLM integration.

## Technical proof reality
Local Python scripts, CSV/JSON fixtures, policy gating, scenario calculation and decision-support arithmetic are implemented. Oracle Fusion HCM/OCI, Microsoft Copilot/Copilot Studio, Power BI, Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, OpenAI Frontier, LLM inference, APIs and live employee systems are **conceptual integration options, not connected services**.
