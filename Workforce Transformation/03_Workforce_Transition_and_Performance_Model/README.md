# 03 — Workforce Transition and Performance Model

**Business problem:** A future-state role chart alone does not ensure a safe, adopted operating model. Managers need a staged transition with realistic accountability.

## Outputs
- [90-day transition plan](30_60_90_transition_plan.md)
- [Illustrative KPI dictionary and gates](metrics.csv)

## Method and governance
1. Baseline process and service quality before discussing efficiency.
2. Consult staff and managers about hidden work, exceptions, fair work allocation, redeployment impacts and customer needs.
3. Agree RACI, role profiles, quality-review checkpoints and an accessible learning plan.
4. Run a limited pilot with opt-out/rollback route and explicit employee safeguards.
5. Revalidate capacity, job architecture and learning needs after observed evidence; human leadership authorizes any workforce change.

**Team integration:** Business sponsor (priorities); finance (economics); operations (service model); HR (people impacts); IT/security (access); AI practitioners (platform quality); legal (employment implications).

**Anti-patterns:** Immediate layoffs based on an AI throughput estimate; treating AI-generated work as automatically correct; using daily activity counts as individual performance ratings; claiming savings before process stabilization.

**Evidence:** Hypothetical implementation framework; no client change programme or measured reductions.
