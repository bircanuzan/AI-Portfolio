## Purpose and scope

This organization will use AI responsibly, with valid purposes, suitable security and privacy, proportionate controls and effective human accountability. Applies to internally built AI, agents, purchased services and third-party integrations within the approved scope.

## Proposed policy commitments

1. **AI inventory:** Every material use case has an owner, version, purpose, data inventory and decision-impact classification before deployment.
2. **Applicable legal obligations:** Privacy/legal assess UAE and any sector/free-zone/cross-border requirements. Do not assume voluntary guidelines replace law.
3. **Fairness:** Identify affected groups, potential unjustified harm and mitigations; test with lawful/authorized data.
4. **Transparency and explainability:** Disclose meaningful AI involvement as appropriate, communicate limitations, and provide human challenge route.
5. **Human oversight:** Qualified people retain final approval for consequential employment decisions. Agents cannot unilaterally reject candidates.
6. **Security and data protection:** Least privilege, approved credentials, encryption, retention controls, supplier review and logging without secrets.
7. **Supplier management:** Verify vendor claims independently; assess contracts and technical controls.
8. **Change control:** Re-assess new models, prompts, data, tools, scope or autonomy before material rollout.
9. **Monitoring:** Define failures, escalation thresholds, review cadence, incident containment and safe rollback.
10. **Evidence retention:** Preserve approvals, assessment versions, tests and exception decisions under an approved retention/access schedule.

## Release gates

| Gate | Required output | Approver |
|---|---|---|
| G0 Intake | Use case registration | Business owner |
| G1 Design | Applicable-law review and impact assessment | Legal/privacy + governance |
| G2 Test | Validated cases and unresolved risks | Evaluator + security |
| G3 Go / Hold | Residual risk, oversight and monitoring decision | Authorized risk owner |
| G4 Operate | Review log and incident process | Operations |
| G5 Retire | Access revocation and approved data disposal | IT + privacy |

**Illustrative hold rule:** a credible unmitigated critical privacy, security or rights impact prevents production authorization until fixed and retested.

## RACI shorthand

**Executive sponsor:** risk appetite and escalation. **AI governance lead:** policy controls. **Legal/privacy:** binding-law interpretation. **Security:** technical access and threat review. **Business owner:** intended use and outcomes. **Human reviewer:** consequential decisions. **Independent assessor:** challenge tests.

## Exceptions

Record exception ID, controls affected, business rationale, residual risk, compensating measures, expiry and independent approval. Never treat this draft as authorization to bypass law.

## Suggested KPIs (not measured)

Systems inventoried (%); high-risk reviews completed (%); unresolved critical findings (count); candidate appeals resolved (count); policy exceptions overdue (count); material changes with test evidence (%).
