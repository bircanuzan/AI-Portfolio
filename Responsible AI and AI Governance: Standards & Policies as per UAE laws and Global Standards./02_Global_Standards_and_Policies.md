# 02 — Global Standards and Policies

Review these **after** the UAE section. Each reference below has a different purpose and legal status.

## ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI Management System

Defines requirements for building, operating, reviewing and improving an AI Management System (AIMS).

**Use in this project:** AI policy, system inventory, accountability, lifecycle controls, internal review, corrective actions and management oversight.

**Evidence:** Policy approval, risk register, evaluation records, review minutes. **No ISO certification is claimed.**

Official: https://www.iso.org/standard/42001

## ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — AI risk management guidance

Supports systematic AI risk identification, evaluation, treatment, communication and monitoring.

**Use:** risk scenarios, owners, mitigation decisions and documented residual risk.

Official: https://www.iso.org/standard/77304.html

## ISO/IEC 42005:2025 — AI system impact assessment

Guidance for considering potential impacts of an AI system across its life cycle, especially on affected individuals and wider society.

**Use:** impacts on candidates and employees, severity, mitigations, consultation and review triggers.

Official: https://www.iso.org/standard/42005

## ISO/IEC 38507:2022 — Governance implications of AI

Guidance for organization leaders/governing bodies regarding responsible use of AI.

**Use:** executive oversight, accountability, strategic alignment and documented decision rights.

Official: https://www.iso.org/standard/56641.html

## NIST AI RMF 1.0 + Generative AI Profile (NIST AI 600-1)

The voluntary framework's four linked functions are **GOVERN, MAP, MEASURE, MANAGE**. Its 2024 GenAI Profile provides additional generative-AI risk considerations.

**Use:** governance rules, context mapping, adversarial and performance evaluations, incident handling and continuous monitoring.

Official: https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework  
GenAI: https://www.nist.gov/publications/artificial-intelligence-risk-management-framework-generative-artificial-intelligence

## EU Artificial Intelligence Act

A European regulation with risk-based obligations and phased applicability. A UAE company must determine whether its activities or outputs bring it within territorial/material scope and what role it performs. Recruitment and employment AI may be classed as high-risk where the legal criteria apply.

**Use:** legal-scope memo, system classification, compliance-owner review and obligation tracking — **only where applicable**.

Official implementation timeline (check for amendments): https://ai-act-service-desk.ec.europa.eu/en/ai-act/eu-ai-act-implementation-timeline

## OECD AI Principles

Intergovernmental principles for trustworthy AI, promoting human rights, transparency, accountability, robustness and inclusive benefit. Updated 2024.

**Use:** transparent user communication and oversight design.

Official: https://www.oecd.org/en/topics/ai-principles.html

## UNESCO Recommendation on the Ethics of AI

Ethical recommendation adopted in 2021, addressing human rights, fairness, inclusion, societal effects, governance and safeguards.

**Use:** stakeholder/impact assessment and risk review for affected groups.

Official: https://www.unesco.org/en/legal-affairs/recommendation-ethics-artificial-intelligence
