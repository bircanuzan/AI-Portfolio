# Optional 60-day HR-to-AI portfolio roadmap — about five hours per week

**Not a prerequisite to applying for jobs.** For a candidate prioritising immediate employment, existing evidence and applications take precedence. The plan can also be used as a team onboarding or capability-assessment exercise.

| Week | Approx. hours | Deliverable and verification |
|---|---:|---|
| 1 | 5 | Read six pathways; calibrate only verified experience levels; identify UAE JD knockout requirements |
| 2 | 5 | Run role matcher; produce shortlist of 2–3 realistic job families with capability gaps |
| 3 | 5 | Check 200-row synthetic dataset, data dictionary, missingness and reproducible attrition rates |
| 4 | 5 | Explain attrition findings, small-sample caveats and privacy-safe recommendations; no causal claims |
| 5 | 5 | Review 12 fictional AI screening risks and define vendor evidence / appeal mechanisms |
| 6 | 5 | Write a risk register, owner/mitigation/status and release HOLD recommendation |
| 7 | 5 | Execute offline 20-question HR policy routing evaluator and inspect failure examples |
| 8 | 5 | Document remediation backlog, human escalation boundary, one-page business case and interview walkthrough |

**Remainder of days 57–60:** Review documentation, portfolio links and job-aligned CV narratives; no additional hours assumed beyond the weekly envelope.

### Quality gates
- Every dataset is labelled synthetic, and every KPI states its denominator.
- Source and version of any actual HR policy must be controlled; **none of the fictional rules here is UAE legal advice**.
- Evaluation must distinguish reference expectations, executable output and analyst commentary.
- A failed test stays a failure; never rewrite the ground truth to improve a percentage.
- A portfolio exercise is never described as live client work.

**Output:** role taxonomy + explainable score + three small evidence cases + reusable job-interview narrative.
