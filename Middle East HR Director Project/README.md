# Middle East HR Director Project
## Regional HR Leadership | GCC Workforce Strategy | Operating Excellence

**Portfolio type:** Independent case study and implementation blueprint. **Not** an Excelerate Energy assignment, deployment, or representation of its internal practices.

### Executive brief
This project demonstrates a practical operating framework for a Middle East HR Director supporting a safety-critical, internationally distributed energy/infrastructure business. It integrates global HR policy with UAE/GCC local requirements and business-led people priorities.

### Scope
1. Regional HR partnering and leadership cadence with regional executives and the global CHRO.
2. Workforce demand, critical-role coverage, successor readiness, country expansion and restructuring scenarios.
3. Employee lifecycle: recruitment, offers, onboarding, probation, visa/mobility interfaces and exit controls.
4. Employee relations: consistent case intake, confidentiality, procedural fairness, Legal/Compliance escalation and jurisdiction-specific advice.
5. Talent/performance: performance calibration, succession, capability development, engagement and retention actions.
6. Compensation and benefits: global/local policy alignment, reward-cycle controls, pay equity checks and approvals.
7. HRIS governance: Workday-style data ownership, data-quality rules, access, audit trails and exception tracking.
8. Change and integration: acquisitions, reorganisations and new-country entry; stakeholder communications and HSE interfaces.
