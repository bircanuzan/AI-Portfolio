# 04 - Employee Relations, Country Compliance and HR Data Controls

## Employee relations escalation (illustrative)
1. **Intake:** capture concern, employee safety / retaliation risk, country/entity and conflict-of-interest checks; restrict access.
2. **Triage:** classify immediate HSE/legal/compliance concern, routine performance, conduct, grievance, or sensitive allegation.
3. **Fact finding:** coordinate impartial review with Legal/Compliance; preserve records under approved retention rules; document employee responses.
4. **Resolution:** decision by appropriate authority with local legal validation, proportionate action, written reasons and appeal/response routes.
5. **Closure:** secure restricted case file, monitor corrective actions and follow up for retaliation/recurrence.

Never treat an AI tool as the final authority for employment, disciplinary or legal decisions.

## Compliance register design
| Control | Owner | Evidence | Review frequency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contracts, addenda and signatures | HR Ops + Legal | Approved templates, employee record | Hire/change |
| Employment eligibility and immigration | HR Ops / PRO | Permit, expiry and status log | Weekly exception check |
| Payroll and statutory requirements | Payroll + Finance | Reconciliation and proof of submission | Monthly |
| Leave, working time and entitlements | HR Ops + Legal | Country matrix and manager guidance | Quarterly and on change |
| Termination and final settlement | HR + Payroll + Legal | Checklist, approvals and settlement | Each case |
| Personal data and access control | HRIS + Security | Roles, access review, audit trail | Quarterly |
| HSE workforce implications | HSE + HR | Incident interfaces and training records | Event-driven |
| Country-entry or integration | Regional HR + Legal | Diligence and labour-risk assessment | Before launch |

**Legal caveat:** Actual UAE/GCC requirements change and vary by legal entity and jurisdiction; this blueprint does not substitute for local legal advice. No real employee files or corporate policies are included.
