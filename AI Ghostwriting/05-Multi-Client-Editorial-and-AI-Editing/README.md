# Multi-Client Editorial System & AI-Assisted Editing
**Independent demonstration | Synthetic briefs and drafts | No live campaign results**

## Brief
Show how a freelancer can manage founder, coach and AI CEO voices, generate angles, meet agreed deadlines and edit AI-assisted drafts. This supports the multi-profile LinkedIn scope and the earlier LinkedIn/X leadership content opportunities.

## Intake template
Audience; business objective; desired reader action; three representative writing examples; approved experiences; evidence for numbers and product claims; prohibited topics; confidentiality boundaries; publishing cadence; reviewer; turnaround time.

## Voice separation
| Persona | Sentence rhythm | Preferred evidence | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|
| Operations founder | Direct and concrete | Workflow decisions and approved examples | Hype and unverified customer wins |
| Executive coach | Reflective and conversational | Questions and management situations | Invented client transformations |
| AI CEO | Precise and explanatory | Design tradeoffs and verified capabilities | Unsupported model superiority |

## AI drafting instruction
Use only the supplied brief and approved source material. Draft three distinct angles for the stated audience. Flag missing facts in editorial notes; never insert invented statistics, client stories or quotations. Follow the voice guide. Keep the draft separate from the claim register. A human reviewer must approve publication.

This is a reusable instruction template, not a claim that a particular model or workflow has been deployed.

## Before and after
### Synthetic rough draft
“In today's rapidly evolving landscape, AI is revolutionizing every aspect of HR. Our cutting-edge solution eliminates bias and boosts productivity by 70%, empowering organizations to unlock unprecedented potential.”

### Editorial diagnosis
The draft has no defined reader, makes an absolute fairness claim and introduces an unsupported percentage. It also asserts a product exists. None is supported by the exercise brief.

### Edited LinkedIn post
Before using AI in recruitment, write down which decisions remain with people.

Start with a narrow task.

Drafting a job description.
Summarizing interview notes.
Checking whether an application contains required information.

Then define what the system must not infer, what evidence the reviewer needs and how a candidate can raise a concern.

A useful pilot makes these boundaries visible.

It also checks for errors across the situations the workflow will actually encounter.

A polished answer is only one part of the review.

The hiring team still needs to understand how the output was produced and whether it is appropriate to use.

Which recruitment task would you test first, and what would make you stop the pilot?

## Four-week editorial plan
| Week | Founder | Coach | AI CEO | Review focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Exception handling | Decision boundaries | Agent authority | Voice calibration |
| 2 | Buying criteria | Delegation | Tool failures | Example specificity |
| 3 | Manual fallback | Feedback conversations | Source conflicts | Claim verification |
| 4 | Reader questions | Manager Q&A | Evaluation design | Audience relevance |

## Proposed delivery workflow
Monday: receive approved inputs and identify missing evidence.
Tuesday: submit angles and draft batch.
Wednesday: revise from consolidated feedback.
Thursday: deliver approved platform versions.
Friday: review audience responses and maintain the next idea bank.
Agree actual cadence and capacity with the client before commitment.

## Quality rubric
Score each dimension 0–2: fidelity to brief, voice distinction, specific insight, clarity, hook relevance, claim support, useful closing and platform fit. Any unsupported factual claim blocks release regardless of total. This rubric is proposed; no fabricated scores or engagement lifts are reported.

## Measurement design
Track posts published, revisions, approval lead time, substantive audience replies and attributable inquiries. Keep reach and engagement separate from commercial outcomes. Compare comparable periods only after enough real data exists. Do not promise inbound revenue or algorithmic reach.

## Final handoff checklist
Source traceable; claims supported; no confidential detail; voice approved; no invented anecdote; links checked; requested format complete; owner approval recorded. Publish only through the client's authorized process.
