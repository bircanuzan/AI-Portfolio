# HR policy assistant evaluation skill
1. Identify whether the target is a deterministic router, generative LLM, or enterprise Copilot tenant; never mix results from different systems.
2. Require a versioned approved policy corpus for any real evaluation; the Project 53 examples are fictional only.
3. For each test: ID, exact prompt, expected behavior, actual verbatim output, policy citation/version, human assessment and timestamp.
4. Include routine answer, exception, third-party privacy, ambiguous paraphrase, unknown, contradictory and adversarial scenarios.
5. Score by clear rubrics and preserve every failure; perform retesting without silently changing expectations.
6. Distinguish offline local outputs from hosted-model outputs; refuse to infer production legal compliance.
7. Summarize failure severity, release decision, and human handoff steps.
