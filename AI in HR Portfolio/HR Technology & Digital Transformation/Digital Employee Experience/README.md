# Digital Employee Experience | HR Service Design & Journey Research

**Independent, fictional service-design case | Employee-centred research and experience optimisation**

## Unique purpose
Understand what employees and managers **experience** across HR touchpoints and define measurable improvements before choosing software. This is a **research / service design** project, not a functional HRIS specification, an employee portal implementation, a vendor purchase or an AI agent.

## Case
A fictional UAE multi-site employer hears that new joiners cannot tell which documents are required and employees repeatedly contact HR for case updates. Research must separate access problems, service design gaps, policy ambiguity and systems defects.

## Deliverables
1. [Research plan, consent and fictional personas](RESEARCH_AND_PERSONAS.md)

## Methods & technology exposure
Design thinking, service design, contextual enquiry, diary study, employee interviews, journey mapping, service blueprint, persona hypotheses, empathy maps, task-based usability testing, accessibility heuristics, service metrics and privacy-aware analytics. Figma/Miro could be used for visuals **but no design account or prototype connection is claimed**.

## What success would look like
Research demonstrates where employees become stuck and whether the proposed change improves task completion, clarity, confidence, accessibility and satisfaction. No measured improvement is invented.

**Evidence boundary:** All scenarios, personas and sample findings are fictional hypotheses, not actual user research or client observations.
