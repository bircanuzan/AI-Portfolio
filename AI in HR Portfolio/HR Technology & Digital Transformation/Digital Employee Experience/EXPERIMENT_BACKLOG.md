# Hypotheses and small experiments (not actual findings)

| Experiment | Hypothesis | Prototype | Measurement | Stop rule |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | Plain-language onboarding checklist improves clarity | 1-page checklist vs existing email sample | Correct next-step recall | Confusing eligibility wording |
| B | Visible service status reduces chasing | Mock status timeline | Follow-up-intent question | Employees mistake status for approval |
| C | Assisted service improves inclusion | Phone/HR-desk option beside digital intake | Completion by access needs | Inaccessible to a participant group |
| D | Clear error states prevent duplicate submissions | Inline validation and confirmation | Repeated submissions per task | Privacy-related error exposure |

Do not present these as proven employee-experience improvements. Capture actual usability sessions, accessibility reviews and HR content approval before making any quantitative claim.

## Handoff boundary
Only validated needs become requirements for **Employee Self-Service** or **HRTech Implementation**. Journey research does not choose suppliers, configure HCM fields or build a production portal.
