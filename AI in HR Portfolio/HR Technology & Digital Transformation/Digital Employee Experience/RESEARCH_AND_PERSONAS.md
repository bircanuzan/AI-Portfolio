# Employee experience research protocol

## Objective
Investigate the end-to-end experience of HR services, not simply the usability of a button or an HRMS screen.

## Proposed sample
- Employee segments: new joiners, frontline non-desk workers, office workers, managers, people requiring assistive technology and HR shared services.
- Explore English and relevant additional languages, shift patterns, device access and assisted-service needs.
- Sampling proposed only; **no real participants were recruited or interviewed**.

## Research questions
1. How do employees discover HR policies and required documents?
2. What is confusing about the initial request versus status updates?
3. Which steps depend on manager/HR hand-offs?
4. What alternate channel is available when an employee cannot use the portal?
5. Which details should employees **not** have to disclose to resolve a request?

## Fictional proto-personas (not validated)
| Persona | Context | Hypothesised friction | Research question |
|---|---|---|---|
| New joiner A | Starts a role in Dubai, mobile-first | Unclear sequence of joining tasks | Can they find the next step in 60 seconds? |
| Manager B | Oversees multiple employees | Cannot see only the cases requiring their approval | Are ownership and decision limits clear? |
| Frontline worker C | Limited desktop access | Long form and inflexible contact hours | Is an assisted, accessible channel possible? |

## Ethical data protocol
Obtain consent, minimise contact details, do not record sensitive employee information in public tools, quote only anonymised responses after permission, and distinguish observations from interpretation. No real data is stored here.
