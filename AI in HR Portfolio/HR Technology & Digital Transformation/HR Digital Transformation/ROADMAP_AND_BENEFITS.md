# 18-month transformation roadmap — proposed, not deployed

| Phase | Time | Major outcomes | Entry / exit gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discover | Months 0-2 | Service baseline, stakeholder interviews, risk register, problem statements | HR/Finance agree measurement definitions |
| Design | Months 3-4 | Prioritised business case, future operating model, product/selection requirements | Steering committee approves budget envelope |
| Select | Months 5-6 | Vendor due diligence managed by the separate selection project | Security/IT/Finance procurement signoff |
| Pilot | Months 7-9 | Small employee-service cohort, assisted support and feedback | UAT + privacy/accessibility signoffs |
| Expand | Months 10-12 | Controlled service rollout, training, documented hypercare | Zero critical defects, support readiness |
| Optimise | Months 13-18 | Feedback-led revisions, data quality and verified benefit reporting | Value owner certifies actual measurements |

## Illustrative assumptions and measurement
Use **AED and hours only as inputs to validate**, not as achieved results. Example starting assumption: 2,400 HR requests/month, 18 minutes average manual handling, AED 85 fully loaded hourly labour cost. Annual handling cost estimate = `2,400 * 18 / 60 * 85 * 12 = AED 734,400` **only under those hypothetical assumptions**. There is no claim any intervention will reduce that amount.

### Benefit register
- Service response time: median open-to-first-human-response (hours).
- Case handling: paid work minutes per resolved case, sampled and signed off by the owner.
- Employee experience: post-service satisfaction, qualitative research and resolved-on-first-contact share.
- Control assurance: unauthorised change incidents, open critical exceptions and audit-finding closure.
- Adoption: eligible employees using approved self-service, with assisted channel retained for accessibility.
- Financial value: verified incremental hours released / external spend avoided **net of** tooling, change, support and risk mitigation costs.

Benefits owner must approve numerator, denominator, before/after window and exclusions. Never count vendor estimates or simulated values as actual ROI.

## Stop/continue criteria
Stop if privacy assessment fails, work is not accessible to affected employees, pay/employment approval is automated without authorised human controls, or the purported benefit lacks a measurable baseline.
