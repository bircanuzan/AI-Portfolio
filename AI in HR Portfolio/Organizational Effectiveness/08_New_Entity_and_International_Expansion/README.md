# 08 | New Company / International Location Human Capital Launch

## Fictional growth scenario
Group launches Cedar Digital Services LLC, with proposed team in a new jurisdiction. **No country-specific legal advice is assumed**; local counsel is a prerequisite.

## Integrated launch gates
- **E0 investment mandate:** legal entity purpose, business model, forecast P&L, location risk, headcount ceilings, accountable executive.
- **E1 jurisdiction readiness:** entity registration, employment/immigration, payroll, licensing, tax, immigration, labour law, data transfers; qualified in-country legal/finance review.
- **E2 operating model:** target customers, service catalogue, activity volumes, retained/outsourced/shared processes, delegated authority, accountable executive/board.
- **E3 jobs and reward:** position ledger, locally compliant JDs, grade-to-pay-market benchmark via licensed vendors, skills, mobility policy, costs and expatriate/host-country terms.
- **E4 hiring/technology:** authorised requisitions, contractor governance, HRIS/legal-entity setup, payroll vendor/expense controls, employee self-service, access/role design and UAT.
- **E5 operational readiness:** employee handbook, privacy notices, grievances, learning/safety, continuity, reporting and management assurance.
- **E6 go-live:** CFO/CPO/Legal/IT security sign-off and 30/60/90 post-opening risk review.

## Decision artefacts
Entity launch checklist; 24-month FTE/cost sensitivity; 0/30/90-day organisation charts; policy localisation matrix; recruitment sequence and lead times; budget vs hiring stage gates; transition support, culture and employee consultation plan.

## Critical safeguard
Do not apply UAE employment, VAT, data or governance law wholesale to another country. Cross-border mobility, employment and tax positions require jurisdiction-specific specialists and effective-dated approvals.
