# 10 | Establishing & Leading the Organisation Effectiveness Function

## Proposed team structure (roles, not incumbents)
Executive Director OE (strategy/committee) → OE Design Lead (org models and change control), Job Architecture / Reward Governance Lead (grade parity), Strategic Workforce Planning Lead (Finance, scenarios and position control), People Analytics / Data Steward (reconciliations and dashboards), and an OE PMO / Business Partnering capability shared across entities.

## Capacity and prioritisation
Work intake scoring based on financial/legal risk, strategy alignment, employee impact, delivery date and dependencies. Reserve delivery slots for monthly reconciliation, controlled structural approvals and strategic initiatives. Cross-train specialists and use independent review to reduce key-person risk.

## Management cadence
- Daily high-priority decisions / exceptions triage; no unapproved HRIS updates.
- Weekly entity HRBPs/TA/Finance workforce and requisition control review.
- Monthly cost close, people scorecard, structural integrity, roles/grading exceptions and people strategy deliverables.
- Quarterly CPO/HCC structural portfolio, scenarios, succession risks and programme decisions.

## Team capability standards
Organisation design (Galbraith Star Model as a reference, not certification), activity analysis, service catalogues, RACI/RAPID and governance; job evaluation / parity; financial modelling; Oracle Fusion/Workday/SAP HCM data semantics; SQL/Python/Power BI; responsible AI use-case assessment; change management and executive storytelling. Distinguish familiar tools from those actually demonstrated in code.

## Demonstrating OE leadership
Set accountable outcomes, role scorecards, feedback and calibration; maintain a skill matrix and peer-review rubric; allocate resources across BAU and transformation; audit retrospective decisions for fairness, timeliness, error rates, stakeholder confidence and documented learning.

## Performance measures
On-time gated org-change approvals (not speed alone), reconciliation closure, budget breaches prevented, consistency of job families and grades, quality of committee briefing, readiness of planned critical roles, and verified change outcomes. No fabricated achievements or team management experience are implied.
