# 02 | Organisation Design, Service Catalogue & Restructuring

## Methodology: strategy → activity → organisation
1. **Strategic intake:** entity targets, decision rights, regulatory constraints, cost envelope, stakeholder map.
2. **Service catalogue:** services, customers, SLAs, volumes, capabilities, criticality, owning team and automation suitability.
3. **Activity-based workload:** transaction volume × handling time ÷ productive annual capacity; normalise seasonality, leave, error handling, supervision and variable demand. Do not infer redundancies mechanically.
4. **Current-state anatomy:** legal entities, reporting lines, spans/layers, managerial ratio, control gaps, decision bottlenecks, capabilities and workforce demographics only where lawful/necessary.
5. **Three futures:** A retain decentralised; B shared-services COE and retained HRBPs; C shared services + tightly governed automation, preserving accountable human decision owners.
6. **Option appraisal:** strategic fit 25%, cost 20%, customer experience 20%, governance/risk 20%, implementation feasibility 15%. Scores are facilitation inputs, not a substitute for leadership judgement.
7. **Costed To-Be:** org chart, approved posts, grade, estimated employee and vendor run-rate, one-time severance/relocation/reskilling/implementation costs, risk and business continuity.
8. **Change control:** written sponsoring CEO/CFO/Reward/Legal views, delegated decision authority, effective-dated HRIS change, employee consultation, communications, 30/60/90 transition and post-go-live review.

## Example: Group People Services
| Service | Current owner | Design-B recommendation | Quality/decision safeguard |
|---|---|---|---|
| Employee policy queries | Entity HRBPs | Group service centre + entity-local exception desk | Legal-approved policy version and escalation |
| Position request entry | Talent Acquisition | Central position-control workflow | No requisition unless approved position or formal exception |
| Organisation charts | Separate entity HRIS exports | One effective-dated position ledger | People Data performs monthly reconciliation |
| Job evaluations | Entity HR partners | Group Reward calibration panel | Documented factors, parity review and appeal |
| Workforce plans | Local Excel forecasts | Group assumptions / entity-specific demand | Finance certifies costs and entity GM signs assumptions |

## Outputs a steering committee receives
Current/To-Be organograms; RACI and retained/central activities; spans/layers by function; vacancy and headcount bridge; expected service-level impacts; benefits **hypothesis** and disbenefits; a risk-adjusted financial case; transition calendar; decision log.

## Risk/people controls
Ensure change proposals are reviewed for employee fairness, discrimination, consultation, labour law, critical-role coverage, retention, safety, cross-border implications and cyber/data access. Do not treat automation-capacity estimates as direct FTE cuts or actual savings.

**Distinct from Workforce Transformation 04:** this is the *formal Group structural approval lifecycle* across entities and budgets, not a digital-worker operating-model experiment.
