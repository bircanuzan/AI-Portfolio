# 08 | CEO / Transformation Steering Committee — Decision Memo

**Portfolio simulation — NO real client endorsement or implementation outcome**

## Decision requested
Authorize a **90-day diagnostic and controlled pilot design** for Northstar Clean Energy Services' integrated organization / people transformation. **Do not** authorize enterprise restructuring, workforce reductions or enterprise HR technology purchase at this stage.

## Business case in one minute
The fictional company’s 600-person, three-country structure faces weak accountability at functional handoffs, incomplete capability visibility, uneven coaching and fragmented HR service. Changing only the org chart would not solve this: the future model requires role clarity, learning investments, manager behavior reinforcement, performance measures and HR-service governance.

### Model choice
**Option B — a value-stream matrix, with country compliance overlays — is recommended for a bounded pilot**, contingent on workforce consultation, compliance review and baseline testing.

**No investment appraisal is validated.** Net run-rate excludes risk, taxes, discounting, phasing, severance, implementation variability and potential other costs. These illustrative numbers are not a client forecast.

## Five integrated interventions
1. **Organization redesign:** identify value-stream owners and document service decision rights and country overlays.
2. **Capability assessment:** validate eight aggregate capability domains; prioritize coaching, critical skills, HR data stewardship and ownership clarity using an auditable score.
3. **Cultural transformation:** design observable collaboration, safe challenge and customer-centric behaviors; measure through representative pulse and process evidence.
4. **Performance architecture:** balance customer outcomes, excellence, collaboration and development; calibrate fairly with human review.
5. **HR transformation:** establish HRBP / CoE / shared-service accountabilities, case ownership, controlled HRIS data and phased UAT.

## What the board should watch
Quality of employee consultation, local legal compliance, manager capacity, safety and customer service during transition, evidence confidence, change fatigue and CFO-verified benefits. Improvement in one metric must not mask harm in another.

## Conditional authority and next 30 days
- CEO: approve charter, named executive sponsors and bounded baseline discovery.
- CHRO + country HR: run employee consultation and human impact safeguards.
- COO: nominate process/value-stream owners and provide service timings.
- CFO: validate cost and benefit definitions prior to investment case.
- CIO and Legal: authorize only safe, minimal data use and HRIS diagnostic access.
- PMO: return with a reconciled baseline, pilot design, risks and a go/no-go recommendation.

**Success criterion for the diagnostic:** a decision-quality business case and a safe, testable implementation plan — **not** a claimed ROI or a promise of headcount savings.
