# 04 | 24-Month Delivery Programme — Diagnose to BAU Handover

## Phased programme
| Months | Workstream | Measurable exit criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1–3 | Mobilise, inventory, discover | Country/BU scope signed; >95% critical job data coverage target; decision rights ratified |
| 4–6 | Design taxonomy and levels | Job family/level dictionary v1, title standards, stakeholder design review |
| 7–9 | Evaluation and grade calibration | Approved calibration samples, outlier log, local grade crosswalk proposals |
| 10–12 | Pilot 1–2 business units/markets | HRIS UAT signed, role mapping and employee communications tested, defects triaged |
| 13–18 | Wave-based international rollout | Batch mapping, local legal checks, grade governance, training by market |
| 19–21 | Stabilise and tune | Exception backlog controlled, audit findings addressed, metrics stable |
| 22–24 | Transfer ownership | Published SOPs, named BAU owners, playbooks, 90-day post-handover review plan |

**Targets are proposed project controls, not actual programme results.**

## Accountabilities (RACI, simplified)
| Decision/activity | Global OD | Reward | Local HR | BU owner | HRIS | Legal |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Job family / level taxonomy | A/R | C | C | C | C | I |
| Approved job content | C | C | R | A | I | C |
| Formal job evaluation / grade approval | C | A/R | C | C | I | C |
| Local title or grade exception | A | R | R | C | I | C |
| HRIS mapping and migration | C | C | C | I | A/R | C |
| Employee communications | C | C | A/R | R | I | C |
| Country legal interpretation | I | C | R | C | I | A |

R = Responsible; A = Accountable; C = Consulted; I = Informed. Adapt to employer authority framework.

## Programme management
Workstreams: design; evaluation; Reward; HRIS/data; local market adaptation; adoption; compliance; PMO. Maintain approved milestone baseline, RAID log, formal scope changes, weekly delivery huddles, monthly steering committee.

## Critical dependencies
- Country law and collective bargaining before crosswalk activation.
- Formal job evaluation before changes with compensation or employee implications.
- Employee data migration and position approvals before HRIS cutover.
- Local operations staffing/safety contingencies before org chart changes.
- Sufficient HRBP and line manager capacity for calibration workshops.

## Stop / redesign triggers
Material unintended pay impact, unapproved role mapping, insufficient manager sign-off, data mismatches, operational safety risk or increased grievances => pause affected wave, contain, remediate and reapprove.
