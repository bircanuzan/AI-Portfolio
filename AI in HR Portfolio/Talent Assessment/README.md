# Talent Assessment

A curated HumanEvoX portfolio of four distinct evidence-based **Talent Assessment** projects. These are original demonstrations; simulated candidate records and hypothetical test scores must not be represented as client work, assessor qualifications or licensed psychometric assessments.

## Evidence standards

- Competency frameworks, structured interviewing, assessment-centre design, scoring and calibration are represented as project-specific examples, not professional accreditation.
- Psychometric-style exercises are based on invented inputs; no proprietary publisher tests or test-user certifications are claimed.
- Individual employment decisions require professional review and appropriate legal, privacy and fairness safeguards.
