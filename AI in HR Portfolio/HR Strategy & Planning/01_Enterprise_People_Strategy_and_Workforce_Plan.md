# 01 | Enterprise People Strategy & Strategic Workforce Planning

**Status:** Proposed framework, not bank delivery.

## 1. Strategy-to-workforce cascade
| Enterprise choice | Workforce implication | Accountable sponsor | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Digital channels / customer growth | Digital product, data, cyber and customer capabilities | Business executive + GCHRO | Approved business demand |
| Service quality and risk | Skill coverage, role clarity and training controls | Business, Risk, HR | Capability and control heatmap |
| UAE national talent development | Attraction, internal movement and career pathways | GCHRO + Emiratization lead | Applicable targets, pipeline and retention |
| Efficiency and productivity | Role redesign, automation impact and skills transitions | COO + Finance + HR | Demand/capacity scenarios |

## 2. Planning cadence
- **Annual:** Agree business assumptions, capability priorities, funded position envelope and critical-role inventory.
- **Quarterly:** Re-forecast demand, talent supply, attrition, costs, national workforce pipeline and capacity constraints.
- **Monthly:** Reconcile vacant/funded positions; monitor recruitment, time to competence, internal movement, people-cost drift and control failures.
- **Event-driven:** Trigger review for regulatory change, restructuring, acquisition, growth shift or material attrition.

## 3. Position data model
A position should carry: effective-dated position ID, entity, function, geography, cost centre, grade, manager, employment type, skills, approved/funded FTE, occupancy, planned start, compensation cost band, successor risk and control status.

**Integrity checks:** Every requisition must map to an approved funded position or controlled exception; monthly ERP/HRIS/payroll totals should reconcile. Headcount and FTE are separate measures.

## 4. Workforce supply and demand model
**Opening supply + confirmed joiners + forecast internal transfers in - forecast attrition - forecast transfers out = expected closing supply.**

**Gap = approved required capacity - expected available capacity** (adjust for part-time FTE, skills and productivity). Review permanent hiring, redeployment, upskilling, contingent capacity and automation, with risk and finance challenge.

## 5. Priority decision artefacts
- 3-year enterprise people-strategy map and 12-month implementation plan
- Workforce demand/supply/cost scenarios by function and critical capability
- Critical-role inventory, succession-readiness register, capability heatmap
- Emiratization-aware pipeline and retention reporting (validated for applicability)
- GCHRO committee memo with decisions, trade-offs, budget impact and controls

## 6. Constraints & evidence
Do not automate employment decisions solely from prediction scores. Separate scenario assumptions from confirmed business forecasts. Avoid publicizing individual succession or performance records in portfolio examples.
