# 02 | Integrated Talent, Learning, Succession & Performance Operating Model

**Status:** Independent design reference, not a deployed banking HR system.

## One annual rhythm
| Cycle | TA and onboarding | Learning | Talent / succession | Performance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | Approved hiring plan, critical jobs | Capability priorities and annual learning plans | Critical role and high-potential definitions | Objectives and goal cascade |
| Q2 | Quality-of-hire and ramp-up review | Delivery and LMS tracking | Leadership assessments, mobility | Mid-year feedback |
| Q3 | Demand reforecast and pipeline interventions | Skill-gap closure and academies | Succession slate review and development | Performance-risk support |
| Q4 | Hiring plan / budget closure | Effectiveness and training ROI review | Readiness calibration and retention action | Year-end reviews, calibration and governance |

## Talent Acquisition & onboarding
- Trigger by approved demand, not ungoverned requests.
- Scorecard: accepted offers, time to fill, new-hire retention, vacancy aging, quality-of-hire proxy, vendor performance and candidate experience.
- Separate quality and fair-selection controls from speed; ensure interview decisions and exceptions are documented.
- Build an onboarding-to-productivity measure at 30/60/90 days.

## Enterprise learning
- Capability taxonomy linked to strategic roles and role-based paths.
- Learning needs analysis through business risks, skills evidence, employee development goals and compliance requirements.
- Learning governance includes LMS accuracy, mandatory training exceptions, content owners, vendor spend, accessibility and outcome checks.
- Learning ROI should distinguish completions, observed capability improvement and business performance; avoid claiming causation from attendance alone.

## Talent & succession
- Define critical roles by business impact, external scarcity, risk concentration and replacement time.
- Use calibrated readiness bands (ready now / 1-2 years / longer horizon) with evidence.
- Pair assessments, coaching, mentoring, leadership academies, mobility and stretch assignments.
- Require fairness review and restricted access to potential/succession flags; keep employee development separate from unsupported algorithmic rankings.

## Performance
1. Objective setting with business outcome alignment.
2. Mid-year conversation and feedback.
3. Year-end evidence and ratings where applicable.
4. Cross-functional calibration with documented decisions, exception checks and appeals.
5. Development or improvement action with review dates.

**Guardrail:** An AI summary may assist preparation but cannot be the sole basis for hire, dismissal, promotion, rating or succession decisions.

## Interface design
- HRBP owns business demand interpretation and action plans.
- TA owns sourcing, selection process integrity and supplier controls.
- Learning owns learning architecture, delivery, LMS evidence and vendor outcomes.
- Talent/Performance owns succession, leadership review and PM governance.
- HR Strategy/Planning owns planning methodology, scorecards, portfolio dependencies and governance escalation.
