# 02 - 30 / 60 / 90-Day Regional HR Leadership Plan

**Status:** Illustrative plan for a Middle East HR Director role, not a commissioned company roadmap.

## Days 1–30 | Listen, assess, stabilise
- Conduct structured stakeholder interviews with country leads, CHRO, Finance, Legal, Compliance, HSE and HR service owners.
- Inventory entities, headcount, contracts, visa lifecycle and critical-role exposures; reconcile available HRIS vs local records.
- Review open employee-relations cases and urgent retention, policy, mobility and payroll risks.
- Map live governance forums, decision rights and exceptions; assess readiness for upcoming expansions.
- Deliver: current-state risk register, quick-win list, ownership map and baseline KPI definitions.

## Days 31–60 | Prioritise, design, align
- Build a 12-month workforce demand and critical-role scenario with operations and Finance.
- Agree talent and succession calibration standards, probation checkpoints, hiring handoffs and retention triggers.
- Create locally validated compliance matrix and ER escalation pathways.
- Specify HRIS data-quality checks, ownership, access model and source-of-truth reconciliation.
- Deliver: regional HR operating cadence, prioritized actions, dashboard prototype and stakeholder-approved governance.

## Days 61–90 | Execute, verify, embed
- Pilot the weekly critical-role / mobility risk meeting and monthly executive scorecard.
- Launch manager toolkits and communication for performance, onboarding and employee-relations workflows.
- Close high-risk data/documentation exceptions with auditable owner records.
- Present CHRO and regional leadership with results, unresolved risks and a 6–12-month implementation roadmap.
- Deliver: baseline-to-action scorecard, action log, residual-risk register and accountable workstream owners.

## Practical success tests
Documented critical-role coverage; audit-ready employee records; timely onboarding; credible HR case resolution; improved SLA discipline; named succession gaps; workforce changes reviewed with local counsel and Finance; leaders affirm usefulness of the HR cadence.

**Measurement note:** No numeric targets are invented for the employer. Thresholds and baselines must be agreed after due diligence.
