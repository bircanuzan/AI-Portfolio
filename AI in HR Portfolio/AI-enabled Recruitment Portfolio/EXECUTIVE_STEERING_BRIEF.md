# CHRO / Chief HR Transformation Officer / Chief AI Officer — Recruitment Transformation Steering Brief

**Independent fictional portfolio case | UAE / KSA multi-entity recruitment operating model | No client deployment or claimed business benefit**

## Investment thesis
Recruitment transformation is not simply recruiting faster or introducing an LLM. The organisational outcome is **evidence-based, fair, governable workforce acquisition that matches business-critical capabilities**. Executive decisions should balance talent demand, selection validity, candidate experience, recruiter capacity, regulatory exposure, inclusion, spend and the work humans must continue to own.

## Proposed 90-day transformation sequence
| Period | Outcome | Verification / gate |
|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 — Diagnose | Map TA tasks, interview capacity, hiring demand, handoffs, quality failures, accessibility and candidate communication; approve baseline measurement | CHRO signs KPI definitions; HR Legal confirms use and access requirements |
| Days 31–60 — Redesign | Simplify requisition and interview evidence workflows; clarify source ownership, job-related criteria and human approval; model no-AI and AI-assist options | Finance signs actual vendor cost assumptions; CHRO/CAIO approve minimal-risk pilot charter |
| Days 61–90 — Controlled pilot design | Pre-register acceptance/stop thresholds, source traceability, candidate experience measures, complaint/appeal channel, holdout comparison and rollback | No employment automation release without real independent review and accountable signoffs |

**No pilot is represented as run.** The plans are executive operating artifacts only.

## Executive scorecard specification (no fabricated observed performance)
- Demand & capacity: approved requisitions, eligible review queue, true reviewer workload, time in queue, capacity by skill/region.
- Quality & validity: structured evidence completeness, inter-rater agreement with appropriate methodology, procedural deviations, assessment accessibility.
- Candidate & hiring manager experience: response timeliness, status clarity, withdrawals by stage, feedback and assisted-channel availability.
- Risk & governance: unsupported sourcing rules, data minimisation, appeal closure, exceptions, vendor version changes, prompt/tool misuse and permission violations.
- Finance & value: fully loaded cost per process unit, real spend, work minutes actually reduced (not just staff capacity), platform and assurance operating cost; separately identify capacity release versus cash savings.
- Learning & adoption: documented task-level adoption, reviewer corrections, exception rate, workforce skills and training.

Each KPI requires owner, grain, numerator, denominator, data source, time period, exclusions, threshold and escalation policy. Suppress small groups; never publish real applicants in this repository.

## Proposed stage-gated funding decisions
- **Gate 0:** Is the hiring problem supported by job/workforce evidence? If no, clarify demand and remove process waste.
- **Gate 1:** Can non-AI standardisation solve the issue? Require comparison to simpler options, not an AI-first default.
- **Gate 2:** Does any AI assistance offer testable incremental value after reviewer time, cost, and legal/data controls? If no, do not fund.
- **Gate 3:** Has a contained nonproduction pilot passed measurable validity, privacy, fairness, accessibility and operational tests? If no, HOLD.
- **Gate 4:** Is there genuine signed authorisation for a *specific* approved task with monitoring, appeal and rollback? This portfolio does not meet it.

## Board risk appetite
Zero tolerance for unapproved automated rejection, undisclosed candidate surveillance, invented licence or immigration determinations, private candidate data placed in public tools, or autonomous offers/pay decisions. Other risks require explicit named owners, review intervals and budgeted mitigations.
