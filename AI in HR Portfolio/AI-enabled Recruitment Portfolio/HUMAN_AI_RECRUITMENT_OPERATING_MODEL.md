# Human–AI recruitment operating model — CHRO / CAIO work design

## Core principle
**Design work, accountability and controls before selecting tools.** A Claude-compatible skill is a *drafting specification*, not a deployed agent or evidence of autonomous action. The existing 17 skills are grouped by role purpose; do not build 17 nearly identical agents.

## Work-allocation blueprint
| TA work step | Eligible technology support (proposed, not executed) | Reserved human authority | Primary control |
|---|---|---|---|
| Requisition intake | Draft purpose, identify incomplete role criteria and source references | Hiring manager + HRBP approve job content and business priority | Missing source = no progression |
| JD drafting | Suggest inclusive phrasing from signed requirements | Qualified hiring owner approves content | No unsupported licences or protected-category proxies |
| Interview plan | Generate structured questions from approved rubric | Qualified assessors design, administer and evaluate | Evidence and accessibility review |
| Evidence note-taking | Organise authorised, non-sensitive interview observations | Trained panel retains original observations and selection judgement | Consent, accuracy, access, correction |
| Scheduling | Prepare slots and time-zone alternatives | Candidate and coordinator confirm | Consent and no automatic outbound contact |
| Offer packs | Assemble checklists and highlight gaps | HR, Finance, Legal and hiring authority approve all terms | Written signoff, no auto-offer |
| Mobility / credential handoffs | Flag unresolved documents and reference sources | Regulated practitioners / mobility / counsel verify law and eligibility | Official current guidance, individual case review |
| Candidate screening | **No autonomous selection, rejection or ranking authorised by this portfolio** | Qualified people conduct job-valid review with explainability/appeal | Project 55 release HOLD |

## Transformation operating model
- **Business / Talent leaders**: accountable for workforce need, critical skills, candidate relationship and human hiring outcomes.
- **HR Operations / TA CoE**: owns processes, accessible communications, HR service catalogue, controls and employee/candidate voice.
- **HRTech product owner**: owns architecture interfaces, workflow backlog, permission model and tested releases.
- **Chief AI Officer / AI assurance**: owns AI model/tool inventory, evaluations, provider contracts, AI security, monitoring, incident response and governance standards.
- **Legal / Privacy / Clinical / Global Mobility**: independent review for high-consequence country or professional requirements.
- **Finance**: signs investment assumptions, actual software costs and economic benefits.
- **Procurement / vendor risk**: owns DPIA/security due diligence and termination/portability clauses.

## Capacity and skills redesign
Recruiter work need not shrink automatically because drafting becomes faster. **Freed time is a capacity opportunity**, not an employment reduction or financial cash saving. The future-state allocation needs assessment validation, candidate guidance, empathy, exception investigation, AI output verification, stakeholder planning and analytics capability. HR and Finance must establish whether activity changes *increase capacity*, reduce external spend, improve service or alter budgeted headcount; each demands separate evidence.

## Phased implementation gates
1. Task-level process mining and initial workforce study.
2. No-AI baseline, privacy assessment, role requirements and data retention rules.
3. Simulated skill output tests with *explicit source traceability*, prompt injection and hallucination challenges.
4. Human-reviewed nonproduction pilot under approved vendor security and model monitoring.
5. Job validity, bias/impact and appeal assessment before any actual high-risk screening.
6. Executive decision, production monitoring, human override and tested incident rollback.

**Assurance boundary:** Project 55 currently validates written guardrails and synthetic Python logic only. It does not evaluate a live Claude model, real ATS or hiring outcomes.
