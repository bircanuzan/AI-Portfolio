# Responsible AI & AI Governance — a career-entry plan grounded in work
**HumanEvoX | 2 November 2026 | Proposed plan, not completed work**

## The practical transition thesis
Move from HR transformation / people governance into **AI Governance & Assurance Analyst**, **Responsible AI Implementation Analyst** or **AI risk / policy / evaluation support**, initially focusing on HR use cases. Do not assume that a Lead Implementer / Lead Auditor course is a substitute for experience. Avoid representing original portfolio designs as commercial deployments or self-audits as independent ISO certification audits.

## Starting evidence already available
- Genuine **open-weight Qwen** local inference on a synthetic HR policy assistant; preserved prompts, actual generated drafts and host-side source-backed outputs in [the companion agent project](../../../AI%20Model%20Training%20%26%20Evaluation%20Freelancer%20Project/Agentic%20AI%20Application%20Testing%20%26%20Evaluation%20for%20HR%20Transformation/evidence/first_run_qwen_0_5b.json).
- **[A real-evidence governance analysis and OPEN corrective-action register](REAL_LLM_ASSURANCE_CASE_STUDY.md)**: risks, potentially affected stakeholders, test limitations, policy/approval safeguards and release hold.
- Separately, a **deterministic authorization-gate demo** with 12 synthetic scenarios and 9 unit tests, described in [the governance project README](../README.md). It did not itself call an LLM.
- HR consulting, change, policy design, stakeholder coordination and learning delivery are transferable domain experience; they are **not** 2 years of formal AI governance practice.

## Four-week study-to-work conversion, with proof gates
| Week | Learn (free first) | Do with current existing assets | Evidence that counts | Completion criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | ISO/IEC 42001 overview; NIST Govern/Map; UAE ethics guidance; source/decision boundaries | Create real versioned system inventory, owner/decision-flow, approved intended use and out-of-scope uses for the demo | Scoped AIMS statement and role diagram | Reviewer can explain how system works and where human responsibility starts |
| 2 | AI risk & impact assessment, privacy/data governance, controls | Verify 6 case-level raw traces; complete independent risk register, impact assessment, test acceptance plan, and source/citation check | Signed-off-by-author draft risk and control register clearly marked **portfolio** | Every material risk maps to a mitigation, an owner role and evidence |
| 3 | Evaluation, audit evidence, LLM failure analysis, CAPA | Write 20+ new held-out prompts before running model; execute real model evaluation and inspect raw outputs; preserve failures, versions and timestamps | Actual run logs with genuine model calls; findings and retest evidence | Tests are reproducible and pass criteria distinguish routing from answer accuracy; if unrun, status stays NOT DONE |
| 4 | Corrective action, audit sampling, management review, consulting scoping | Remediate at least one observed defect and truly rerun; conduct a **simulated** internal audit, issue reviewed finding, prepare 2-page pilot scope | Before/after test artifacts, independent peer feedback if available, proposal and constraints | A third party can reproduce the result; no unverified certification or client-success claims |

## A first real HumanEvoX paid pilot (the missing bridge)
**Offer:** 'Responsible AI Readiness & Assurance Review for one HR AI workflow'. Possible customers: a recruitment boutique using an ATS AI feature, an HR consulting firm deploying a policy chatbot, or a learning provider using GenAI-generated content. Price and terms are negotiated; do not promise compliance certification.

**Scope (bounded):** 1 AI use case, approved sample data or synthetic-only testing; intake and inventory; process/data flow; risk and AI-impact worksheet; evaluation and privacy/human-oversight tests; prioritized control register; one decision meeting. Independent technical penetration testing and legal advice are **out of scope** unless separately qualified.

**Outputs to a real customer:** 2-page system/use-case inventory, risk register, ten or more authorized tests with preserved actual outputs, approval/escalation matrix, signed actions, a decision-ready remediation report. No real employee/candidate details in the public repo.

**Proof of real delivery:** engagement letter/SOW, client-signed permission and scope, dated personal timesheet, original anonymized work products with permission, reviewer approvals and documented customer sign-off. Paid work for one's own consultancy can be relevant experience, but acceptance as certification project hours is determined by the credential issuer. A demo is **not** an actual customer engagement.

**Commercial outreach target:** partner with governance consultants, data-protection consultancies and HRTech implementers as a delivery subcontractor on limited testing/evidence tasks; target billable work rather than months of unpaid internships.

## Which vacancies to seek and which to avoid
| Apply first | Apply selectively | Deprioritize without required experience |
|---|---|---|
| Responsible AI Analyst / AI Governance Analyst (documentation and risk focus) | AI governance associate or AI adoption governance coordinator | Head/Director of AI Governance |
| AI Assurance / AI Evaluation Analyst, non-engineering testing and evidence | Junior GRC / privacy consultant with AI workstream | External Lead Auditor requiring audit days and assurance experience |
| AI implementation / change / controls delivery for HRTech | Compliance analyst where AI/privacy is in the scope | AI security engineering roles requiring deep cyberengineering |
| HR transformation and client delivery roles that own AI governance adoption | Vendor risk / procurement governance with AI controls | Model validation scientists requiring substantial statistics and ML expertise |

**Filter before applying:** UAE location/authorization; compensation and employment type; whether real junior-level skills are accepted; whether professional experience can be transferable; no unearned certification requirement; English-language eligibility; no mandatory Arabic; no new-logo quota or cold acquisition.

## Apply with accurate wording
- **Possible CV headline now:** 'HR Transformation & AI Governance Portfolio Practitioner | Responsible AI Risk and Evaluation | HumanEvoX'.
- **Evidence-based project claim:** 'Evaluated preserved outputs from a locally executed open-weight HR-policy LLM experiment (3 real completions, 6 synthetic routing scenarios); documented 3 material answer-grounding/completeness concerns and a corrective-action plan. No client deployment or formal certification claimed.'
- **Not allowed:** 'ISO/IEC 42001 Certified Lead Implementer', 'Led enterprise AI governance audits', 'Deployed Responsible AI controls across real customers' or imaginary project hours.
- Explain the career transition candidly: long HR/enterprise-consulting experience, applied AI governance lab, interest in practical controls and assurance implementation; willing to own risk documentation, workshops, test protocols, evidence and stakeholder decisions.

## Job-market signal test (do not buy a course blindly)
For each genuinely open vacancy record: company, source URL, location, advertised date, core experience requirements, specific ISO/IEC 42001 status (**mandatory / preferred / merely referenced / absent**), whether applied, screening outcome, and recruiter response. After 15–20 *real, relevant* applications and direct conversations, review whether Lead Implementer would clear a specific eligibility hurdle. One or two employer statements are more probative than generic certification marketing.
