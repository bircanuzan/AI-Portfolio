# PECB credential options and honest experience accounting
**Read this before paying for a course.** [Source: PECB official ISO/IEC 42001 Lead Implementer route](https://pecb.com/en/education-and-certification-for-individuals/iso-iec-42001/iso-iec-42001-lead-implementer). Current requirements should be confirmed with PECB at application time.

| Credential obtainable after passing the required exam (and other conditions) | General professional experience | AI experience | AI-management-system project hours |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| **PECB Certified ISO/IEC 42001 Provisional Implementer** | None | None | None |
| PECB Certified ISO/IEC 42001 Implementer | 2 years | At least 1 year | At least 200 |
| PECB Certified ISO/IEC 42001 **Lead Implementer** | 5 years | At least 2 years | At least 300 |

**Plain-language explanation:** "Provisional" is an **individual** PECB credential you can apply for after passing the qualifying exam even without AI work history. It is **not** an automatically issued certification after viewing this GitHub project, and **does not certify HumanEvoX**. Lead Implementer has substantial separate experience and project-hour requirements; an exam pass alone does not grant that title. Compare official training/exam fees with career value before purchasing.

## Legitimate work-hours practice
The personal log starts **empty**. Never backdate employment, double-count existing HR experience as AI-management-system experience, turn a list of GitHub files into work-hours, or claim a client project not actually performed. No hours are credited to the user by this repository creation or the assistant's local tests.

For each genuinely performed activity, record:
1. Actual start/end date, time spent and activity you personally performed.
2. The named organization, project, scope, and accountable reviewer/supervisor who can verify it.
3. Link to a non-confidential deliverable or internal record and the specific AIMS role.
4. Whether the entry is a **personal learning/simulation**, **authorized internal company project**, or **client assignment**.
5. Evidence of formal approval, project assignment and feedback where available.

Use [the blank CSV](../evidence/real_project_hours_log.csv). Only PECB can decide whether a particular activity and reference meet its scheme requirements. An independent self-study exercise does not automatically satisfy organizational project experience.

## Suggested activities for genuine internal work
Document actual AI asset inventory, risk assessments, AI policy review meetings, controller/processor and supplier evaluations, guardrail requirements, real system testing with captured responses, treatment decisions, management reviews and corrective actions. Seek legitimate management sponsorship before labeling an activity a HumanEvoX operational AIMS implementation.
