# 01 — Dubai / UAE: Ethics, Governance and Applicable Laws

The following four entries preserve the **UAE-first** order and core meaning of the supplied screenshots. For operational use, their legal status must be distinguished.

## 1. Digital Dubai AI Ethics

**Screenshot wording:** Dubai toolkit for responsible AI, focused on fairness, accountability, transparency and explainability.

**What it means:** Fairness requires scrutiny of unjustified unequal results; accountability means assigning owners; transparency means making the AI role understandable; explainability means supporting interpretation of significant outputs.

**Portfolio implementation:** Document intended use, responsible business owner, affected people, fairness-test plan, explanation standard and incident owner.

**Status:** Digital Dubai's ethics principles/guidelines are guidance rather than a standalone binding AI law.

Official: https://www.digitaldubai.ae/knowledge-hub/publications/ai-ethics-principles-guidelines

## 2. AI Ethics Self-Assessment Tool

**Screenshot wording:** Helps AI developers/operators assess ethical performance, gaps and mitigation measures for each AI system.

**What it means:** An operator can assess the use case and decision criticality, then examine fairness, accountability, transparency and explainability; identify gaps; and record proposed mitigating actions.

**Portfolio implementation:** Create one self-assessment per material AI system; store owner, applicable questions, reason for exclusions, risk gaps and closure evidence.

**Status:** Digital Dubai labels its tool beta and its guidelines *recommended instead of compulsory*. A positive self-rating is not a formal certification.

Official: https://www.digitaldubai.ae/self-assessment

## 3. UAE AI Charter

**Screenshot wording:** National principles for ethical AI, privacy and data security, human oversight, accountability and compliance.

**What it means:** The UAE Charter for the Development & Use of Artificial Intelligence describes human-centered, transparent, safe and accountable AI. Organizations should manage algorithmic bias, privacy and inclusion, preserve suitable human oversight and obey laws applicable to the activity.

**Portfolio implementation:** Document the human reviewer, automated-decision boundaries, right to challenge, protection of data, bias monitoring, and compliance ownership.

**Status:** A national policy charter of principles; compliance analysis must still identify the specific laws and sector obligations.

Official published charter: https://ai.gov.ae/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/UAEAI-Methaq-EN2-3.pdf

## 4. Dubai Universal Blueprint / AI Seal

**Screenshot wording:** Dubai roadmap and trust mechanism for AI adoption, AI leadership and verified AI suppliers.

**Important:** This refers to **two related but different initiatives**.

- **Dubai Universal Blueprint for Artificial Intelligence:** A Dubai strategy announced in 2024 to accelerate AI adoption and ecosystem development. It does not by itself certify an organization's AI product.
- **Dubai AI Seal:** An initiative connected to Dubai's AI ecosystem that offers supplier verification/classification. Supplier verification is not a guarantee of the suitability, privacy or safety of every proposed deployment.

**Portfolio implementation:** When buying agentic or generative AI services, record supplier verification (where relevant), security due diligence, privacy terms and acceptance-testing evidence.

Official Blueprint: https://www.protocol.dubai.ae/en/media-listing/news-events/hamdan-bin-mohammed-launches-dubai-universal-blueprint-for-artificial-intelligence/  
AI Seal: https://dub.ai/en/ai-seal/

## Additional actual legal consideration: UAE Personal Data Protection Law

The **Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data** should be considered where personal-data processing is in scope. An enterprise needs legal review of processing grounds, purpose, retention, security, transfers and any exceptions or sector/free-zone-specific regimes.

Official UAE Government overview: https://u.ae/en/about-the-uae/digital-uae/data/data-protection-laws
