# 30 / 60 / 90 days — transition with human decision rights

| Timing | Workstream | Deliverables | Owner | Exit gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1–30 | Discover and safeguard | Stakeholder map, activity/quality baseline, case mix, consent/data assessment, existing role RACI, risk log | Transformation lead + HR/operations | Leaders sign off on data, scope and restrictions |
| Days 1–30 | Design | Workforce scenario, proposed human–AI task map, options paper, employee consultation plan | Workforce consultant | No unvalidated employment decisions |
| Days 31–60 | Pilot | Controlled group, quality rubric, exception tracker, structured review, manager coaching, learning support | Operations + IT/security + HR | Service quality and privacy gates pass |
| Days 31–60 | Talent | Draft role profiles, skills frameworks, evidence-based assessment approach, redeployment pathways | HR + managers | Human review and employment-law check |
| Days 61–90 | Evaluate | Compare quality, backlog, review burden, costs, accessibility, employee feedback and actual usage | Transformation PMO | Finance/HR/operations decision board |
| Days 61–90 | Scale or stop | Confirm process redesign, learning budget, demand-based hiring requirements, ownership and rollback plan | Leadership sponsor | Written go/no-go approval |

## Artifacts a client would sign
- Decision register, RACI, data owner/retention register and exception approvals.
- Change impact and workforce risk assessment; employee consultation and communication pack.
- Pilot evaluation with quantitative and qualitative evidence, issue and remediation log.
- Human-approved future role architecture, structured hiring specification, learning pathway and revised workforce plan.

**No automated selection, compensation or redundancy decisions.**
