## Employer role-to-portfolio fit: practical priorities
This matrix weights **known career adjacency**, not probability of receiving an offer. In the example profile, experience in stakeholder delivery, HR services, staffing/training solutions and program coordination is treated separately from **portfolio-only** AI/engineering demonstrations.

1. **HR Technology client delivery / transformation coordination:** most directly transferable; verify no hands-on developer/configuration requirement.
2. **AI adoption program coordination / AI-in-HR process improvement:** transferable if the role values process redesign and governance rather than workshop delivery.
3. **Talent/workforce intelligence:** transferable staffing domain; check analyst/assessment qualification requirements.
4. **AI governance PMO:** possible adjacent route; stronger professional control/testing evidence needed for auditor or lead roles.
5. **People Analytics:** apply where reporting and KPI coordination suffice; specialist analyst roles may demand SQL/BI years.

**Mandatory vacancy filters:** UAE-based (remote/hybrid/onsite), English-language viable, no new-logo sales quota, no training-first job; senior specialist/engineering roles only when evidence meets requirements.

## Cross-cutting competency examples
- Business case and senior stakeholder interviews: **discovery, action register, governance decisions**.
- HR process design: **AS-IS/TO-BE, service categories, exception escalation**.
- Evidence quality: **source/version logs, metric definitions, reproducibility**.
- Responsible AI: **privacy by design, non-discrimination, human review, contestability**.
- Deployment adoption: **rollout checkpoints, ownership, measurable service KPIs**.

For real jobs, read the entire official JD and use [the matcher](02_Role_Matcher_Methodology.md) as a self-assessment, not an automated application decision.
