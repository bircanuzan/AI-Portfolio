# Skills framework and human-led transition design

**Target proficiency scale:** 1 = awareness, 2 = guided, 3 = independent, 4 = coach, 5 = design/lead. No fabricated incumbent ratings are collected.

| Capability | Employee Service Partner | Customer Operations Partner | Recruitment Operations Analyst | Workforce Transformation Consultant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Process and task mapping | 3 | 3 | 3 | 5 |
| Human review and AI output evaluation | 3 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| Data analysis/capacity measurement | 2 | 2 | 2 | 5 |
| Human judgment and exception handling | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| Privacy, confidentiality, and escalation | 4 | 4 | 5 | 5 |
| Change facilitation | 2 | 2 | 2 | 5 |

## Four-step evidence-based methodology
1. Map real tasks; validate with incumbents, managers, process owners and HR.
2. Establish capability baseline with **opt-in human-led** observations, work samples and structured interviews; avoid individual profiling by unreviewed AI.
3. Define learning/development and redeployment pathways with transparent criteria, accommodations, consent where required and fair opportunity.
4. Reassess 30/60/90-day outcomes with qualitative feedback, employee experience, quality and workload data; do not equate tool usage with competence.

## Example pathways
- Recruiter coordinator → AI-assisted recruitment operations specialist: scheduling controls, ATS workflow literacy, candidate privacy, fairness and human oversight.
- Employee service analyst → knowledge-quality and escalation specialist: policy-source validation, access restrictions, exception resolution and safe review.
- Workforce analyst → human/AI workforce architect: task analysis, capacity modeling, decision rights and executive scenario facilitation.

**Decision governance:** HR and management must approve job changes, redeployment, selection, training allocations and employment outcomes; consult relevant UAE legal specialists before HR-sensitive deployment. This lab provides no automated employment decisions.
