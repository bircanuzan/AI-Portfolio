# Task-level workforce economics and sourcing decisions

## Design principle
Do not start with job reductions. Start with purpose, desired value, process friction and task decomposition. Eliminate unnecessary handoffs *before* deciding whether to assist work with AI. Every initiative should preserve service quality, privacy, judgment and early-career learning.

## Evidence inputs for real future study
| Field | Method of measurement | Owner / verification |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly task volume | Documented service transactions including exceptions | Operations |
| Task time (minutes) | Representative process observation | Operations / HR |
| Loaded human hourly cost | Actual approved accounting assumptions | Finance |
| Assistable share | Process decomposition + risk/technical feasibility | Process + cyber |
| Minutes saved | Controlled pilot measurement at equal quality | Operations |
| Human review minutes | Review, rework, approvals and escalations | Service owner |
| Platform cost | Licenses, inference, orchestration, support, evaluation, security | CAIO/Finance |
| Change and learning | Training, rollout, reskilling and employee support | CHRO/Finance |
| Realized benefit | Cost avoidance, quality, growth capacity or verified cash savings | CFO |

## BOT / BUILD / BORROW / BUY options (publicly attributed to reAImagination's discussed 4B frame)
- **BOT:** supervised AI or digital worker for low-consequence, permitted and tested tasks.
- **BUILD:** reskill existing talent and redesign roles to retain human judgment and career mobility.
- **BORROW:** time-limited contractor or specialist capacity under contract, privacy and control.
- **BUY:** source capability externally if appropriate on cost/quality/risk; account for vendor lock-in.

This section is an original option-assessment example, **not reAImagination platform access or their proprietary scoring formula**. The final option must be selected by accountable humans using evidence, timeframe, talent/nationalization commitments, employee impact and financial constraints.

## Independent illustrative arithmetic
Baseline hours/month = monthly volume × minutes per task / 60.

Released hours/month = volume × assistable share × (minutes saved per assisted task − additional human review minutes) / 60.

Modeled human-time equivalent (AED/month) = released hours × Finance-validated loaded hourly cost.

Modeled net economic **opportunity**, not cash value = human-time equivalent − platform monthly cost.

Verified cash savings = **0**, unless budget and ledger changes are *separately established and approved*. Releasing capacity is not evidence of a salary expense reduction.

## Risks and disqualifiers
If human review burden exceeds minutes saved, net released hours can be negative. Confidential HR, legal, remuneration, performance, selection and financial decisions cannot be automated solely from model outputs. A pilot must compare quality-adjusted case outcomes, not merely token count.

## Source caveat
No task numbers, costs or savings here come from actual UAE businesses or the leaders/companies cited. The data is synthetic and intended to show method and calculation design.
