Evidence-first recruitment | proposed governance audit

Framework only. These are suggested audit controls—not results from an ATS, candidate assessment, psychometric instrument or recruitment client. No candidates were evaluated.

Suggested hiring decision evidence chain

01 / Criteria

Translate job duties into essential, measurable criteria before screening.

02 / Evidence

Capture demonstrable skills, structured examples and validated assessments, not writing polish.

03 / AI support

Use AI to organize evidence and flag missing information; do not infer protected traits.

04 / Human decision

Require recruiter/hiring-manager adjudication for consequential decisions and exceptions.

05 / Review

Track rationale, consistency, candidate recourse, data minimization and periodic validity checks.

Minimum validation design before a real pilot

Inputs: approved job criteria, consented or anonymized candidate records, structured assessment evidence, process timestamps and human decisions.

Methods: independent labeling; eligibility and evidence trace tests; consistency checks; human override review; appropriate fairness assessment with legal/privacy oversight.

Metrics to collect: traceable-evidence coverage, decision disagreements, corrected AI recommendations, reviewer time, candidate escalation rate and suitable downstream hiring-quality proxies.

Current result: original audit blueprint prepared; no empirical hiring improvements, predictive validity or bias reduction has been measured.
