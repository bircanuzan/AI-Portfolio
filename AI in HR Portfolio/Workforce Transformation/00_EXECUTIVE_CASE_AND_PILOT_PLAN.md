# Enterprise board brief and controlled 90-day human–AI pilot plan

**Fictional Atlas UAE Shared Services, independent demonstration. No actual enterprise pilot has taken place.**

## Executive question
What work should people do, what activities might an approved AI system assist, which human decisions stay non-delegable, what must change in teams/skills, what should engineering build, and how will actual value and risk be measured?

## Who owns each decision
| Executive | Responsibility | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|---|
| CEO | Strategic objective and business value | Value-stream outcome map |
| CHRO | Work/roles/skills/employee safeguards and organization design | Job architecture, impact assessment, consultation |
| CFO | Capacity vs cost, verified financial outcomes | Baselines, cost model, ledger reconciliation |
| CAIO/CTO | Product strategy, data architecture, agent lifecycle and infrastructure | API/identity controls, tests, telemetry |
| CISO/Legal | Data rights, applicable law, security and independent assurance | Control register, legal/privacy assessment |
| Functional service owner | Service quality, operational review and actual outcome ownership | Task observation, QA, exception data |

## 90-day proposed schedule (NOT completed work)
- **Days 01–15 — Discover:** sponsor outcome, current task/skill metrics, service cases, workforce data and control owners.
- **Days 16–30 — Design:** future task/role/organization options; human-agent rights; skills transition and workforce scenarios.
- **Days 31–45 — Specify:** agent permissions and exception paths, product stories, data contracts, privacy assessment, cost assumptions.
- **Days 46–60 — Build/test:** deterministic proof-of-concept, synthetic negative cases, actual independent acceptance plan for authorized future pilot.
- **Days 61–75 — Decide:** risk/legal signoffs, skills, communication and funding; formal *hold/go* decision before real integration.
- **Days 76–90 — Evaluate:** if authorized, gather representative real measures, compare baseline, remediate or stop; otherwise record evidence gaps.

## Executable artifacts available now

## Executive pilot scorecard to collect in future
Real service quality and first-time resolution; employee satisfaction and appeal; incident severity; approved source accuracy; human escalations; human review time; cost per verified result; skill development; utilization reallocation; Finance-confirmed savings **only where independently evidenced**.

## Safety and commercial integrity
The independent Python proof samples do not deploy any LLM/agent, connect to an HRIS or make employment decisions. All scripts operate on fictional data and require explicit human signoff for any real implementation. A hypothetical efficiency calculation cannot authorize redundancy or establish cash ROI.
