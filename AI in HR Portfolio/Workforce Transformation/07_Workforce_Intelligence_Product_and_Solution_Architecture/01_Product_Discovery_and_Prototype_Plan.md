# Product discovery and controlled prototype roadmap

## Customer and buyer hypotheses
- **CEO/CFO:** Where is business value created, which decisions change and what economic evidence exists?
- **CHRO:** What work, jobs, skills and talent should change, with lawful and fair people outcomes?
- **CAIO/CTO:** What systems, agents, identities and APIs support safe integration and observability?
- **HR/operations analyst:** Which task/skills/data assumptions are reliable, and can I challenge them?
- **Employee/manager:** Which recommendations are transparent, contestable, accessible and useful?

## Four product capabilities (separate from implementation delivery)
1. **Work Graph:** Business outcome -> value stream -> task -> human/agent allocation -> accountabilities and risk.
2. **Workforce Intelligence:** Role and skill taxonomy, demand/supply scenarios and talent mobility choices.
3. **Digital Worker Register:** Agent status/permissions, risk, owners, evaluations and operational telemetry.
4. **Value and Decision Room:** Investment hypotheses, baseline, exceptions, decisions and realized outcomes.

## Problem-to-requirement map
| ID | User story | Acceptance criterion | Safety condition |
|---|---|---|---|
| US-01 | As CHRO, trace a strategic objective to tasks and role change | Traceable linked work objects; missing owner flagged | No automatic employment action |
| US-02 | As CFO, compare investment hypotheses | Baseline, cost components, assumptions clearly labeled | Released hours not presented as cash saved |
| US-03 | As CAIO, inspect digital worker status | Displays owner, permission, review, version and incident | No hidden escalation of access |
| US-04 | As analyst, test scenarios | Named scenario, effective dates and confidence | Sources verifiable and no unconsented PII |
| US-05 | As manager, challenge a recommendation | Human review, override reason and audit trail | No model-only final approval |
| US-06 | As product team, prioritize use cases | Evidence-based intake + safety gate + backlog card | High-risk proposals held for review |
| US-07 | As people analytics lead, align role/skill data | Source-of-truth, role taxonomy, quality checks | No secret employee profiling |
| US-08 | As buyer, assess AI product impact | Pilot outcomes versus baseline, exceptions and cost | No unvalidated ROI claims |

## Discovery workshop
1. Clarify sponsor outcome and user archetypes.
2. Map recurring work decisions and data ownership.
3. Observe present workflows and exceptions.
4. Produce 3 alternative end-user concepts; validate accessibility and trust.
5. Prioritize using value, data readiness, effort and risk.
6. Whiteboard interfaces and failure paths with engineering and cybersecurity.
7. Agree a testable MVP hypothesis, owner, dataset, control gates and launch criteria.

## Product roadmap (proposal)
- **0–30 days:** interview users; map data; publish story map, nonfunctional requirements, privacy design, trust hypotheses.
- **31–60:** simulated MVP / integration spike, data contracts, false-confidence and escalation tests, prototype usability.
- **61–90:** limited authorized sandbox pilot *if* platform licensing and security permit; validate real users, no hidden decisions; update product backlog based on evidence.

## Runnable slice
The code reads fictional use cases, validates prerequisites, **holds** privacy-sensitive proposals, calculates a transparent discussion-only priority for non-sensitive opportunities and generates a first backlog card. It deliberately does not claim to recommend automated HR or finance decisions.
