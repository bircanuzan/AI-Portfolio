# 01 | HR Shared Services Operating Model

**Project:** Independent portfolio example; not a client deployment.

## Service catalogue and accountable owners
| Service | Accountable owner | Delivery partners | System of record | Control |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requisition approval | Recruitment lead | Operations / Finance | ATS | Approved headcount and budget |
| Offer & pre-boarding | TA supervisor | HR Ops / Hiring Manager | ATS + HCM | Offer approval, joining checklist |
| Immigration handoff | HR Shared Services lead | Authorised PRO, Legal, authorities | Restricted case register | Work-authorisation gate before joining |
| ID and permit renewals | PRO case owner | Employee, HR Ops | Restricted expiry tracker | Risk-based renewal reminders |
| Employee record changes | HR Ops lead | Payroll, employee, manager | HCM | Maker-checker approval and audit trail |
| Transfer and exit | HR Ops lead | Operations, Payroll, PRO | HCM + case register | Separation checklist and accountabilities |
| Employee questions | Case management supervisor | HRBP, PRO, Payroll | Case management queue | Categorise, assign, resolve, verify |

## End-to-end employee joining process
1. Operations submits approved requisition with job location, entity, work arrangement, planned start date and cost centre.
2. Recruitment completes screening, approvals and documented offer.
3. HR Ops creates a controlled pre-boarding case and a minimum-document checklist; access restricted by role.
4. Authorised PRO/legal owner determines the correct work-authorisation process and confirms case-specific government requirements.
5. Payroll/Finance validate entity, cost centre and pay setup (with no public storage of private payroll data).
6. HR Ops checks all required pre-employment gates and confirms approved joining date.
7. Manager completes first-day access/equipment/induction checklist and records completion.
8. Employee has a named service contact; unresolved problems receive SLA-based escalation.
9. Case is closed only when HR Ops, manager and required specialists sign off.

## Team cadence
**Daily:** critical joins; cases at risk; permit expiries; unresolved employee issues; hiring blockers.  
**Weekly:** recruitment funnel, ageing, supplier/progress performance, service defect review.  
**Monthly:** SLA dashboard, recurring exception trends, headcount reconciliation, compliance-control sample and action log.

## Quality and privacy
- Least-privilege access to employee records and identity documentation.
- No identity document attachments in public reports, emails or repositories.
- Separate case closure from case approval; retain evidence according to employer policy.
- Log changes with requester, approver, date, system and reason.
- Check applicable legal and regulatory requirements with UAE HR Legal and authorised PRO representatives.
