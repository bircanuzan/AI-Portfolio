## 1. Purpose
We aim to assess working-pattern requests consistently, transparently and in line with local legal duties, employee contracts, collective arrangements, safety obligations and operational requirements.

## 2. Eligibility and scope
Employees may request eligible arrangements through the documented process. Access, response time, statutory rights, notice, grounds for refusal and appeals **vary by jurisdiction and employment category**. The local addendum must specify the governing requirements. Flight operations, safety-critical, airport-based and 24/7 customer-facing work may require distinct feasibility and fatigue/safety evaluation. **No blanket exclusion is established by this sample.**

## 3. Request and review steps
1. Employee submits a request through the approved HR channel without disclosing unnecessary sensitive information.
2. HR confirms location, contractual terms, any applicable protected request category and the correct country addendum.
3. Manager assesses coverage, fatigue/safety, service continuity, performance measures, equipment, security, scheduling and fairness against **documented** criteria.
4. HRBP/ER consults Country Legal when required; Payroll/Reward reviews any working-hours, allowance or tax implications; Data Privacy/InfoSec reviews remote-access and data transfer risks.
5. Authorized decision maker issues an accessible written decision within the **country-specific lawful deadline**, including reasons, any trial period and review/appeal route.
6. Any variation is formally documented, acknowledged, effective-dated and configured in applicable systems only after required approvals.

## 4. Fairness and protection
No retaliation for raising a lawful request. Decisions must not rely on stereotypes or irrelevant personal characteristics. Accommodation and statutory requests require separate applicable legal and HR safeguards.

## 5. Working conditions
Pay, benefits, expenses, working time, cross-border work, insurance and tax/social-security implications must be determined from locally reviewed terms. Do not assume one arrangement automatically changes contractual conditions.

## 6. Operational controls
Maintain safe rostering, confidentiality, information security, duty-of-care and business continuity. Before cross-border working approval, obtain relevant immigration, tax, employment, privacy and security assessments.

## 7. Appeals, exceptions and recordkeeping
Provide a locally reviewed appeal route, evidence trail and retention schedule. Exceptions cannot waive statutory rights. HR Policy reviews aggregate outcomes by market and role family, tracking access, decisions, time to response, appeals, employee feedback and adverse-impact risk.
