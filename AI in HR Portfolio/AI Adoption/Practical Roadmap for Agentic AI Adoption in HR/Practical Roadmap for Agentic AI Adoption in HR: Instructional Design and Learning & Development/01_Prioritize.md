# Step 1: PRIORITIZE — Select a high-value, manageable-risk HR agent

## Problem to solve

L&D designers repeatedly translate skill gaps into measurable objectives, storyboards, activities and assessment specifications; source licensing and accessibility create rework.

## Why this agent is the best *provisional* candidate

The agent can draft bounded design artifacts by mapping approved competencies to learning objectives and activities. Instructional designers remain responsible for content accuracy, pedagogy, copyright and publication.

The specific business outcome is **effort per approved and assessment-aligned microlearning module**. Confirm demand with 4–6 weeks of appropriate process/ticket/request metadata, interviews with Head of Learning & Development, and a valid baseline, without collecting or exporting unnecessary personal information. Document exceptions and current rework, not just the easy cases.

## Alternatives actually compared

A transparent, **illustrative analyst screen** uses six dimensions weighted: repeat demand/value (25%), bounded agent suitability (20%), source/data readiness (15%), integration feasibility (15%), manageable risk (15%), and measurability (10%). A 5/5 risk rating means *more manageable* risk, not more harm.

| Option | Ratings by six criteria (1–5) | Weighted /100 | Interpretation |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Selected: Learning Needs-to-Curriculum Design Agent | 5, 5, 4, 4, 4, 5 | 91.0 | Hypothesized high business value and controllable autonomy |
| Alternative: Fully automated LMS course publishing | 4, 4, 3, 3, 2, 4 | 68.0 | Different risk/control profile; not first deployment choice |
| Alternative: Unrestricted text-to-course writer | 3, 3, 3, 2, 1, 3 | 51.0 | More exposure or lower bounded value |

**These scores are not real evidence.** Re-score using source quality, actual permissions, actual workforce needs, vendor licensing, costs and privacy/security findings. Perform an independent comparison with ordinary search, reporting or rules-based workflow before committing to an agent.

## Value hypothesis — falsifiable

- **Primary unit:** effort per approved and assessment-aligned microlearning module.
- **Required baseline:** manual process handling time, completion quality, delay, rework and correction minutes across comparable cases.
- **Proposed benefit:** avoid repetitive coordination while improving evidence accuracy and reducing manual rework, subject to human approval.
- **Measured net value:** (baseline human minutes − agent-assisted human minutes including review, corrections, incidents and support) / 60 × approved loaded hourly cost; subtract license, usage, deployment, security and maintenance spend.
- **Important:** freed work capacity is not booked cash savings or job reduction; do not report it as financial realization without separate finance verification.

## Selection gate

Proceed only if (1) adequate recurring demand exists; (2) no simpler controlled process delivers equal value at lower lifecycle cost; (3) approved source/data and limited system permissions are feasible; (4) Head of Learning & Development accepts human accountability; (5) privacy/legal and security approve a restricted test; and (6) effort per approved and assessment-aligned microlearning module can be measured. Otherwise hold or select a simpler solution.
