## Method limitations

- Risk scores, baseline ticket volumes, KPI thresholds, AED rates, readiness scores and pilot sample sizes are **illustrative project decisions**, not surveyed employer results or regulatory thresholds.
- Controls map to published *principles* and *summaries*, not reproduction of licensed full ISO standards.
- "Zero observed incidents" over a small test set does not prove zero future risk. All tests and their evaluation must be run against the actual intended stack and data permissions before approval.
- Any organization-specific obligations, current framework editions or product features should be reverified before real rollout.
