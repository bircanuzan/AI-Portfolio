# 12.01 — Protect Confidential Talent Assessment and Skills Data

**Portfolio status:** original strategy/design case with a worked artifact; synthetic clean-energy scenario; not a deployed clean_energy_group system.

A shared talent review export exposes confidential successor ratings. The proposed executive intervention is: **Restrict roles enforce audit and retention with privacy response procedure**.

RBAC; MFA; encryption; UAE law; GDPR where applicable. Technology references are proposed integration/assessment options only; current clean_energy_group systems and software licenses are unverified.

## Outcomes to demonstrate
**Primary KPI:** Unauthorized access exceptions and remediation time. An authentic claim requires a signed baseline, real measured outcome, confounder assessment and owner approval.

## Key risk and guardrail
