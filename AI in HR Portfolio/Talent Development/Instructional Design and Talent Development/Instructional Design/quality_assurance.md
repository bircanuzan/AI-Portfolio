# Quality, Accessibility and Academic Integrity Gates

**Evidence status:** Reusable proposed controls, not a record of institutional approval.

| Gate | Review question | Owner | Required evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 Needs and learner analysis | Is a real performance gap documented rather than assuming training? | Instructional designer | Needs brief and learner personas |
| G2 Outcome clarity | Are outcomes observable, measurable and mapped to Bloom? | Faculty/SME | Signed ILO map |
| G3 Constructive alignment | Do learning activities and assessment directly test the outcome? | Instructional designer | Alignment CSV and design review |
| G4 Assessment validity | Does rubric describe observable performance and critical failures? | Assessment lead | Rubric and moderation samples |
| G5 Academic quality | Are references accurate, versioned and owned by competent SME? | Faculty/SME | Source register and sign-off |
| G6 Accessibility | Keyboard sequence, captions, transcripts, alt text, contrast, reading level? | Accessibility reviewer | WCAG 2.2 AA-oriented checklist and findings |
| G7 Data and privacy | Are learner data de-identified, minimally retained and access controlled? | Data owner | Retention/access note |
| G8 LMS readiness | Are navigation, prerequisites, mobile responsiveness, quizzes tested? | LMS administrator | UAT log with screenshots from *future* real build |
| G9 Pilot analysis | Does post-pilot performance support the claimed outcome? | Learning analyst | Participation and attainment report |
| G10 Release | Has institution approved publication and accreditation mapping? | Authorized owner | Release approval and change log |

## Analytic rubric: final employee FAQ artifact (1–4)
- **Accuracy (25%):** 1 unsupported/incorrect, 2 several issues, 3 factually correct with minor gaps, 4 verified and clear.
- **Privacy (25%, critical):** 1 exposes personal data, 2 unnecessary data, 3 de-identified with proper control, 4 minimised and reviewed. **Must earn 3 or 4.**
- **Critical evaluation (25%):** 1 no checks, 2 superficial checks, 3 systematic checks, 4 evidenced red-team / correction log.
- **Usability and accessibility (15%):** 1 inaccessible, 2 major barriers, 3 usable/accessible, 4 tested and refined.
- **Traceability (10%):** 1 no review, 2 partial, 3 named human sign-off and version, 4 full reproducible audit trail.

A minimum of 3/4 on every critical criterion is required, independent of overall weighted average.
