# 01 | Business objectives, needs assessment and approval brief

## Fictional operating context
NovaLink is a fabricated B2B workplace-technology vendor launching the invented ConnectHub meeting-room endpoint with an optional ConnectCare managed support plan. Illustrative programme reach: **60 account executives + 30 channel sellers**, located in UAE/GCC (24), Europe (30), APAC (20), North America (16). These are **planning assumptions**, not actual employees or customers.

## Problem statement
The future-state sales team must sell outcomes, not recite specifications. In an **illustrative** baseline, product knowledge is inconsistent, security questions are handled without evidence, and partners use different demo narratives. No real baseline assessment has taken place.

| Stakeholder | Job-to-be-done | Interview question | Evidence to request |
|---|---|---|---|
| Global sales VP (sponsor) | Faster, higher-quality opportunity qualification | What buyer problems must sellers diagnose? | Strategic account priorities; target use cases |
| Regional sales leads | Relevance to local sectors | What varies by customer and region? | Objection examples; local segment profile |
| Product manager | Correct scope and claims | Which features and limits are approved? | Versioned product sheet; release notes |
| Solutions engineer | Reliable demos and escalation | Which steps fail most often? | Approved demo path; troubleshooting SOP |
| Security / legal | Defensible wording and data boundaries | Which statements require qualification? | Approved claims register; restricted claims |
| L&D lead | Consistent learning outcomes | What must sellers **do** after training? | Skill matrix; LMS constraints; learner profile |

## Baseline instruments (not administered)
**5-minute seller survey:** (a) Can I explain 3 customer problems? (b) Can I demonstrate a meeting-room handoff? (c) Do I know where security claims must be checked? (d) Can I distinguish license/service limitations? (e) Which one objection blocks me? Use confidence 1–5 and a free-text example; confidence **is not** a substitute for demonstrated ability.

**Observed diagnostic task:** 7-minute call simulation: discover requirements, connect product benefits, avoid unsupported cybersecurity promises, request next-step commitment. Two independent reviewers score behaviours from a predefined rubric.

**Documentation review:** Examine only sanitized approved content; reconcile contradictions in an issue log. Never train on confidential customer diagrams without permission.

## Hypothetical objectives, not outcomes
1. By end of onboarding, at least **80%** of assessed learners pass the performance rubric **and** all critical safety gates.
2. At least **85%** reach course completion within 21 days of assigned enrolment.
3. At least **90%** of published learning assets have named product and compliance approval, current version and review date.
4. Within 45 days of launch, collect at least 20 structured manager observations across regions (quality of sample, not statistical performance claim).

**Business hypothesis:** Better discovery and product knowledge may improve the accuracy of demos and next-step recommendations. Sales-conversion uplift is **not assumed or measured**.

## Decision brief
- **In:** product positioning, buyer diagnosis, approved features, service comparison, solution-demo flow, safe claims, objection handling, regional adaptation, manager follow-up.
- **Out:** real sales certification, sector_organization-specific specifications, technical deployment instructions, personal-data collection, regulatory assurance claims.
- **Dependencies:** product and security sign-off; sample demo environment; legal-marketing approval of sales language; LMS accessibility check; regional scheduling.
- **Go / no-go:** Sponsor, product, security, regional lead, L&D and operations sign the scope and current claims baseline before learners receive material.
