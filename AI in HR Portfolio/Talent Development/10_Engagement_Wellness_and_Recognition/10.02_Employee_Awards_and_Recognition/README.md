# 10.02 — Employee Awards and Recognition Programme

**Portfolio status:** original strategy/design case with a worked artifact; synthetic clean-energy scenario; not a deployed a clean-energy employer system.

## The business mandate
Technical field achievements receive fewer nominations than headquarters work. The proposed executive intervention is: **Clarify nomination evidence and review awards distribution by function**.

## Project deliverable
- [Open Recognition Equity and Impact Scorecard](WORKING_ARTIFACT.md) — specific decision template, worked synthetic case, implementation gates and validation checklist.
- [Open project data capture form](evidence_capture.csv) — structured sample record for baseline, approval and outcome tracking.
- [Shared case assumptions](../../00_SYNTHETIC_CASE_AND_EVIDENCE_BOUNDARY.md).

## Methods and proposed tools
Annual Awards; employee recognition; nomination calibration. Technology references are proposed integration/assessment options only; current a clean-energy employer systems and software licenses are unverified.

## Outcomes to demonstrate
**Primary KPI:** Recognition reach and perceived fairness. An authentic claim requires a signed baseline, real measured outcome, confounder assessment and owner approval.

## Key risk and guardrail
Do not equate recognition participation with performance.

## Portfolio integration, without duplication
This subsection owns the **decision framework** for employee awards and recognition programme; pre-existing technical prototypes and standalone tools remain in their original directories.
