# Organisation Design, Reward & Workforce-Cost Governance Framework

**Scope:** illustrated governance design accompanying a Python prototype with entirely synthetic data. It is not a production system or evidence of a prior Dubai Holding engagement.

## Control register

| Control | Accountability and evidence | Escalation |
|---|---|---|
| Approved position and headcount budget | Business head requests; Finance confirms funds; Human Capital owns position register | New positions and material budget amendments need authorised approval |
| Organisational design and job architecture | HR/OD owns grade architecture, role profiles and benchmark provenance | Off-band compensation cases require senior reward/Finance review |
| HRIS vs payroll | Payroll preparer verifies HRIS change log, salary components, effective dates and employee status | Stop and reconcile unexplained mismatches |
| Payroll vs Finance | Independent reviewer reconciles gross cash totals and cost-centre classification to Finance | Escalate material variance with root-cause and corrective action |
| Short-term incentives | HR/Rewards validates incentive policy, eligibility, performance source and arithmetic | Unapproved or inconsistent values are held for review |
| Delegation of Authority and maker/checker | Preparers, reviewers and final approvers are distinct and logged | Do not release payments without the correct sign-off |
| Data quality and confidentiality | HR data steward defines fields, access permissions, retention and versioned source evidence | Investigate missing or duplicate records; restrict sensitive detail |
| Audit action management | Control owner retains extracts, approvals, evidence and remediation dates | Track overdue/critical findings and independent closure evidence |

## Suggested monthly senior leadership pack

1. Position register and actual headcount by department, job grade and employee category.
2. Payroll monthly cost by function, cost centre and workforce category, with budget and trend variance.
3. Annualized scenario analysis: proposed positions, redeployment, salary changes, incentive forecasts and affordability assumptions.
4. Salary-band and market midpoint dispersion, with equity assessment and policy exception cases.
5. Incentive payment population, calculations, approval status, exception queue and disbursement control.
6. HRIS / payroll / Finance unmatched items with control owner, severity, closure date and decision.
7. Audit remediation dashboard and outstanding risks.

## Delivery workflow

**Business unit / Finance:** agree approved organisational positions, workforce budget and permissible changes.

**HR operations / Payroll preparation:** validate employee master data and pay components against HRIS effective-dated changes.

**Independent Human Capital / Finance review:** reconcile cost-centre reports, pay exceptions and policy adherence; obtain evidence.

**Authorised approver:** review options and material risks and sign off in line with Delegation of Authority.

**Group Services / Finance:** process only approved payment instructions within agreed timelines.

**Internal audit / control owner:** preserve evidence, assign remediation and verify closure; do not substitute automatic analytic flags for judgement.

## Testing and privacy

Synthetic seeded failures include mismatched HRIS/payroll values, payroll/Finance mismatches, overspending, low salary compa-ratio, missing incentive approval and maker/checker conflict. All require a reviewer to investigate, never automatic correction. Use aggregated leadership reporting and restricted item-level payroll information.

## Statutory and policy boundaries

All benchmarks, band limits and incentive rates are fabricated. The project does **not** calculate actual UAE WPS, pensions, GPSSA, gratuity, employee settlements, deductions, or statutory requirements. Any operational adaptation needs up-to-date legal, payroll, finance and internal policy validation, approved entitlements, tested systems, robust access controls and sign-off.
