# HumanEvoX | HR & People Analytics Research-to-Practice Dashboard Library

**60 individually documented, GitHub-previewable dashboards — original portfolio collection.**

> **Evidence disclosure:** These are **concept demonstrations built using synthetic sample data**. No company commissioned them, no confidential employee records were processed, and none establishes an achieved business result. Published research and proposed metrics discussed by Nicolas Behbahani are cited as topic inspiration; the chart designs, example values and operational workflows are independent HumanEvoX work. This is **not a collaboration, endorsement or reproduction of his graphics**.

## Why this portfolio exists

**Hypothetical client pain points:** HR metrics can overemphasize employee activity instead of value, overlook unequal access to development, fail to cost AI review/rework, confuse AI adoption with competence, and obscure career, job quality, trust and wellbeing risks.

**Methods:** concept mapping from accessible 2026 editions; operational metric definitions; explicit eligible denominators; cohort comparisons; synthetic examples; audit / human review controls; accountable decisions; prospective pilot validation.

**Tools actually used:** GitHub, SVG, HTML, CSS, vanilla JavaScript and Markdown. These dashboards are not Power BI, DAX, Copilot Studio or a live HRIS deployment.

**Result:** 60 original chart artifacts with topic-specific business problem, metric, numerical illustration, decision, technical description, disclosure and original source citation. **No measured client outcomes**.
