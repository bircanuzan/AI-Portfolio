# Head of Talent Development Interview — Presentation Walkthrough

**Independent design exercise. Not a proposal claiming a real company’s undisclosed performance, an Oracle deployment or actual platform access.**

## Suggested introduction (30 seconds)

“I developed two related talent operating reporting designs. The Oracle-first version is the primary case because it shows how I would connect Goal Management, Performance Management, Talent Review and Succession with OTBI operational dashboards, Analytics Publisher executive reporting, and a governed PMR scorecard. The numbers are illustrative. My focus is how executive leaders would identify risks, make decisions and verify results.”

## 1-minute dashboard story
1. **Outcome definition**: annual goals cascade, performance review completion, calibrated talent and succession coverage each have clear eligibility and owner.
2. **Diagnosis**: 62% critical succession coverage against an 85% hypothetical target exposes seven critical roles; critical skills readiness is 71% against 85%.
3. **Action and value**: a funded skill-verification cohort, deputies for critical posts and manager exception closure should yield measurable 90-day evidence; no invented ROI.
4. **Governance**: OTBI source context and security; Publisher formatted reports; finance reconciliation; optional licensed advanced Analytics; executive PMR with no assumed vendor.

## Three-slide speaking points
- Slide 1: “We should assess job capability and goal quality, not only whether annual reviews and learning activity are completed.”
- Slide 2: “I would translate red KPIs into named accountable risk mitigation, prioritizing critical project positions and scarce technical skills.”
- Slide 3: “I would present a decision paper: investment, accountable owner, gate, success evidence and executive review date. Those are demonstration asks, not actual employer budgets.”

## What not to say
- Do not say that you retrieved actual internal employee or talent results.
- Do not assert a named organisation uses Oracle OTBI, Analytics Publisher, Fusion HCM Analytics or Power BI unless independently confirmed.
- Do not imply these reports are tenant screenshots or that the sample Publisher RTF was technically validated.
- Do not label 2025 fictional metrics as actual or current results.

## How to show depth in interview
Ask which Oracle HCM modules are implemented, who owns KPI definitions, which executive PMR cadence is in place, whether Fusion HCM Analytics is licensed, and how Finance-approved programme spend is reconciled. Offer a tenant-validated 30/60/90-day build plan rather than assuming system architecture.
