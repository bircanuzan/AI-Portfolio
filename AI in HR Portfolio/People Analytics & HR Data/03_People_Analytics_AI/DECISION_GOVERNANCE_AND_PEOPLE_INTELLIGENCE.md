# People Analytics & AI | Executive decision framework

**Distinct role of Project 03:** strategic questions, measurable decision rights, value hypotheses, risk appetite, ethical limits and operating model. Original PDF remains unchanged.

## Decision-led analytics charter (fictional UAE diversified employer)
| Executive decision | Business question | Metric owner | Source/evidence | Human decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Retention investment | Where is the departure burden actually elevated? | HR Analytics and HRBPs | Signed workforce exit definitions and time windows | Target service improvements, not individual exclusions |
| Workforce capacity | Are planned hires enough under alternative exit assumptions? | Strategic Workforce Planning + Finance | Project 54 synthetic scenario *method*, production data only after authorization | Approve scenario assumptions and staffing plan |
| Skills strategy | Are capability gaps more critical than open positions? | Talent Management | Validated skill taxonomy and eligible populations | Approve learning or internal mobility programs |
| Digital HR reporting | Which insights should a CHRO see monthly? | People Analytics and Privacy | Project 11 metrics and Project 52 measurement contracts | Approve release, small-group suppression and narrative |
| AI adoption value | What tasks are redesigned, what is verified and what stays human? | AI Governance + OD | Workflow task evidence and risk assessment | Approve human/AI role allocation |

## Classification
- **Descriptive**: What happened? Counts, snapshot averages, flags. Project 11/54 cohort baseline.
- **Diagnostic**: What factors may be associated? Requires stronger sampling and methodology, not causation.
- **Predictive / forecast**: What might happen under assumptions? Requires chronological history, time-aware validation and uncertainty. Project 54 demonstrates **methodology on invented monthly data**, not reliable real-world predictions.
- **Prescriptive**: Which choices should humans consider? Cost, fairness, capacity, constraints and tradeoffs; no automatic employment decision.

## Executive acceptance rules
1. Each decision specifies one population, as-of date, measurement owner, eligible denominator, exclusion policy, and data lineage.
2. A synthetic snapshot cannot become a performance target or employer benchmark.
3. Financial and staffing projections must include low/base/high assumptions; Finance confirms vacancy cost, budget and capacity assumptions separately.
4. When reportable segment n < 10, suppress the segment; also assess complementary disclosure risk.
5. Do not infer protected characteristics, health/ethnicity, dismissal likelihood or individual performance from fabricated correlates.
6. Any personnel or pay decision is reviewed by authorised human leaders with fairness and legal safeguards.

## Decision meeting agenda
**Input:** verified source -> descriptive insight -> forecast/assumption comparisons -> risks/fairness -> recommended experiments -> approval owner -> pilot measurement plan -> follow-up review date.
