# 03 | Employment Contract and Employee Handbook Template Library

**Drafting work samples only. Not legal instruments. All fields, wording and applicability require jurisdiction-specific counsel approval.**

## A. Contract template — controlled clause inventory
| Clause family | Illustrative drafting prompt / placeholder | Mandatory review |
|---|---|---|
| Parties & employing entity | [REGISTERED EMPLOYER], [EMPLOYEE NAME], [EMPLOYEE ID] | Entity existence, signatory authority, locally prescribed format |
| Work location | [BASE LOCATION], [TRAVEL / ASSIGNMENT CONDITIONS] | Immigration, mobility, tax, local rules |
| Role and reporting | [JOB TITLE], [FUNCTION], [MANAGER], [DUTIES ANNEX] | Classification, regulatory/safety roles |
| Commencement and contract type | [START DATE], [TYPE], [END DATE IF APPLICABLE] | Form, fixed-term constraints, probation |
| Working time and rest | [LOCAL HOURS / ROSTER], [REST], [OVERTIME PROCESS] | Statutory minima, aviation fatigue/safety rules |
| Pay and allowances | [CURRENCY], [BASE], [ALLOWANCES], [PAY FREQUENCY] | Payroll, statutory entitlements, benefits |
| Leave and absence | [LOCAL APPROVED SCHEDULE] | Mandatory leave, sickness, family entitlements |
| Confidentiality and data | [LOCAL APPROVED CLAUSES] | Privacy, information security, proportionality |
| Conduct and grievance | [LOCAL HANDBOOK REFERENCE] | Procedural fairness and employee rights |
| Termination and disputes | [LOCAL APPROVED TERMS] | Statutory protection, notice, language, forum |
| Variation and precedence | [LOCALLY REVIEWED CHANGE PROCESS] | Consent and non-waivable rights |

**Important:** This is a *clause checklist*, not a ready-to-sign employment agreement. The actual agreement must be drafted and approved in the correct language, format and regulatory setting by qualified counsel.

## C. Employee handbook — controlled index
1. Welcome, scope, location addenda and where to ask for advice
2. Employment fundamentals and contract precedence (**locally verified**)
3. Ethics, conduct, conflicts, grievance and protection from retaliation
4. Working time, rostering, attendance and aviation safety
5. Leave, benefits and pay — with approved local schedules
6. Equal opportunity, accessibility and accommodations
7. Flexible working, travel, mobility and data security
8. Development, performance, disciplinary and appeal processes
9. Employee privacy, records and monitoring notices
10. Acknowledgments, contacts, approved version and change notices

**Employee acknowledgment sample:** "I acknowledge receipt of the employee handbook, version [x], applicable to my employing entity and work location. This acknowledgment does not waive my statutory or contractual rights." Local counsel must determine whether and how to collect consent or acknowledgment.

## D. Redline example
**Old manager-guide statement:** "All office workers may work remotely two days every week."  
**Proposed policy statement:** "Eligible working arrangements will be assessed against applicable local rights, contracts, business and safety requirements and the approved location addendum."  
**Rationale:** avoid blanket assurance unsupported by a cross-jurisdiction review. **Not a statement that the proposed wording is legally sufficient.**

## E. Controls and audit tests
- Validate authorized template and country/employee-type compatibility.
- Confirm every materially revised clause appears in the signed-off redline.
- Confirm HRIS contract-type/pay fields reconcile to approved template.
- Confirm the handbook version and notice were delivered in appropriate language.
- Preserve employee consent, acknowledgments and consultation evidence where required.
- Lock archived templates to prevent accidental use; retain according to applicable records rules.
