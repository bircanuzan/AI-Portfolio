# Evidence register — no fabricated deployment, measures or results

## Truthful current state

This portfolio project contains a **proposed** adoption framework, readiness assessments, governance templates, risk/control maps and planned evaluations. It is not evidence of a real enterprise pilot, Azure tenant build, production agent, actual HR ticket dataset, ISO certificate or UAE regulatory approval.

Existing Project 43 and Project 56 document **local deterministic policy prototypes and evaluation results**. They are not proof that THIS proposed agentic HR triage process, Copilot Studio environment or enterprise integration has been tested.

## Evidence hygiene

Keep timestamps, exact model/prompt versions and reproducible permitted test fixtures. Redact personal information. Do not present expected test output as observed. Require actual connector/tool traces for security conclusions. Differentiate objective facts from sponsor assumptions, usability perceptions, deterministic prototype results and LLM behaviors.
