# Step 1: PRIORITIZE — Select a high-value, manageable-risk HR agent

## Problem to solve

HR talent teams and managers manually align assessed competencies, employee goals, learning opportunities and mentoring actions into consistent development plans.

## Why this agent is the best *provisional* candidate

Employee-controlled draft IDPs link skills evidence to options and allow easy human review. No algorithm determines potential, promotions, performance ratings or career assignments.

The specific business outcome is **manager and employee time per mutually accepted skill-linked development plan**. Confirm demand with 4–6 weeks of appropriate process/ticket/request metadata, interviews with Talent Development Lead, and a valid baseline, without collecting or exporting unnecessary personal information. Document exceptions and current rework, not just the easy cases.

## Alternatives actually compared

A transparent, **illustrative analyst screen** uses six dimensions weighted: repeat demand/value (25%), bounded agent suitability (20%), source/data readiness (15%), integration feasibility (15%), manageable risk (15%), and measurability (10%). A 5/5 risk rating means *more manageable* risk, not more harm.

| Option | Ratings by six criteria (1–5) | Weighted /100 | Interpretation |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Selected: Competency Gap-to-Individual Development Plan Co-Design Agent | 5, 5, 4, 4, 4, 5 | 91.0 | Hypothesized high business value and controllable autonomy |
| Alternative: Autonomous promotion and succession ranking | 4, 4, 3, 3, 2, 4 | 68.0 | Different risk/control profile; not first deployment choice |
| Alternative: Mandatory AI-generated career assignment | 3, 3, 3, 2, 1, 3 | 51.0 | More exposure or lower bounded value |

**These scores are not real evidence.** Re-score using source quality, actual permissions, actual workforce needs, vendor licensing, costs and privacy/security findings. Perform an independent comparison with ordinary search, reporting or rules-based workflow before committing to an agent.

## Value hypothesis — falsifiable

- **Primary unit:** manager and employee time per mutually accepted skill-linked development plan.
- **Required baseline:** manual process handling time, completion quality, delay, rework and correction minutes across comparable cases.
- **Proposed benefit:** avoid repetitive coordination while improving evidence accuracy and reducing manual rework, subject to human approval.
- **Measured net value:** (baseline human minutes − agent-assisted human minutes including review, corrections, incidents and support) / 60 × approved loaded hourly cost; subtract license, usage, deployment, security and maintenance spend.
- **Important:** freed work capacity is not booked cash savings or job reduction; do not report it as financial realization without separate finance verification.

## Selection gate

Proceed only if (1) adequate recurring demand exists; (2) no simpler controlled process delivers equal value at lower lifecycle cost; (3) approved source/data and limited system permissions are feasible; (4) Talent Development Lead accepts human accountability; (5) privacy/legal and security approve a restricted test; and (6) manager and employee time per mutually accepted skill-linked development plan can be measured. Otherwise hold or select a simpler solution.
