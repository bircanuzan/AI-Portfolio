## Authority envelope

**Approved input candidate:** Approved competency rubric; authorized publisher report metadata; licensed extract rights; assessor notes with permission.

**May perform (subject to actual authorization):** flag missing score fields, mismatched candidate IDs and rubric evidence; draft human assessor briefing.

**May NOT perform:** no test item disclosure, invented normative scores, clinical inferences, proprietary report redistribution or candidate selection.

**Human sign-off needed:** impactful/ambiguous outputs, contested claims, exception decisions, any release, external communication and changes to production authority.
