## Proposed agent

**Human–AI Task Allocation & Capacity Scenario Agent**

### Why this specific HR use case?

**Business problem:** HR transformation teams need task-level process maps, baseline work volumes, responsible-human ownership and credible options for AI, deterministic automation and human work.

**High-value selection rationale:** Agent-assisted planning can summarize task inventories and prepare sensitivity-tested scenarios while leaders retain all decisions on roles, redeployment, compensation and staffing.

**Value to prove:** time and quality of governance-approved scenario planning. The agent must be benchmarked against simpler workflow automation and manual handling; popularity of agentic AI by itself is not sufficient justification.

## Five practical adoption steps

1. **Prioritize:** Compare Human–AI Task Allocation & Capacity Scenario Agent with higher-risk and simpler options, inspect actual HR demand, choose only if controllable value is credible. See [01](01_Prioritize.md).
2. **Design:** Document organizational readiness, authorized sources, identity, tools, permitted actions, human review, escalation and manual fallback. See [02](02_Readiness_and_Design.md).
3. **Pilot:** Test first in an authorized synthetic/shadow environment, then in a bounded real-user cohort, comparing speed/quality/risk with the manual process. See [04](04_Pilot_Evaluation_and_Evidence.md).
4. **Govern:** Map relevant UAE law and guidance and international governance/security standards; require enforceable tool controls, privacy review, fairness safeguards and audit evidence BEFORE exposing users. See [03](03_Governance_Standards_and_Risks.md).
5. **Scale:** Expand capabilities and user groups only after independently reviewed value, reliability and security control gates, signed by accountable humans. See [05](05_90_Day_Gates_and_RACI.md).
