## Application architecture / actual technology

| Function | Oracle technology | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Employee performance documents, appraisals | **Oracle Fusion Cloud Talent Management — Performance Management** | Completion, missing reviews, ratings governance |
| Annual goals and KPI cascade | **Oracle Fusion Cloud Talent Management — Goal Management** | Goal status and strategic alignment |
| Talent-review calibration / nine-box | **Oracle Fusion Cloud Talent Management — Talent Review** | Final meeting completion and moderated talent review |
| Succession and careers | **Oracle Fusion Cloud Talent Management — Succession Management / Career Development** | Critical positions, readiness and development |
| Transactional executive dashboard | **Oracle Transactional Business Intelligence (OTBI)** | Real-time dashboards and report prompts |
| Formatted scheduled executive reporting | **Oracle Analytics Publisher** (formerly BI Publisher) | Monthly / quarterly / annual PDFs and spreadsheets |
| Advanced cross-domain historical analytics | **Oracle Fusion HCM Analytics** via Fusion Data Intelligence | Optional; provisioned **only if separately licensed** |
| Group executive/board reporting | **PMR scorecard and reporting framework** | Consolidated financial/operational reporting; specific software **not identified** |

## Three board questions
1. Are strategic objectives cascaded and performance reviews sufficiently complete to support sound talent decisions?
2. Are critical role successors and verified skills prepared to support growth?
3. Which interventions and funding should be approved, with which 90-day evidence?

**The case requests AED 1.20m for a successor sprint and AED 0.85m for technical capability verification, plus a cost-neutral appraisal governance mandate.** These requests, every KPI and all owners are synthetic. Nothing has been submitted to, approved by or deployed at an actual company.

**Reporting premise:** Oracle Fusion HCM is the *proposed* system of record for this design. Public disclosures alone do not establish an organisation's exact Oracle tenant, enabled products, subject areas, licenses, Analytics Publisher templates, or PMR vendor.
