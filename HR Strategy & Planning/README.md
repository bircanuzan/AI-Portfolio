# HR Strategy & Planning
### Enterprise People Strategy | Strategic Workforce Planning | Talent, Learning & Performance | Governance & Executive Analytics

**Project status:** Independent executive portfolio case study and proposed operating model (November 2026)  
**Context:** Hypothetical UAE regulated banking environment; inspired by published senior HR strategy role requirements.  
**Author:** Bircan Uzan | HumanEvoX Talent Consultancy  
**Evidence boundary:** This is **not** work delivered for Abu Dhabi Islamic Bank or another bank. It uses no ADIB internal documents, personnel data, policies, budgets, or confidential information. The framework is proposed; example KPIs and assumptions are illustrative, not observed institutional performance.

## Executive thesis
A bank-wide HR strategy becomes executable when four related functions — Talent Acquisition, Strategic Workforce Planning/HR Governance, Learning, and Talent Development & Performance — operate to one planning cadence, common people-data definitions and GCHRO-level decisions. Hiring volume alone is not a people strategy: it needs to connect to enterprise capability, succession risk, Emiratization obligations, people cost, control effectiveness and business outcomes.

## Business question
How can a GCHRO govern the right size, skills, readiness and cost of the workforce while strengthening leadership pipelines, fair performance management, employee development and regulatory assurance?

## Target operating model
**Business growth and strategy → critical capability demand → workforce/headcount/cost scenarios → TA and internal mobility → learning and leadership pipelines → performance and succession decisions → executive scorecard and control assurance → quarterly resource reallocation.**

### Six integrated design pillars
1. **Enterprise HR strategy:** 3-year strategic choices, annual planning, quarterly reviews, clear functional ownership.
2. **Strategic workforce planning:** work and role demand, current supply, attrition, productivity, approved positions, cost, sensitivity scenarios.
3. **Talent acquisition and onboarding:** requisition and headcount controls, sourcing, selection quality, inclusion, vendor performance and onboarding.
4. **Learning and leadership capability:** critical-skill taxonomy, needs assessment, academies, LMS data, assessments, mobility, succession readiness.
5. **Performance and talent governance:** goal cascade, calibration, differentiated development, documented decisions, improvement and appeals.
6. **Risk, compliance and people analytics:** UAE workforce requirements and Emiratization-aware reporting; data quality, regulatory liaison, audit logs and decision rights.

## Governance principles
- Executive ownership: GCHRO; delegated operational decisions to relevant HR functional heads with Finance, Business, Risk and Legal challenge.
- Distinguish **planned, approved, funded, occupied and vacant** positions; prohibit conflating them.
- Use a documented employee-data dictionary with effective dates, source systems and reconciliations.
- Review UAE employment obligations, CBUAE expectations and Emiratization applicability with qualified Legal/Compliance specialists; this portfolio is **not legal or regulatory advice**.
- Minimize sensitive HR data, use role-based access, document decision evidence, and require human review of material talent decisions.
- Show no fabricated real-world results: case study KPI targets are proposals and scenarios.
