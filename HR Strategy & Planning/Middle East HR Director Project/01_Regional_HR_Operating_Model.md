# 01 - Regional HR Operating Model (Independent Blueprint)

## 1. Governance and accountabilities
| Workstream | Regional HR Director | Regional Business Leader | Global HR / CHRO | Functional SMEs |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Workforce plan | Leads scenarios, skills and risk assessment | Owns demand and approval | Aligns global policy and budget | Finance, Talent Acquisition |
| Employee relations | Triage and coordinate fair process | Supplies business facts; avoids prejudgment | Escalation per delegation | Legal, Compliance, HSE |
| Compensation | Reviews local competitiveness and anomalies | Inputs performance/criticality | Owns program architecture and approvals | C&B, Payroll, Finance |
| HRIS / records | Owns local completeness and controls | Ensures managers close actions | Owns enterprise system standards | Workday/HRIS, Security |
| Talent/performance | Runs cycle, calibration and succession review | Owns assessment and development | Sets enterprise frameworks | L&D, Talent Management |
| Mobilisation | Tracks employee lifecycle and country obligations | Confirms start and operational readiness | Approves relevant exceptions | PRO, Payroll, HSE, Legal |

## 2. Decision rhythm
- **Weekly:** leadership staffing/critical-role risks; open employee issues; onboarding/mobility; exceptions and high-priority deadlines.
- **Monthly:** headcount vs approved workforce plan; overdue actions; attrition; probation; recruiting cycle time; employee-service SLAs.
- **Quarterly:** succession risk; capability gap reviews; engagement action progress; reward-cycle readiness; workforce scenarios and governance exceptions.
- **Event-driven:** country-entry evaluation, M&A/integration, material ER incident, audit finding, restructuring, HSE-related workforce concern.

## 3. Country-sensitive controls
Maintain legal applicability matrix per country/entity: employment terms, visa/work permit, working time, leave, payroll/WPS where applicable, data privacy, mandatory insurance/benefits, termination, grievances, record retention and local content/nationalisation. **Local Legal/Payroll/PRO confirm current requirements** before implementation.

## 4. Interfaces
Regional CEO/GM and CHRO; HR Operations; TA; Payroll; Global Reward; Finance; Legal; Compliance; HSE; HRIS; immigration advisers. Implement clear RACI, escalation points and maximum response time by severity.

## 5. Operating principles
Business continuity; safety and employee welfare; documentation and fair process; confidentiality; local-law compliance; consistent global policy with approved local exceptions; verifiable data; no AI-only adverse employee decisions.
