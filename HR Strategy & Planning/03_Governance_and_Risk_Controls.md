# 03 | HR Governance, Controls & Regulatory Assurance

**Status:** Proposed control map; review with UAE legal, compliance and banking regulatory specialists before any deployment.

## Regulatory verification checklist
- Confirm applicable Central Bank of the UAE requirements for the specific licensed bank and role.
- Verify current UAE labor law, nationalization rules, local regulatory reporting requirements and contractual distinctions with Legal.
- Map data privacy, security, retention and audit expectations to the bank's actual policy stack.
- Never infer that statutory targets or thresholds are universal: verify entity/sector applicability and effective date.
- Maintain an issues register with accountable owner, evidence, regulatory source, remediation and close date.

## Reporting integrity
A monthly control pack should show reconciled source totals, exceptions, control owner sign-off, open issues, audit actions and prior-period restatements. Executive dashboard data should always be traceable to a source and definition.
