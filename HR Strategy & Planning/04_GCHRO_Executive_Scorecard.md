# 04 | GCHRO Executive Scorecard & KPI Dictionary

**Status:** Illustrative scorecard; no ADIB performance data or bank-approved targets.

## Executive scorecard design
A single monthly/quarterly board-facing view should separate **results, leading indicators, risk and economics**. Every KPI has a numerator, denominator, period, source, owner and exception policy.

| Domain | KPI | Definition | Primary source | Executive owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Workforce | Funded FTE variance | Actual occupied FTE minus approved funded FTE | HRIS + Finance | Workforce Planning |
| Workforce | People cost variance % | (Actual or forecast people cost - approved budget) / approved budget | Finance + Payroll | Finance / HR |
| Talent Acquisition | Time to fill | Days from approved requisition to accepted offer, median and P90 | ATS | TA |
| Talent Acquisition | New-hire 6-month retention | 6-month retained hires / eligible hires | HRIS | TA + HRBP |
| Talent Acquisition | Critical-role vacancy aging | Vacant critical positions in age buckets | ATS + position master | TA |
| Emiratization | Applicable national workforce measure | Jurisdiction/sector-specific approved calculation | HRIS + validated policy | Emiratization lead |
| Learning | Critical skill coverage | Assessed proficient incumbents / role population | Skills + LMS / assessment | Learning |
| Learning | Mandatory learning exceptions | Overdue required learning by risk level | LMS + compliance map | Learning / Compliance |
| Talent | Critical-role succession coverage | Critical roles with assessed qualified successors / critical roles | Talent restricted register | Talent |
| Leadership | Ready-now successor depth | Number of evidence-supported ready-now successors per critical role | Talent review | Talent |
| Performance | On-time cycle completion | Completed eligible reviews / eligible reviews | Performance system | Performance lead |
| Performance | Calibration exceptions | Unresolved policy/process exceptions | Calibrations log | Performance |
| Risk | High-severity overdue HR controls | Open overdue high-severity issues | Risk/action register | HR Governance |
| Adoption | Transformation outcome rate | Realized target behavior / eligible population by cohort | Validated adoption dataset | Programme owner |

## Board-page layout
1. **Executive narrative** — 3 decisions needed this quarter, not 20 unprioritized charts.
2. **Workforce economics** — capacity, cost, attrition, talent supply; baseline, plan, forecast.
3. **Talent resilience** — critical vacancies, succession and skill exposure.
4. **Controls and outcomes** — regulatory exceptions, fair process, learning, performance and transformation.
5. **Decisions and owners** — risk/return trade-offs with named executive sponsors.

## Example escalation logic
- Trigger an **amber review** when an agreed KPI departs from the plan or deteriorates across two periods.
- Trigger **red escalation** for a material control failure, unapproved spending, privacy concern or compliance obligation.
- Thresholds require GCHRO/Finance/Risk approval; none are claimed as ADIB standards.

## Data quality
Denominators must be effective-dated; distinguish employees, positions, FTE and requisitions; exclude duplicate HRIS records; reconcile totals to payroll and Finance. Include a versioned KPI catalogue and restatement log.
