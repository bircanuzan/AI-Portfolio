# 06 | Illustrative Workforce & People-Cost Scenarios

**Status:** Fictional mathematical example for method demonstration only. No real bank figures, grade rates, salary assumptions or employee data.

## Reference case
Assume a hypothetical business function has:
- Opening occupied workforce = 100.0 FTE
- Approved next-year funded capacity = 112.0 FTE
- Expected exits = 8.0 FTE
- Confirmed external hires = 4.0 FTE
- Planned net internal movement into the function = +2.0 FTE
- Assumed average fully-loaded annual people cost = AED 300,000 per FTE (fictional assumption; not a bank benchmark)

Expected closing supply = 100 - 8 + 4 + 2 = **98 FTE**.

Gap to approved plan = 112 - 98 = **14 FTE**.

## Three management alternatives
| Scenario | Intervention | Expected added capacity | Remaining gap | Illustrative annualized cost for added FTE |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A | Recruit 14 FTE | 14 | 0 | AED 4.2m |
| B | Recruit 10 and redeploy 4 | 14 | 0 | AED 3.0m external hiring cost + internal transfer effects |
| C | Recruit 8; redeploy 4; add verified process capacity equivalent to 2 FTE | 14 FTE equivalent | 0 | AED 2.4m external hiring cost + investment and capacity-assurance costs |

**Important:** This is a *capacity*, not profit, illustration. Scenario C is only feasible if service, quality, risk and productivity evidence validates the 2-FTE-equivalent gain. Redeployment does not automatically save enterprise-wide cost; hiring start dates and total annual costs must be time-weighted. Do not add 'saved FTE' to actual headcount or budget without business sign-off.

## Finance-ready sensitivity dimensions
- Vacancy fill lag and offer conversion
- Attrition distribution by job family and grade
- Fully-loaded people cost, training and backfill costs
- Skills productivity and time-to-competence
- Internal mobility and Emiratization obligations
- Regulatory criticality, resilience and controls

## Decision memo structure
For each alternative, compare: demand met, timing, current/future cost, risk, internal pipeline, candidate market, skills development feasibility, applicable regulation, controls and accountable owner. Present the recommended scenario with explicit assumptions, residual risk and next validation step.

## Auditability
Every scenario version should carry model date, input owner, effective dates, data extracts, formulas, assumption register, sign-off trail and confidential data access controls.
