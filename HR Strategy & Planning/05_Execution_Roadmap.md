# 05 | 30/60/90-Day Executive HR Strategy & Planning Roadmap

**Status:** Proposed entry plan for a hypothetical UAE bank; not executed at ADIB.

| Period | Priority | Deliverables | Executive decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Days 1-30 | Diagnose and baseline | Stakeholder interviews, strategy and business priorities, HR operating model map, data lineage, workforce inventory, critical-role list, governance gaps | Confirm top 5 people priorities and sponsors |
| Days 31-60 | Architect and align | Future-state integrated HR planning rhythm, headcount/cost scenario model, enterprise capability heatmap, talent pipeline and succession review protocol, KPI definitions, control map | Approve prioritization, pilots, funding guardrails |
| Days 61-90 | Pilot and institutionalize | One function-level workforce scenario, monthly GCHRO scorecard pilot, critical-role talent review, learning needs and ROI logic, data controls, change communications | Scale or revise based on evidence |

## Sponsorship
- **GCHRO:** overall sponsor, decisions, culture and operating accountability.
- **CFO / Finance:** people-cost definitions, approved funded FTE and scenario challenge.
- **Business heads / HRBPs:** strategic demand and operational adoption.
- **TA / Learning / Talent / Performance heads:** functional execution and service quality.
- **Risk / Compliance / Legal / Privacy:** regulatory interpretation, assurance and controls.
- **HRIS / Analytics:** source quality, automation feasibility and reproducibility.

## Change and stakeholder engagement
- Identify champions across business units and HR functions.
- Use a short message explaining *what changes for whom* and *what is not changing*.
- Train HRBPs and leaders on scenario interpretation, scorecards and approval responsibilities.
- Establish monthly feedback and exception retrospectives with named owners.

## Exit criteria
A viable pilot has approved scope and decision rights, source-reconciled headcount/cost baseline, at least one tested forecasting scenario, documented succession controls and an executive dashboard that decision-makers understand. Label prototypes as prototypes until deployment evidence exists.
