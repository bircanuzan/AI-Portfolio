## Application notes

- **Standards and guidance differ from law.** Legal applicability and compliance require qualified review of the facts and relevant jurisdiction.
- **Runtime control is stronger than a prompt-only rule.** Validate agent permissions in the actual tool-execution path.
- **Evidence must come from actual tests.** This repository has test plans but does not claim observed agent results.
- **Five areas:** The thematic organization in this project was written as an original operational summary, not presented as an exact transcription of inaccessible slide text.
