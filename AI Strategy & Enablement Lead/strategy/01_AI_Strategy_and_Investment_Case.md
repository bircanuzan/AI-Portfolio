# AI strategy and CEO investment case

## North star
Make AI a governed component of the enterprise operating model, rather than a collection of disconnected tools. Two strategic horizons run in parallel:
- **Horizon 1 — Internal productivity and quality:** faster, safer engineering, QA, documentation, product discovery and customer-facing preparation.
- **Horizon 2 — Platform differentiation:** auditable AI-assisted KYC/KYB document intelligence, discrepancy workflows and client-facing intelligence.

## Initial discovery questions
Interview Engineering, Architecture, Product, QA, Commercial, Security, Compliance, Customer Success and select consenting clients. Map **outcomes -> processes -> tasks -> data -> decision rights -> AI vs human allocation**. Capture software delivery lead time, QA escape rate, manual KYB handling time and tool spend.

## Portfolio prioritization (illustrative, not actual norbloc priorities)
| Opportunity | Value hypothesis | Dependency | Starting recommendation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Code assistance | Reduce routine coding and documentation cycle time | secure code policies, IP controls, pilot champions | pilot |
| Test generation | Increase meaningful coverage, reduce regression rework | test baseline and engineer review | pilot |
| Product-spec copilot | Faster requirements and release documentation | approved knowledge store and traceability | pilot |
| Sales knowledge assistant | Shorter response time to technical/RFP questions | approved sources, review and citations | pilot |
| KYB evidence extraction | Less manual field entry, quicker discrepancy identification | privacy review, sample taxonomy, evaluation | design + sandbox |
| Anomaly triage | Improve reviewer focus on inconsistent records | false-positive controls and audit evidence | research, human-led |

## Decision framework
Scored 1–5: expected value 25%, technical feasibility 20%, data readiness 15%, control feasibility 20%, time to validated pilot 10%, change readiness 10%. Keep a separate **hard gate** for unapproved data processing, data residency, legal restrictions, cross-border transfer and unmitigated high-impact decisions; a high score cannot override a failed gate.

## Target business outcomes (proposed, unverified)
1. **>=80% weekly active adoption** among *eligible* technical employees, with opt-outs and approved alternative workflows documented.
2. Engineering and QA cycle-time improvement **demonstrated against baseline**, not claimed in advance.
3. Pilot-backed product feature candidates with documented accuracy, auditability, reviewer workload and error rates.
4. A clear AI spend register, owners, risk classification and quarterly CEO decision log.

## CEO decisions required
- Executive sponsor and cross-functional owners; accountable data / compliance approvers.
- Pilot budgets, vendor shortlist and conditions for approval.
- Which client segments and workflows can be tested under appropriate agreements.
- Explicit **go/no-go** milestones based on experimental evidence and risk thresholds.

## Economics
Use a business case by use case: addressable volume × validated net minutes saved × fully loaded labor rate × realization factor **minus** model/tool spend, integration, review, security and training costs. Separate one-off and recurring costs; distinguish time savings from cash savings and avoid double-counting.
