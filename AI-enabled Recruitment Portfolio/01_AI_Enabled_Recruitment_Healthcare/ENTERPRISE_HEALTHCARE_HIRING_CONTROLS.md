# Regulated healthcare talent acquisition — CHRO operating model

**Project 01 executive extension | Fictional UAE/KSA examples; legal and licensing requirements are not provided or validated here**

## Critical business problem
Healthcare talent acquisition requires evidence of approved patient-safety competencies, clinical scope, service-line workforce demand, professional licence applicability and safe hiring authority. No LLM or automated screening can certify professional registration or suitability.

## Enterprise controls before any sourcing/screening
| Step | Accountable owner | Mandatory output | Escalation / stop rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Clinical workforce demand | Hospital service line + CHRO | Critical role priority, patient-care and staffing assumptions | No approved role → stop sourcing authorization |
| Jurisdiction applicability | Local HR Legal / licensing specialist | Documented country/emirate, licensing authority and version | Ambiguous jurisdiction → hold for legal |
| Clinical requirements | Clinical department head + qualified assessor | Role scope, skills and verified assessment method | Missing essential criterion → no screen |
| Credential provenance | Privileged credential verification team | Authorised source verification protocol and escalation | Cannot infer from a CV |
| Recruitment process & candidate experience | TA operations and patient-care leadership | Accessible fair process, communication SLA, escalation | Harm or privacy risk → service incident |
| Offer and work eligibility | Qualified legal, immigration and HR approvers | Explicit current work-authorisation and contract checks | Unverified legal status → no decision |

## UAE/KSA deployment decision
- UAE and Saudi hiring rules, professional licences, permitted data processing, public-sector requirements and employer approvals **differ**. Do not use one hardcoded general regulation or one universal eligibility score.
- Require named current official source for each role type and hiring location; Project 01's synthetic role source fields remain blank/unverified.
- Clinical verification is a professional specialist function; no autonomous AI statement that someone “is licensed” or safe to practise.
- Review relevant candidate communication, privacy, accessibility and clinical consequences before prioritising faster throughput.

## Executive measures
Vacancy-criticality coverage, clinical source completeness, human credential review delay, overdue approvals, quality of hire **with pre-agreed observational definitions**, candidate withdrawal and complaint themes, qualified assessor capacity, high-severity rule ambiguities and exception age.

A live executive scorecard would use verified HRIS/ATS and clinical onboarding evidence, not Project 07's separate synthetic capacity fixtures.

## 90-day CHRO / CAIO plan
- **30 days:** map critical services and role families; identify jurisdictions and existing recruiter/clinical handoffs.
- **60 days:** validate source-owner governance, specialist escalation, reviewer training and vendor security; remove unnecessary intake rework.
- **90 days:** design a role-readiness pilot with audit trail and human acceptance criteria; retain the HOLD condition until real regulations and staffing conditions verified.
