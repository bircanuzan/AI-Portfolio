## Shared handoff controls
- Mandatory draft fields: source version/id, verified facts versus assumptions, missing inputs, risk flags, named human reviewer and `required_human_review.decision=PENDING`.
- HR/legal/finance/clinical/immigration facts without current trusted source must remain `MISSING`; do not fabricate eligibility or approval.
- Stage owners hold final decisions; recruitment communication drafts are **never sent**, offers **never approved**, candidate records **never written to an ATS** by this portfolio.
- No candidate-level ranking is generated by joining workflow outputs. The separate fictional screening mock is deliberately unsafe and remains HOLD.
- [Project 55's catalogue integrity checks](55_AI_Recruitment_Screening_Risk_Assessment/src/catalog_integrity.py) verify the 17 existing skill files and mandatory prohibited-decision language; they do **not** invoke Claude.
