# 04 | 30 / 60 / 90 Day HR Shared Services Improvement Roadmap

**Status:** Proposed managerial roadmap, subject to organisation access, existing process ownership and validation.

## Days 1-30 | Establish control and baseline
- Map stakeholder ownership across HR Shared Services, TA, PRO, Legal, Finance, Payroll and Operations.
- Catalogue employee-service cases, visa/permit categories, recruitment pipelines and customer journeys.
- Validate current official immigration processes with the competent specialist owners.
- Baseline case volume, first-response time, SLA attainment, upcoming expiries, joiner readiness and recruitment demand.
- Establish a daily risk/priority huddle and a restricted log for open compliance exceptions.
- Identify quick improvements that do not bypass controls.

## Days 31-60 | Standardise execution
- Publish service catalogue, escalation matrix, job aids and checklists.
- Agree measurable SLAs, critical exceptions, repeatable case categorisation and ownership.
- Create an anonymised dashboard for HR leaders and Operations.
- Improve handoffs between ATS, HR Ops and payroll; reconcile joiner and exit statuses.
- Conduct vendor performance reviews with verified turnaround and quality metrics.
- Test new controls on an approved low-risk process segment; record issues and changes.

## Days 61-90 | Improve and embed
- Refine operating model based on case data, frontline feedback and HR Legal review.
- Extend approved workflow controls to additional accounts and employee segments.
- Institute formal monthly service reviews with trend analysis, process defects and priority actions.
- Define next-phase automation opportunities with explicit human decision rights and auditability.
- Establish sustainable ownership, training, procedure version control and control-testing cadence.

## Success criteria (to be agreed; not fabricated targets)
- Reliable joining readiness and clearer exception ownership.
- Fewer avoidable documentation defects and improved visibility of pending permits.
- More consistent recruitment turnaround and employee query service levels.
- Transparent manager reporting and audit-ready governance evidence.
