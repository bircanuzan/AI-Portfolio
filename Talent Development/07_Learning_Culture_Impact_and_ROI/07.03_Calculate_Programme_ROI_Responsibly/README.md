# 07.03 — Calculate Programme ROI Responsibly

**Portfolio status:** original strategy/design case with a worked artifact; synthetic clean-energy scenario; not a deployed Masdar system.

## The business mandate
A technical academy claims savings without isolating effects of training. The proposed executive intervention is: **Quantify eligible benefits and full costs and show attribution uncertainty**.

## Project deliverable
- [Open Finance-Assured ROI Calculation Workbook Spec](WORKING_ARTIFACT.md) — specific decision template, worked synthetic case, implementation gates and validation checklist.
- [Open project data capture form](evidence_capture.csv) — structured sample record for baseline, approval and outcome tracking.
- [Shared case assumptions](../../00_SYNTHETIC_CASE_AND_EVIDENCE_BOUNDARY.md).

## Methods and proposed tools
Phillips ROI; counterfactuals; Finance sign-off. Technology references are proposed integration/assessment options only; current Masdar systems and software licenses are unverified.

## Outcomes to demonstrate
**Primary KPI:** Net programme benefit and qualified ROI estimate. An authentic claim requires a signed baseline, real measured outcome, confounder assessment and owner approval.

## Key risk and guardrail
Record benefit exclusions and non-financial outcomes.
