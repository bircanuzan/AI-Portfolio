# 04 | Programme management from initiation to retirement

## Eight-week sample project plan (hypothetical)
| Week | Workstream | Exit evidence | Gate owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Kickoff, success outcomes, scope and regional sponsor interviews | Charter, objectives, decision log | Global sales sponsor |
| 2 | Baseline TNA, persona/needs assessment and claims inventory | Signed competency map | L&D lead + sales leaders |
| 3 | Curriculum map, learning objectives, delivery modalities | Curriculum approval | Sales sponsor + product SME |
| 4 | Scripts, exercises, demo cards and initial digital storyboard | Content v0.1 + traceability | Content lead |
| 5 | SME validation, compliance review, accessibility and localisation | Approved release candidate | Product/security reviewers |
| 6 | Controlled sample cohort pilot (planned, **not run**) | Pilot survey and observations template | L&D + regional leads |
| 7 | Remediation, LMS metadata and communications | Launch checklist | Training operations |
| 8 | Global phased launch with support channels | Go/no-go record | Sponsor + L&D |

**After release:** Weekly issue triage for weeks 9–12; month-one review of early competency patterns; quarterly source review; maintenance and retirement criteria below.

## Controls and templates
**Change control entry:** request ID, reason, triggering product version, impacted modules, SME, risk severity, approval owner, review-by date, training impact, rollback.

**Risk register examples**
- R1: New product release makes demo inaccurate. *Likelihood medium / impact high*; release-note review, mandatory update within 10 business days of approved changes; owner: product SME.
- R2: Regional learners cannot attend same time zone. *Likelihood high / impact medium*; staggered live cohorts and self-paced alternatives; owner: operations.
- R3: Security statement not approved. *Likelihood medium / impact critical*; block publishing and require security sign-off; owner: security.
- R4: LMS tracking fails. *Likelihood medium / impact medium*; UAT test accounts, event reconciliation and fallback reports; owner: LMS admin.

## Maintenance and retirement
**Review triggers:** material release change, support policy change, compliance advice, learner complaint, product rebranding, or quarterly scheduled review. Publish version notes and re-issue impacted knowledge checks.

**Retirement triggers:** product decommission, superseding course version, content owner unavailable without replacement, no longer accurate claims, or no audience demand after sponsor review. Preserve audit logs, record replacement mappings, withdraw old LMS assignments and notify managers. Never leave obsolete product guidance discoverable as current.

**Evidence boundary:** These are model planning artefacts; no approvals, launch, retirement or live pilots have occurred.
