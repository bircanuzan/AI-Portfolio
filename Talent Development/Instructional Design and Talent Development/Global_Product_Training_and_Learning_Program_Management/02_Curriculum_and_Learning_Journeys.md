# 02 | Modular learning architecture and competency map

## Segmented audiences
- **New account executives:** foundational product narrative and discovery practice.
- **Experienced account executives:** skip orientation after a diagnostic; complete advanced needs analysis and risk-calibrated objections.
- **Channel partners:** partner-approved positioning, scope boundaries, lead handoff and escalation; exclude internal-only pricing details.
- **Regional managers:** observation rubric, coaching and reinforcement; no automatic certification by attendance.

## Job competencies and evidence
| Competency | Observable behaviour | Evidence | Learning component |
|---|---|---|---|
| Needs discovery | Asks open questions and checks use case before pitching | Simulated buyer call | M1 |
| Product and service knowledge | Separates hardware, subscription and support responsibilities | Scenario quiz + explanation | M2 |
| Value mapping | Maps 2 customer pain points to approved capabilities | Value mapping worksheet | M2/M3 |
| Demonstration | Follows approved 6-step story with transitions | Screen/demo walk-through | M3 |
| Security and claims discipline | Flags unverified claims, routes questions to SME | Critical-safety challenge | M4 |
| Objection and next-step handling | Responds honestly and proposes responsible follow-up | Live role play | M5 |

## 5-module syllabus
| Module | Mode | Duration | Outcome | Artefact |
|---|---|---:|---|---|
| M1 Customer context and qualification | Virtual or live workshop | 30 min | Perform 5-question discovery | Discovery canvas |
| M2 ConnectHub and ConnectCare value | Asynchronous microlearning | 20 min | Distinguish products, services and assumptions | Product value map |
| M3 Demonstrating the use case | Guided demo + practice | 35 min | Follow approved demo workflow | Demo checklist |
| M4 Claims, security and escalation | Asynchronous branching scenario | 15 min | Stop unsupported claims | Safety decision card |
| M5 Objections and consultative follow-through | Small-group workshop | 45 min | Handle objection + set next step | Performance assessment |

**Total designed time:** 145 minutes excluding assessments, break time and optional remediation. Product specifications are intentionally fictional; every claim in actual client materials requires approved source mapping.

## Example lesson objective and assessment
**M4 objective:** Given three buyer objections and a versioned claims register, identify which questions the seller may answer and which require the security or product team, with 100% compliance on critical-prohibition items.

**Learning activity:** A customer asks, “Does ConnectHub guarantee compliance with every local privacy law?” Learner selects (A) “Absolutely”; (B) “Our compliance team can confirm supported controls and contractual details”; (C) “We don't need to discuss this.” **B is correct**, pending review against the real vendor's policy.

## Production and localisation brief
Master authoring pack includes text script, slide/storyboard, alt-text/transcripts, quiz answer key, editable SME review comments, accessibility checks and versioned approved claims. Localise examples into region-relevant buyers; *do not* change approved service scope or legal claims. Sign off regional variants individually.
