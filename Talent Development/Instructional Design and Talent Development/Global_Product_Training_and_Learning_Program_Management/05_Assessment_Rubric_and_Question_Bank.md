# 05 | Competency assessment and evidence-based certification design

## Performance task
**Task:** In a 7-minute simulated customer conversation, identify the buyer's needs, match needs to approved product benefits, handle uncertainty and recommend a safe next step.

| Criterion | 0 points | 3 points | 5 points |
|---|---|---|---|
| Discovery | Skips customer context | Asks 2–3 useful questions | Asks 5 relevant open questions and verifies |
| Product / service clarity | Confuses options | Mostly accurate distinctions | Correctly separates hardware, service and limits |
| Value articulation | Generic features only | Links one need to outcome | Links two needs to evidenced outcomes |
| Safe claims and escalation | Promises unsupported outcomes | Recognises risk after prompt | Proactively avoids unsupported claims and routes |
| Next step and communication | No clear follow-up | Partial plan | Summarises needs and agreed evidence/next action |

**Score:** 0–25; for intermediate performance assign 1, 2 or 4 when evidence lies between anchors. **Proposed pass threshold: 20/25 AND zero critical errors.** Critical errors: guarantees zero downtime, asserts universal regulatory certification, shares customer confidential data without approval, invents security controls. Pass is a **portfolio-defined hypothetical standard**, not external certification.

**Calibration:** Two observers independently score at least five sample role plays; review criterion disagreements greater than 1 point before scaling. Record objective behavioural examples, not impressions.

## Five sample knowledge-check questions
1. A prospect asks if every country’s law is automatically satisfied. **Correct:** Consult the approved compliance materials and legal expert; no universal guarantee.
2. Which comes first in consultative product training: feature pitch or understanding the customer's pain points? **Correct:** Clarifying pain points.
3. Which asset controls a product claim? **Correct:** The latest versioned, approved product/claims register.
4. A regional distributor has an outdated slide. **Correct:** Withdraw and replace after review; document the version and notify distributor.
5. A seller has 95% course completion but makes unsupported promises. **Correct:** Do not approve competency; remediate and reassess the safety gate.

## Coaching/remediation workflow
1. Capture observable response and rubric scores.
2. Give learner one strength and one specific improvement behaviour.
3. Assign 10-minute targeted practice and source materials.
4. Reassess using a **different fictional buyer scenario**.
5. Record *completion*, *knowledge score*, *observed behaviour* and *critical error* as separate fields.

## Data ethics
Use learner consent and least-privilege access to scorecards. No automatic employment decisions. Do not use synthetic exercise scores to make real HR judgements. Set retention periods with the real employer; none are implemented here.
