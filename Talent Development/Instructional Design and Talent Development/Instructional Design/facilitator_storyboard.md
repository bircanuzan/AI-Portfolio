# Digital Lesson and SME Facilitation Storyboard

| Minute | Screen / component | Learner does | Facilitator / SME does | Check |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0–3 | Clear ILOs and captioned introduction | Selects accessible format | Frames applied business case | Welcome acknowledgement |
| 3–8 | Interactive before/after prompts | Spots identifiable data | Explains privacy conditions | 3-question diagnostic |
| 8–18 | Worked prompting example | Rewrites fictional HR request | Demonstrates safe content boundary | Feedback against checklist |
| 18–30 | Scenario branching | Chooses correct escalation | Reviews wrong-turn consequences | 2 of 3 branches correct |
| 30–40 | Peer critique + independent artifact | Applies rubric to draft output | Moderates source and quality review | Saved feedback |
| 40–45 | Exit ticket and resources | Records confidence + one gap | Defines follow-up and office hours | Survey response |

## Source materials required
Approved HR policy excerpts, learning objectives, glossary, de-identified prompt examples, two intentionally flawed AI drafts, content review checklist, plain-language help resources.

## Storyboard production notes
Use heading structure, alt text, transcript, captions and keyboard-friendly controls. Media is optional; equivalent textual learning path is mandatory. Any generated images or narration must be reviewed for rights, accuracy, bias and accessibility. No media or course assets are claimed created in proprietary authoring tools.

## SME review cadence
Draft > accuracy review > accessibility review > assessment moderation > UAT > limited pilot > post-pilot revisions > authorized release. Capture reviewer ID/role and approval date in a nonpublic document.
