# Course Blueprint: Responsible AI in HR Operations (Synthetic Case)

## Audience, constraints and needs analysis
Audience: 24 illustrative adult learners; HR coordinators, team leads and system administrators. Prior knowledge ranges from beginner to intermediate. Constraints: mixed devices, varied English proficiency, minimal synchronous time, confidentiality and restricted HR data. All numbers are invented example inputs.

### Personas
- **HR Coordinator:** needs safe prompts and escalation for employee-policy questions; 30 minutes per week available.
- **People Manager:** needs to interpret an AI recommendation critically and retain human decision rights.
- **Learning Administrator:** needs assessment records, accessibility evidence and quality monitoring.

### Performance goal
Within four weeks, learners can apply a documented, privacy-aware process to use AI for low-risk HR content drafting and verify outputs before release.

## Intended learning outcomes (ILOs)
- **ILO1 — Understand:** Explain three constraints on AI use with employee data (Bloom: Understand).
- **ILO2 — Apply:** Construct a well-scoped, de-identified HR content prompt (Apply).
- **ILO3 — Evaluate:** Evaluate the quality, privacy and fairness of an AI response using a checklist (Evaluate).
- **ILO4 — Create:** Produce a revised employee-facing artefact with human review, source attribution and escalation routing (Create).

## Four-module course map
| Module | Learner activity | Evidence | Modality |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Safe AI foundations | Annotated micro-lecture, 6-question quiz, reflections | ILO1 check (80% target) | Asynchronous |
| 2. Structured prompting | Prompt reconstruction, paired practice, feedback | ILO2 prompt file scored by criteria | Blended |
| 3. Critical AI output review | Compare flawed and controlled responses; decision log | ILO3 judgement worksheet | Case-based |
| 4. Applied performance task | Produce a de-identified employee FAQ draft with evaluation | ILO4 artifact + rubric + reviewer sign-off | Authentic task |

## Learning pathways
- Beginner: 12-minute foundational explainer, glossary and example scaffolds.
- Experienced: optional skip-ahead diagnostic with equivalent evidence, advanced fault cases.
- Accessibility: keyboard-friendly document structure, plain-language text, image alt descriptions, transcripts/captions and no color-only instructions.

## Evaluation plan
**Diagnostic** before course (8 short items); **formative** quizzes, prompted revisions and peer checkpoints; **summative** ILO4 authentic task. Readiness threshold: 3/4 across every critical rubric criterion and passing privacy criterion. Final human assessor confirms competency. No fictitious achievement claim.

## Adaptive decision rules
When learner scores under 80% on ILO1 quiz, redirect to guided examples and a new parallel-item quiz. When the ILO3 privacy criterion fails, stop progression and require corrective coaching. Track attempts without publishing individual learner identities.

## Stakeholder roles
Faculty/SME approves outcomes and content accuracy; instructional designer owns alignment and design QA; LMS administrator handles build and access; accessibility reviewer audits learning materials; assessment lead verifies rubrics and moderation.
