# Instructional Design — Outcome-Aligned Digital Learning Lab

**Portfolio type:** Original, synthetic case study and reusable instructional design templates. **No HBMSU affiliation, accredited course delivery, published LMS course, or verified client outcomes are claimed.**

## Business challenge
A Dubai-based smart-learning provider wants an accessible, scalable four-module course titled **Responsible AI in HR Operations**, serving mixed-experience working adults. Design must show academic rigor, constructive alignment and measurable competency gains.

## End-to-end method
1. Discovery: stakeholder interview, performance problem, learner personas, readiness survey and accessibility needs.
2. Backward Design: define authentic final task, assessment evidence, then activities and content.
3. ADDIE: analyse, design, develop, implement (future LMS pilot), evaluate.
4. Constructivism: applied scenarios, reflection, peer feedback and authentic transfer.
5. Bloom's Taxonomy: sequence from identify/explain to apply/evaluate/create.
6. Assessment: formative checks, authentic summative task, four-level analytic rubric.
7. Quality: outcome-assessment mapping, accessibility checks, content review, versioned evidence.
8. Measurement: completion, item performance, proficiency, learner feedback and iteration.
