# 05 | Hiring Controls, Exceptions, Rehire & Contingent Workforce

## Six non-negotiable controls
1. Vacancy belongs to an approved, open, correctly graded, effective-dated position; TA validates before opening requisition.
2. Business leader and Finance verify strategic need and funding; Reward verifies role/grade and parity.
3. Hiring freeze exceptions include essential-business-case rationale, alternatives, incremental annual/partial-year cost, sign-offs and expiry.
4. Unbudgeted positions are reserved matters for CPO/HCC per the fictional delegation matrix; no automatic approval.
5. Rehires pass eligibility and Legal/HR compliance review independently of headcount availability.
6. Contingent suppliers require status, classification, vendor diligence, rate-card, data-access and contract liability checks before engagement.

## Monthly vacancy review template
Requisition ID; approved position ID; vacancy ageing; business criticality; funded status; grade and comparators; recruitment funnel; alternative internal mobility; date-needed; challenge / decision / owner; approval reference; candidate data minimized.

## Fictional delegated authority table
| Scenario | Engine result | Accountable human route |
|---|---|---|
| Funded, correctly controlled and below AED 400k **annual loaded cost** | RECOMMEND_FOR_APPROVAL | Delegated OE approver + Finance evidence |
| No approved position / unbudgeted hire | ESCALATE_HCC | CPO recommendation → Human Capital Committee |
| Freeze exception | ESCALATE_CPO | CPO, Finance and any reserved governance |
| Rehire with legal hold | HOLD_LEGAL | Legal/ER independent clearance |
| Unvetted contingent supplier | HOLD_VENDOR | Procurement + Legal + Finance |
| Any questionable role parity / master data | HOLD_JOB_ARCH / HOLD_MASTER_DATA | Reward or Data Steward |

These are illustrative routing thresholds only, not Dubai Holding policy.

## Cost and compliance
Assess contractor true cost including supplier margin, benefits/taxes where applicable, equipment, supervision, visa/employment classification, data security, conversion/early termination and procurement procurement policies. Compare total cost and risk against permanent employment; no blanket assumption that contractors are cheaper.
