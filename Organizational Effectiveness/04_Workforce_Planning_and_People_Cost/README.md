# 04 | Strategic Workforce Planning, Position Control & People Cost

## Group operating rhythm
Annual 3-year scenario cycle; monthly actual vs plan; quarterly rolling forecast; vacancy reviews with Talent Acquisition; Finance-signed headcount and wage bill. Tie each role to entity, cost centre, grade, approved position ID, role purpose, effective date and funding source.

## Distinct ledgers
1. **Approved positions** — budgeted future and active seats; not the same as occupied headcount.
2. **Occupied headcount/FTE** — employees currently assigned to one controlled position.
3. **Contingent workforce** — contractors, consultants and agency labour separately tracked for classification, supplier and total cost.
4. **Open demand** — authorised requisitions and a separate exception backlog.
5. **Budget** — fixed pay, benefits, statutory employer obligations, incentives, recruitment, contingent cost and one-off transformation spend in distinct cost buckets.

## Example equations
- Planned loaded annual people cost = Σ approved position base salary × (1 + assumed burden %); add variable pay and contingents as separate model lines if reliable.
- Annualised hiring delta = authorised vacancy run-rate × planned fill probability × fraction of financial year employed; not automatically the same as full-year committed cost.
- Demand capacity = Σ service annual volume × standard handling time / productive annual hours, adjusted for skills and realistic supervision/quality.
- Workforce bridge = start FTE + approved hires – forecast exits ± internal transfers. Track exact position and entity lineage.

## Case constraints
Fictional Group approved positions by entity: Corporate 5, Investment 3, Infrastructure 3. The sample contains **9 active position records**, six filled. The budget and grade settings are illustrative, not Dubai Holding costs. The engine explicitly calculates each entity run-rate and flags when the model's approved record count exceeds cap or cost exceeds budget.

## Scenarios to present
- **Base:** hold current positions, fill only approved critical vacancies.
- **Growth:** launch new entity with explicit Finance budget, local law and talent supply constraints, plus phased approvals.
- **Efficiency:** consolidate duplicate service work after quality, risk, employee impact and transition checks, without counting AI productivity twice.
- **Stress:** slower asset/project demand; prioritise redeployment, hiring gates and reskilling.

## Decision pack
Demand/supply waterfall, 12-month rolling wage bill, sensitivity to attrition and benefits rates, critical-skill vulnerability, impact on customer SLAs, proposed decisions with confidence/assumptions. Executive owners decide; the engine provides decision support only.
