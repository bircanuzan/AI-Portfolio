# 09 | CPO, Executive Committee & Human Capital Committee Advisory

## Example decision note (fictional)
**Topic:** central position controls and enterprise role architecture before three-vertical restructure.

**Issue:** Group lacks traceable linkage from business case to approved seat, grade, budget, recruitment and HRIS entry. Sample independent model shows one payroll discrepancy and request cases requiring CPO/HCC, Legal or Procurement review.

**Options:**
A. Maintain vertical practices, with a monthly Group report (faster, weaker standards).
B. Enforce a Group position/JD/grade register and human-approved exception route (recommended baseline; moderate change).
C. Full HRIS workflow automation before data cleansing (rejected initially; automates inconsistent controls).

**Recommendation:** sponsor option B for a controlled 90-day pilot. Implement structural changes only when the appropriate sign-off, legal reviews and change consultation are complete.

## Proposed reserved-matter matrix
| Matter | Decision route | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Routine funded approved vacancy | Delegated OE / Finance authority | Approved position, cost, grade, requisition |
| New unbudgeted position or material restructure | CPO → Human Capital Committee | Multiple options, business case, total cost, legal/reputation risks |
| New entity/local workforce model | Group CEO/CFO/CPO/Legal as applicable | Entity mandate, local compliance, operating model and workforce plan |
| Grade policy exception / parity issue | Reward panel + CPO for material cases | Scoring evidence and comparators |
| Cross-border data processing / high-risk AI | Privacy, Legal, CISO, CPO | Impact assessment, access, testing, rights and audit trail |

No actual delegated authority is conferred by this demonstration.

## Governance forum pack
One-page decision request; option scores and uncertainty; financial base/downside; employee/contractor implications; legal/regulatory checklist; data quality; risk owner and residual risk; cross-entity dependencies; proposed owner/date; motion, decision, abstentions and signed minutes.

## 90-day executive agenda
- **Days 1–30:** CPO/CEO/Finance interviews; position/HRIS/finance baseline, critical risks, design principles, stakeholder map and 10 priority controls.
- **Days 31–60:** pilot role taxonomy/evaluation moderation, monthly position reconciliation, vacancy review and service-catalogue/design options; establish approvals and committee formats.
- **Days 61–90:** two-vertical rollout of controlled position ledger; first costed scenario and quality review; capability handoff; decision backlog and risk dashboard; retrospective.

## Consultants & external specialists
Issue defined SOWs with objective deliverables, independence/conflict checks, confidentiality, acceptance criteria, data protection, cost cap, IP rights and assurance review. External JE and legal advisory remain subject to professional expertise and contractual rights.
