# 03 | Enterprise Job Architecture, Evaluation & Grading Integrity

## Group taxonomy and interfaces
**Job family → subfamily → career stream → level → role → position → person.** Role is a reusable job design; position is an approved, costed seat in an entity; a person may occupy a position but cannot be used as the basis for job worth.

Suggested families: Executive Leadership; Human Capital & OE; Finance & Investment; Portfolio Operations; Infrastructure & Engineering; Technology, Data & Cyber; Commercial; Risk, Audit & Legal. Career streams: Individual Contributor, People Leader, Specialist/Expert, Executive.

## Standard job description schema
Purpose; strategic deliverables; decision authority; financial authority; reporting relationships; work outputs; skills and behavioural competencies; minimum **job-relevant** qualifications; role-specific licences; work context; Grade and evaluation version; effective date; owner/approver; equal-opportunity/privacy check. Separate objective **job requirements** from personal incumbent attributes.

## Illustrative point-factor sandbox — NOT Mercer IPE or Aon
A custom training-only rubric scores each role 0–5 for business impact (35%), problem complexity (25%), expertise (25%) and people/operational leadership (15%); scaled to 1000 points. Fictional reference bands: G4 <400; G5 400–549; G6 550–699; G7 700–849; G8 850–1000. No vendor IP, certified method, external salary benchmark or legal grade determination is claimed.

**Four-eye evaluation workflow:** role owner submits evidence; OE confirms JD and reporting context; Reward independent scoring; Finance checks affordability; moderation panel tests internal comparators; disagreement documented; an appeal and change-history record is retained. An evaluation tool only highlights possible grade mismatch; panel decides.

## Skills and competencies
Role-level: workforce modelling, HCM data stewardship, org design, strategic facilitation, position controls, responsible automation. Proficiency anchors: Awareness → Working → Advanced → Expert, with independently observable work examples. Map skills to selection, internal mobility, performance, succession and training; avoid using generative AI to score people without validated controls.

## Internal parity checks
Compare same-family/similar-responsibility roles, score rationales, approved grades, effective dates, compensation range position and outliers. No race/gender/other protected-group modelling using this synthetic demo. A real pay equity assessment requires lawfully processed appropriate data, qualified reviewers and jurisdiction-specific advice.
