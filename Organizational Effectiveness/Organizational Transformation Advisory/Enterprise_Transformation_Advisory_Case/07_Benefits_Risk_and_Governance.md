# 07 | Benefits Realization, Risks, Executive Governance

## Value hypothesis, not a savings claim
Model outcomes in three buckets: **customer/operations** (cycle time and rework), **people and culture** (skills, manager effectiveness, trust) and **HR service** (response, data quality, employee effort). Financial projections in the option comparison are illustrative assumptions and not independently validated.

Source tracking: [benefits register](data/benefits_register.csv); [design option economics](data/operating_model_options.csv).

### Measurement and attribution rules
- CFO signs off approved baseline, cost definition, one-time change investment and recurring run-rate costs.
- COO confirms process timestamps, customer/quality metrics and safety guardrails.
- CHRO validates employee outcomes and quality of calibration evidence.
- Analytics lead publishes source, denominator, sampling, comparison period and missingness.
- Prefer phased pilots and matched comparator units where feasible; document other initiatives and market effects.

### Enterprise risk register
| Risk | Trigger | Mitigation / decision | Executive owner |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Dual reporting and unclear authority | Escalation backlog > agreed limit | Publish value-stream RACI; resolve via COO | COO |
| Employee trust / restructuring harm | Survey deterioration or grievances | Employee consultation, support and pause on structural move | CHRO |
| Country labor or data compliance | Unresolved statutory approval | Stop country rollout pending local counsel | Country HR / Legal |
| Safety and quality tradeoffs | Higher errors while speed improves | Safety override, pilot rollback, independent quality checks | COO / Risk |
| HRIS / case data corruption | Failed reconciliations or access incidents | Freeze deployment, backup, audit and remediation | CIO / CHRO |
| Unsubstantiated financial benefit | CFO cannot evidence savings | Remove from ROI; label unverified | CFO |
| Capability-score misuse | Individual adverse decision proposed from aggregate score | Prohibit, retrain reviewers, escalate privacy / ethics | CHRO |

## Governance cadence
- **Weekly PMO:** dependencies, active risks, change fatigue, owners.
- **Biweekly design authority:** process/organization choices, controlled exceptions.
- **Monthly SteerCo:** value realization, people impact, country readiness and decisions.
- **Quarterly ExCo/Board:** outcomes, revised economics, risks, workforce confidence and go/stop/adjust decisions.

## Formal gates
G0 mandate and safe access → G1 validated baseline → G2 accepted target design + workforce impact assessment → G3 controlled pilot passed → G4 rollout authorization → G5 benefits verification and BAU handover. An unpassed gate prevents automatic progression.

## Hand-over artifacts
Signed TOM, country legal approval log, decision register, role/RACI change log, capability validation evidence, manager framework, training register, HR service catalogue, dashboard dictionary, owners and control testing.
