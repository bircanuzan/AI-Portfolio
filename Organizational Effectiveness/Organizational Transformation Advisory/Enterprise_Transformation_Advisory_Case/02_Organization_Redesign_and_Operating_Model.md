# 02 | Organization Redesign & Target Operating Model

## Current-state problem statement (case assumptions)
Functional silos within engineering, digital, commercial and shared services each optimize local KPIs; decisions cross three management layers without a single value-stream owner. Country teams use different approval matrices and HR-service routing. Formal validation would require role interviews, position data, process mining and customer-journey timing.

## Alternative model comparison
Source assumptions: [operating-model option economics](data/operating_model_options.csv).

| Option | Model | Pros | Risks | Preliminary view |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Maintain function-led structure with process fixes | Minimal transition cost, continuity | Ownership fragmentation remains | Baseline comparator |
| B | Value-stream matrix with country compliance overlays | Outcome ownership, reusable cross-market service design | Dual reporting ambiguity and manager overload | **Pilot candidate, subject to consultation** |
| C | Fully centralized regional business-service model | Standardization and scale | High employee disruption and local compliance friction | Defer without stronger evidence |

Do not infer cost savings from a new org chart. Annual benefits and restructuring costs are illustrative, exclude attrition and implementation uncertainty, and require CFO verification.

## Target model — Option B proposed
```text
Board / CEO
  ├── Enterprise Transformation Steering Committee (decision forum)
  ├── Customer & Market Growth Value Stream Owner
  ├── Project Delivery & Service Value Stream Owner
  ├── Digital Energy Solutions Value Stream Owner
  ├── CFO: Finance, controls and benefit validation
  └── CHRO: People Strategy and HR Transformation
          ├── Strategic HR Business Partners (value-stream aligned)
          ├── People Centers of Expertise (talent, reward, OD, L&D)
          └── HR Shared Services & Data / HRIS Governance
Country leaders retain delegated legal, regulatory, safety and employment accountabilities.
```

**Decision rights:** Value-stream owners set customer outcomes and cross-functional delivery priorities; functional leaders own technical standards, capacity, careers and controls; country leaders approve local statutory matters; CFO validates economics; CHRO approves people-process design; SteerCo resolves conflicts.

## Design checks and gates
- **Structural checks:** spans and layers are diagnostic signals, not rigid cutoffs; inspect mission complexity and specialist roles before changing reporting lines.
- **Work allocation:** decision rights, handoffs, cross-border interfaces, job-level impact and reskilling capacity are explicitly mapped.
- **Country legal gate:** UAE, KSA and Qatar specialists validate consultations, contracts, localization, employment protections and data transfer implications.
- **Pilot:** one value stream and one HR-service journey, 8–12 weeks, with employee pulse and customer response baseline.
- **Stop rules:** service continuity failure, material unresolved employee impact, deteriorating safety or data privacy incident.
