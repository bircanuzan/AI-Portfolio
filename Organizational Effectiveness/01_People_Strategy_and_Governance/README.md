# 01 | Group People Strategy, Governance & Scorecards

**Mandate:** turn corporate strategy into a documented, owned, financially feasible People Strategy across diversified verticals.

## People Strategy cascade (fictional)
| Group outcome | People objective | Owner | Quarterly measurement | Escalation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Capital discipline | Workforce cost and position controls | Group OE + CFO | Forecast cost vs approved budget and approved/filled FTE | >2% unapproved variance to CPO/CFO |

## Governance model
**Accountable:** Group CPO for strategy; Group OE Director for structural design standard, evaluation governance and workforce decision recommendation; CFO for budget confirmation; entity CEOs for operational needs; Reward for grading parity; Legal/ER/Privacy for law, fairness and safeguards; HCC for reserved material matters.

**Proposed gates:** G0 business case and strategy; G1 position/data baseline; G2 service design and options; G3 reward/Finance/privacy/legal consultation; G4 delegated approver or HCC decision; G5 controlled HRIS and recruitment implementation; G6 post-implementation measurement. Each gate has a signed decision record and named accountable reviewer.

## Group scorecard design
- **Structural integrity:** approved vs actual positions, orphan employees, multiple incumbents, outdated organisation chart, role without family/grade.
- **Workforce cost:** occupied, vacant and committed positions at full-year loaded run-rate vs budget; forecasts by entity and function; contingent cost.
- **Execution:** percentage of DHC-cascaded-style Group People deliverables completed on time, corrective action owner, accepted quality criteria. Here DHC means generic Group-cascaded priorities; no access to sector_organization systems is implied.
- **People:** quality of hire, critical-role bench, internal movement, learning outcomes, employee experience (metrics require valid sources).

## Department budget and portfolio controls
Budget register fields: owner, cost centre, baseline, committed spend, actuals, forecast to completion, approved change, invoice reference and variance reason. Track People Department programme capex/opex **separately** from employee payroll and headcount costs. Refresh monthly with Finance; freeze unapproved spend; approval authority must be delegated in writing.
