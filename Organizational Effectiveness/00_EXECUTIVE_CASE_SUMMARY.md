# Executive case | Group Organisation Effectiveness Reset

## Board-level problem statement
A diversified group has three competing forms of control: (1) vertical leaders propose new roles without consistently referring to approved position IDs, (2) shared-service and vertical HR records differ on incumbency/pay, and (3) Group transformation decisions lack a single owner for structural integrity, workforce economics and job architecture.

## Illustrative baseline (synthetic)
Three entities; 9 position records; 6 incumbents; 3 unfilled posts. A single payroll-to-HRIS base-pay discrepancy has been deliberately injected for the controls demonstration. Financial values are AED and loaded costs use a simple 30% illustrative uplift on base salary (not a market benchmark).

## Recommendations to the Group Chief People Officer
1. Approve a common **position control ledger** and change-request schema before recruiting, with Finance validating budgets and Reward validating grades.
2. Require **three designs** for material restructure: no-change baseline, service consolidation and automation-augmented model, each compared for service outcomes, costs, risk, pay equity, span/layer and employee impacts.
3. Adopt **decision thresholds** separating manager recommendation, delegated approval and escalation to CPO/Human Capital Committee. No algorithm signs approvals.
4. Commission a monthly HRIS–payroll–org structure reconciliation and materiality-based remediation queue.
5. Establish a Group scorecard reviewed monthly and a leadership / Human Capital Committee pack reviewed quarterly.

## Distinctive deliverable
A control system that can answer together: *What work is needed? Which structure is accountable? Which approved job and grade owns it? How many positions and at what full-year cost? What decision rights apply? Does the master data agree? Which human approvals are required?* See workstreams 01–10 and the executable local fixture.

## Decision requests
- Sponsor: approve principles and the Group OE mandate (not a live approval in this scenario).
- CPO/Finance: approve a position master-data owner and financial modelling assumptions.
- Reward: sponsor proper licensed job evaluation where applicable.
- Legal/ER and each entity: validate redundancy, consultation, employment, immigration and cross-border labour-law requirements before any structural changes.
