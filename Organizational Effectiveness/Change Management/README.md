# Change Management

HumanEvoX | A separate main portfolio folder for organisational and HR change management.

## Project

**[Project 48 — Change Management in HR Program](48_Change_Management_in_HR_Program/)** — simulated UAE employee self-service and HR case-management transformation: change charter, stakeholder/role impact and RACI, ADKAR-informed readiness, communications, resistance, champions, 90-day rollout, synthetic adoption KPIs, and executive steering materials.

The project remains independent from **[AI Adoption](../../AI%20Adoption/)**, which focuses on AI enablement, platform training and adoption programme management.

**Evidence integrity:** Fictional case and synthetic datasets; no client engagement, implemented HRIS, observed outcomes or certification is claimed.
