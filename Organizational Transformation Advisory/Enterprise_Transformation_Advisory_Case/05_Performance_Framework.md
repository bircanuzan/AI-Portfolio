# 05 | Performance Architecture & Fair Calibration

## Objective
Translate enterprise strategy into **team outcomes, role contribution, collaboration behaviors and continuous development**. A performance score is not equivalent to an individual’s worth; decisions require documented evidence, fair access to resources and human review.

## Illustrative enterprise balanced scorecard
| Dimension | Weight | Sample measures | Assessment safeguards |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Customer / business outcomes | 40% | Service quality, customer commitments, value-stream outcomes | Outcome attribution and controllability |
| Operational excellence | 25% | Cycle time, safety, rework, compliance controls | No reward for unsafe speed |
| Culture / collaboration | 20% | Joint problem solving, coaching others, learning contribution | Behavioral examples, multiple perspectives |
| Learning / capability development | 15% | Validated future skills, applied learning, knowledge sharing | Equal opportunity and accessible learning |

**Weights total 100%.** These are illustrative defaults; calibrate by job family, level and business realities rather than impose uniform metrics on all roles.

## Strategy-to-work cascade
**Enterprise strategic outcome:** Faster, safer cross-border deployment.
→ **Value stream:** Customer order-to-commissioning cycle with quality gates.
→ **Team:** Reduce avoidable handoffs and rework while preserving safety sign-offs.
→ **Individual:** Evidence-backed deliverables and improvement contribution, limited to controllable responsibilities.

### Operating cadence
- Start of quarter: agreed objectives, behavioral anchors, support needs and check-in schedule.
- Monthly: developmental coaching, project evidence, workload and objective adjustment.
- Quarterly: documented evidence discussion, proportionate calibration checks across teams and countries.
- Year-end: multiple sources of evidence; opportunity for challenge/correction; decisions separated from automated scoring.

## Example behaviorally anchored rating guide
| Level | Evidence-based description |
| --- | --- |
| 1 — Needs focused support | Repeated missed agreed outcomes with documented context and support planning |
| 2 — Meets expectations | Reliably delivers agreed, controllable outcomes and demonstrates expected behaviors |
| 3 — Exceeds | Sustained exceptional contribution and collaborative spillover with verified evidence |
| 4 — Transformational | Creates repeatable system-level improvement, ethical results and capability transfer |

No forced distribution or AI-generated final ratings. Calibrators check role comparability, disability accommodations, leave effects, language and local country requirements. Confidential records are access-controlled.

## Performance-governance deliverables
- Outcome dictionary: definition, data source, denominator, owner and review date.
- Manager check-in quality rubric and coaching playbook.
- Calibration challenge log and appeal workflow.
- Fairness review: distribution trends by appropriate protected dimensions only where legal, authorized and privacy-safe.
- 60/90-day pilot report: quality of evidence, complaints, administrative burden, trust and measured outcomes.

## Implementation decision
Pilot with two functionally different teams and a non-punitive, developmental-only cycle before any link to variable pay, promotion or retention decisions.
