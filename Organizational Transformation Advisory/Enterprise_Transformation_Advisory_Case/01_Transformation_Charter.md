# 01 | Executive Transformation Charter & Diagnostic

## Business mandate
Illustrative sponsor: CEO; accountable executive: CHRO in partnership with COO and CFO. Geography: UAE / KSA / Qatar; baseline headcount assumption: **600**. Workstreams: engineering delivery, digital solutions, commercial, shared services and people.

**Strategic thesis:** Shift from functional handoffs to owned, cross-functional customer value streams without compromising safety, employment protections, country controls or employee trust.

## Synthetic baseline: unverified hypotheses, not observed company data

| Indicator | Assumed baseline | Desired 12-month direction | Owner / evidence needed |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Cross-functional project approval cycle | 18 business days | <= 10 days | COO; workflow timestamp sample |
| Manager quarterly check-in completion | 46% | >= 90% | CHRO; auditable check-in log |
| Critical-role skill coverage documented | 35% | >= 85% | Talent; validated role-skill mapping |
| HR employee-case first response median | 4.5 business days | <= 1 business day | HR Operations; case timestamps |
| Internal fill rate for priority roles | 19% | >= 30% | TA; validated positions and transfers |
| Favorable employee trust pulse | 58% | >= 70% | Independent survey lead; representative sample |
| Voluntary attrition (trailing 12 months) | 16% | <= 12% | People Analytics; denominators and exit reasons |

The numbers above are deliberately invented for training the method. They must **not** be presented as observed baselines or promised results.

## Discovery plan and sampling
1. Sponsor interviews: CEO, COO, CFO, CHRO, country heads and customer delivery leadership.
2. Workforce listening: anonymous pulse; stratify by function, location, level and tenure; avoid releasing identifiable small cells.
3. Evidence pack: strategic plans, process maps, position and vacancy ledger, org charts, role and skill inventories, exit themes, employee case data, KPI definitions.
4. Journey mapping: customer request → proposal → deployment → support; employee request → resolution.
5. Challenge sessions: test the hypothesis against safety, regulations, labor relations, customer commitments and frontline operating realities.

## Design principles / non-negotiables
- Service ownership follows measurable customer value; country statutory accountabilities remain visible.
- No layoffs or individual performance impacts from unverified synthetic scoring.
- Material org changes require finance, legal and employee relations review.
- Employees have opportunities to participate, challenge and request corrections.
- HR and analytics tools support decisions; designated humans approve them.
- Track equity, workload, service continuity, change fatigue and privacy alongside cost.

## Steering decisions required
Approve diagnostic scope, permissions for data access, country legal counsel, employee consultation, design criteria and baseline validation plan. Do not authorize headcount changes or vendor procurement at the diagnostic gate.
