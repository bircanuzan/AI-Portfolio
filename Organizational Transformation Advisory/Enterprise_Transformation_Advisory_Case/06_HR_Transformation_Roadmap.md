# 06 | HR Transformation & Digital Enablement Roadmap

## Target HR operating model
- **HR Business Partners (HRBPs):** accountable for strategic workforce consultation, operating-model change support, talent risks and value-stream business partnerships.
- **Centers of Expertise (CoEs):** own job/skill frameworks, learning, talent, reward, performance policy, organizational development and governance.
- **HR Shared Services:** first-line employee services, case resolution, knowledge articles, service-level commitments and transaction controls.
- **HR Technology / Data Office:** HRIS ownership, data glossary, access roles, auditability, change control, system integration and dashboards.
- **Country HR / legal:** validate employment practice, local privacy, languages, payroll and local compliance.

## Service-design example: employee request-to-resolution
**As-is assumption:** email-based routing, unclear owner, no common status, weak country escalation.
**To-be:** simple intake → tagged service category → accountable first-line case owner → country/legal escalation where needed → employee confirmation → privacy-safe quality reporting.

Process controls:
1. Case classification and priority matrix with clear statutory/safety escalation.
2. Role-based access; do not store sensitive documents in generic channels.
3. SLA start/stop timestamps and exceptions; include employee clarity and quality, not just speed.
4. Data retention, jurisdiction and cross-border transfer review.
5. HRIS UAT with country representatives, sample reconciliation, rollback plan and BAU handover.

## Phased execution
| Time | Transformation output | Gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–30 days | HR maturity and service-journey discovery; current systems and data inventory | Data/privacy authority and baseline validated |
| 31–90 days | Target TOM, service catalog, RACI, data ownership, prioritized requirements | CEO/CHRO/CFO and local legal review |
| Months 4–6 | Case-management pilot, knowledge base, manager toolkit, HRIS quality remediation | UAT, case quality, adoption and rollback checks |
| Months 7–9 | Expand employee services and talent/performance workflows | Service continuity and employee-feedback gate |
| Months 10–12 | Transition to BAU control owners and monthly benefits review | Finance-validated measures and audit evidence |

## HR technology architecture (vendor-neutral)
HRIS employee master (controlled system of record) → secure integration / role-based API → case management and learning / performance services → governed analytics semantic layer → aggregate executive reporting. Oracle Fusion HCM, Workday or SAP SuccessFactors are **technology alternatives**, not systems used or connected by this portfolio case.

## Change and success measures
- Employee effort-to-resolution, first-response and quality, repeat contacts, knowledge article usefulness.
- HR data completeness and reconciliation exception rate.
- Manager adoption of coaching and competency review.
- Country sign-off, segregation-of-duties approval and payroll/control integrity.

## Critical risks
Unapproved personal data transfer, unstable integrations, country policy conflicts, poor case classification, manager burden and inconsistent scorecard definitions. Owner: CHRO + CIO + country legal counsel, monitored via the integrated risk register.
