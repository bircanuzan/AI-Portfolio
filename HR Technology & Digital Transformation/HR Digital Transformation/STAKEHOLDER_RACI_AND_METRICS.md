# Stakeholder model and governance

| Decision | HR Transformation Lead | HR Operations | IT / Security | Finance | Legal/Compliance | Employee representatives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Prioritise service journeys | A | R | C | C | C | C |
| Approve payroll-sensitive redesign | C | R | C | A | C | I |
| Approve personal-data handling | C | R | R | I | A | C |
| Select investment | R | C | C | A | C | I |
| Sign off employee-service release | A | R | R | C | C | C |
| Certify realised business value | R | C | I | A | I | I |

A=accountable; R=responsible; C=consulted; I=informed. Adapt to actual employing entity.

## Operating cadence
- Weekly delivery and risk triage: new blockers, actions, owners, deadlines.
- Fortnightly product and process design review: scope tradeoffs, accessibility, employee feedback.
- Monthly executive steering: budget, dependencies, risk thresholds and benefit baseline.
- Quarterly post-release review: measured impact vs control group or matched historical baseline.

## Transformation deliverable quality checklist
- Each problem grounded in evidence, not vendor marketing
- Each benefit linked to a baseline, owner and method
- Each service handoff has a process owner, data owner and escalation route
- No dependency on an unimplemented AI assistant for minimum viable service
- All high-impact HR decisions retained by accountable humans
