# Diagnostic and target HR operating model

## Baseline discovery protocol
- Interviews: HR Operations, HRBP, Payroll, IT, Compliance, Finance, executives, employee representatives and managers; record consent, themes and contradictory evidence.
- Process inventory: joiner, mover, leaver, HR documentation, payroll enquiry, policy request, leave enquiry and learning support.
- Data sources (proposed): anonymised service tickets, process maps, HRMS event counts, employee surveys, escalations and finance activity-based costing.
- Baseline dimensions: accessibility, handling time, hand-offs, rework, control exceptions, service SLA, data completeness, satisfaction and privacy risk.
- **No real baseline has been collected**; below are hypotheses for a fictional organisation.

## AS-IS problem hypotheses -> TO-BE design decisions
| Current-state symptom | Proposed change | Accountable owner | Evidence needed before rollout |
|---|---|---|---|
| HR requests enter personal email inboxes | One governed service catalogue and ticket intake | HR Services | Sampled tickets, ownership review, privacy assessment |
| Repeated manual status chasing | Transparent request status and SLA tracking | HR Service Owner | Journey research, service pilot results |
| HRMS change tickets lack traceability | Standard business event and change-control handoff | HRIS Product Owner | Signed business rule and access matrix |
| Process exceptions resolved informally | Defined exception workflow, segregated approvals | HR Operations + Finance | Controls walk-through and audit sign-off |
| Operational data fragmented | Approved KPI dictionary and reconciled management views | HR Analytics | Data lineage and metric acceptance |

## Target operating model
1. **Employee front door**: understandable service catalogue and accessible intake channel.
2. **Service operations**: defined work queues, service owners, documented response standards, clear appeals/escalations.
3. **HR technology product ownership**: backlog, integration dependency coordination and change control.
4. **Assurance**: HR, Finance, Legal and Security approval for high-risk workflows.
5. **Insights and improvement**: monthly KPI review, root-cause trends, employee feedback, verified business benefits.
6. **Human decision authority**: AI assistance may be considered later, but employment, payroll and sensitive ER decisions remain with qualified humans.

## Capability maturity rubric (illustrative, not scored)
Levels 1-5 for channel consistency, service ownership, process standardisation, digital adoption, auditability and reporting. A future analyst records baseline evidence and reviewer signoff; do not fill in numbers without interviews.
