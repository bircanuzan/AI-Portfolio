# HR Digital Transformation | Strategy, Operating Model & Value Realisation

**HumanEvoX independent portfolio case | Fictional UAE multi-entity services organisation | Business-side design**

## Business problem
An illustrative 1,200-person enterprise handles HR services across disconnected emails, spreadsheets, HRMS transactions and inconsistent employee-service channels. HR leaders want a prioritised transformation plan that is commercially justified, transparent, inclusive and measurable.

## Scope (and deliberate exclusions)
**This project owns the WHY / WHAT / WHEN of transformation:** value diagnosis, process inventory, target operating model, funding sequence, governance, change readiness and benefits management. It does **not** select a vendor (see HR Technology Selection), design UX journeys (Digital Employee Experience), implement software (HRTech Implementation), build a portal (Employee Self-Service), configure Oracle/Workday (HRIS & HCM Systems), or develop AI agents (Agentic AI Automation).

## Methods and tools
Voice-of-employee interviews, service-volume analysis, AS-IS / TO-BE mapping, value-stream mapping, capability maturity, change impact assessment, prioritisation with weights, dependency mapping, OKRs, HR operating model, governance RACI, Excel/Power BI *specification* (not deployed dashboard).

## Defensible deliverables
- Current-state control and service map, friction hypotheses, cost-of-delay model
- Product-neutral future HR operating model and investment scenarios
- Sequenced releases with go/no-go gates, business owner per benefit, post-launch measurement plan

**Evidence classification:** Original design from fictional inputs. Illustrative headcount, volume and costs are assumptions; no employer implementation, observed savings, paid software, live employee data or quantified real benefit claimed.
