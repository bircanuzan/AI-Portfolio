# Usability and accessibility research — planned, not executed

## Moderated task scripts
| Test | Participant goal | Observable success | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| UX01 | Find required onboarding documents | Correct source identified without external help | Stale policy content |
| UX02 | Request an employment certificate | Correct category and confirmation reached | Overcollection of personal data |
| UX03 | Find case status | Participant correctly explains status and next action | Confusing terminology |
| UX04 | Escalate an urgent HR issue | Appropriate **human** contact visible | Unsafe AI response |
| UX05 | Use keyboard only | Controls reachable, focus visible and order logical | Inaccessible flow |
| UX06 | Use screen reader + text zoom | Form names, errors and confirmation communicated | Hidden validation |
| UX07 | Change language | Meaning consistent, no unreviewed automated legal translations | Misleading policy advice |

## Measurement design
Task completion %, time-on-task, number of requests for assistance, confidence scale, satisfaction, first-contact resolution, inclusive-channel coverage and complaint themes. Exclude unusable trials explicitly and record sample size.

## Exit criteria
- Essential journey stages are understandable to all sampled user segments.
- No unresolved critical accessibility/privacy issues.
- HR policy and service owners approve content and escalation.
- Qualitative research evidence, not hypothetical personas, drives prioritisation.
