# Methodology, Research Attribution, Technology & Result

## Purpose

Create an original, practical collection of people-analytics dashboard examples, one per distinct research concept identified in selected issues of Nicolas Behbahani's *HR & People Analytics Research*. The aim is to show how a consulting team can turn research questions into measurable organizational decisions **without pretending that illustrative numbers are achieved results**.

## Research source

Reviewed accessible editions from **2026-08-23 to 2026-10-25** and the author's **2026-10-28** article. Individual topic pages link the specific original issue. Topics include AI agent governance and real value, skills-based hiring, internal mobility, quality jobs, leadership, career progression, wellbeing, change and human-AI work redesign.

**Attribution boundary:** source authors may have independently proposed similarly named operational metrics. This portfolio does not claim to invent Nicolas's formulas or source research. The charts, explanatory use cases, sample values, program logic and methods are original HumanEvoX additions. No source graphics were copied.

## Business problem — hypothetical

A potential UAE employer / HR consultancy might lack a decision-oriented measurement framework for AI and people transformation, hiring quality, employee experience or workforce skills. This is a **business hypothesis**, not a client discovery finding or paid engagement.

## Research-to-dashboard workflow

1. Identify a distinct question from the newsletter topic and classify it under an HR workstream.
2. Write a problem statement and a testable denominator/eligible cohort definition.
3. Create a neutral KPI specification; avoid causal claims or importing external survey results as if they were local company findings.
4. Generate clearly labeled **synthetic numerator/denominator examples** (or two synthetic comparable cohort rates for gap indicators). Values vary across dashboard topics but are not drawn from the external research.
5. Render a 1200 × 720 original black/gold/white SVG executive visualization for each concept, with a preview-compatible Markdown page.
6. Provide a local interactive explorer with metric selector and adjustable inputs; its graph and formula recalculate in the browser without transmitting data.
7. Add recommendations, human review gates, privacy/consent precautions and prospective validation steps before any employer-facing use.
8. Verify links, source provenance and internal consistency, and avoid duplicate dashboards or borrowed graphic designs.

## Technical tools actually used

- **GitHub connector:** repository writes, version control and read-back verification.
- **JavaScript:** parameterized deterministic example generation, SVG markup creation, local chart calculations, metric editing.
- **SVG:** original native-vector executive dashboards previewed directly by GitHub.
- **HTML/CSS:** offline dashboard explorer UI with filters, accessible select controls and updateable sample sizes.
- **Markdown:** per-concept methodology, user pain points, definitions, tools and evidence limitations.

Not used: live Power BI, Power Query/DAX, Tableau, Copilot Studio, enterprise ATS, employee HRIS, live LLM agent monitoring, real job-posting dataset, private employee surveys or paid research licenses.

## Example value explanation

Every percentage in this collection is synthetic. A generic illustration is *eligible records meeting the criterion / all eligible records × 100*; a gap measure is *cohort A percentage − cohort B percentage* and is reported in percentage points. A change in a risk metric isn't proof an intervention worked. All targets require organizational sign-off. No external benchmark is smuggled into the calculations.

## Deliverable and result

**Achieved:** a documented, visually inspectable set of **60 concept dashboards** with a browsable catalogue, offline scenario editor and auditable sources/assumptions.

**Not achieved:** commercial client impact, change in employee outcomes, prediction accuracy, improved fairness, proven return on investment or model readiness. Nothing here should be represented as a commissioned case study.

## Future credible engagement / pilot

Secure a real prospect's consent and data governance approval; agree baseline and outcomes; run a small comparative pilot; document data-source lineage, exclusions, denominator, reviewer signoff and cohort reliability; track actual results and cost of human oversight. Add a separate measured-results section only **after** supporting evidence exists.

## Privacy / professional safeguards

Health, menopause, financial stress and similar sensitive topics should be analyzed with aggregated anonymous or privacy-protected reporting, not inferred from individual records. Hiring outcomes require valid role-related instruments, fairness/legal review, human oversight and relevant local law checks. Never treat descriptive rate dashboards as diagnostic medical or psychometric tools.
