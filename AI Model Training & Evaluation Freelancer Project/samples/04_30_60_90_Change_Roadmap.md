# 30/60/90-Day Implementation & Change Roadmap — Fictional Case

| Period | Deliverable and prerequisites | Accountable role | Acceptance gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–30 | Confirm scope, access controls, stakeholder map, case timestamps, baseline volumes and current-state SOP | COO Sponsor | Signed baseline and risk register before automation |
| Days 31–60 | Configure sandbox workflow, create scripts and RACI, test exceptions, train champions, evaluate sample cases | Service Lead | Risk/IT sign-off; no unresolved critical data leaks |
| Days 61–90 | Run supervised pilot, weekly KPI review, targeted coaching, report adoption and deviations | COO Sponsor | Go/no-go decision with CFO, COO and Risk approval |

**Dependencies:** baseline before target; risk approval before pilot; training before adoption claims; finance reconciliation before NPV recommendation.

**Change plan:** executive sponsor kickoff; role-based manager briefings; hands-on scenario walkthroughs; champions feedback; weekly pulse and corrections; accountable escalation.

**Metrics and definitions:** median response time (case timestamps), rework rate (reopened cases / closed), exception escalation rate (escalated / total), workflow usage (assisted cases / eligible), audit completeness (approved exception logs / exception count). No value is reported until real baseline captured.

**RAID:** low data quality (Data Owner); scope creep (PMO); unsupported auto-approvals (Risk); adoption resistance (Service Lead). Pause pilot on unapproved data exposure, repeat critical formula errors or bypassed human approvals.
