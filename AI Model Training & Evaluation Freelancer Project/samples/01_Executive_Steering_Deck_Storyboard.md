# Six-Slide Steering Committee Storyboard (Fictional)

**Decision:** authorize or defer a 90-day, $300,000 discovery and control-tested service pilot.

1. **Decision now: fund a bounded pilot, not enterprise rollout.** Provisional recommendation only, subject to sponsor approval and finance verification.
2. **Why act:** five process handoffs create a plausible but unproven delay hypothesis; existing service volume is an assumed 1,800 cases per month.
3. **Three options:** do nothing and measure; limited manual redesign; governed AI-assist pilot. Score cost, risk, measurable learning and reversibility.
4. **Economics:** use workbook-linked NPV, payback and low/base/high adoption. Base financial inputs are hypothetical, not validated benefits.
5. **Controls:** no autonomous exception approval; role-based access; event logging; stop criterion for confidentiality or material error.
6. **Decision request:** authorize the limited budget and named executive sponsor, mandate baseline collection by day 30, review go/no-go on day 60.

**Presenter rule:** when actual measurement is absent, explicitly say so rather than turning scenario assumptions into claims.
