# Project Charter — Consulting Deliverable AI Evaluation

**Objective:** make AI-produced management consulting artifacts reviewable, numerically auditable, and decision-ready.

**Operating context:** fictional mid-market organization "Northstar Services Group" (NSG), examining entry into a second market and automation-enabled service delivery. This is a synthetic training environment only.

## Questions
1. Does the AI identify the executive decision, assumptions, alternatives, and decision criteria?
2. Can numbers be traced from cited data to formulas, sensitivities and recommendations?
3. Are operating-model responsibilities and control boundaries consistent?
4. Can stakeholders implement the change with owners, dependencies and leading indicators?
5. Are fabricated evidence, unearned certainty and unsafe disclosure flagged?

## Deliverable lifecycle
Brief / source pack → baseline output → deterministic validation → human rubric scoring → critical-defect triage → targeted revision → re-score → decision log.

## Roles
- **Scenario owner:** sets fictional brief and acceptance criteria.
- **Evaluator:** independently scores a response; cites evidence and records uncertainty.
- **Numerical reviewer:** cross-checks workbook arithmetic, assumptions and discounting.
- **Quality lead:** adjudicates critical issues and reviewer disagreements.
- **Executive audience:** hypothetical CEO, COO, CFO and CHRO; not actual participants.

## In and out of scope
In: strategy decks, memos, market entry, operating-model RACI, Excel models, synthesis, change plans. Out: proprietary client materials, undisclosed personal data, claims of actual deployed model changes, scraping or real-world investment recommendations.

## Done criteria
Six task briefs, clear acceptance checks, rubric, reproducible script, scenario model, three editable Office samples, four reference consulting artifacts and unit tests. Human judgment remains mandatory for reasoning and evidence quality.
