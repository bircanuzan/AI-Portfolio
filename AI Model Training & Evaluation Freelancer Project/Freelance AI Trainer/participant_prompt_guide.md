# Quick-Reference Prompt Guide

## Template (copy and adapt)
**Role:** You are assisting with [specific business workflow].  
**Goal:** Produce [deliverable] for [audience].  
**Verified context:** [paste only authorized, non-sensitive facts].  
**Constraints:** [length, format, geography, dates, explicit exclusions].  
**Success criteria:** [what correct output must contain].  
**Verification:** Separate verified facts, assumptions and open questions. Do not invent figures or citations.  
**Human approval:** Flag decisions that require a qualified reviewer.

## Weak vs improved
**Weak:** "Make a market report."  
**Improved:** "Summarize only the three supplied synthetic market tables; show changes year over year, cite source row numbers, flag missing periods and return a 5-row table plus three open questions."

## Six-point participant check
- Is the task and audience specific?
- Is the data approved and appropriately anonymized?
- Are constraints and output format explicit?
- Is the model instructed not to invent sources?
- Can a human reperform/check critical figures?
- Is final accountability assigned to a person?

## Responsible use
Check organization policy and tool permissions. Do not enter personal, legal, medical, financial or confidential data without formal authorization. Treat AI-generated content as a draft needing review.
