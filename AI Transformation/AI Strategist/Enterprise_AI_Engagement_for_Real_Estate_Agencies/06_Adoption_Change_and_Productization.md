# 06 | Adoption, Handover and Reusable Enterprise Product Playbook

## Front-line enablement
- Week -2: shadow agents, identify barriers, recruit champions; agree agent override and escalation rights.
- Week 0: train on what AI cannot know, privacy do/don't, approval flow and factuality checks.
- Weeks 1–2: daily office hours; review edits/rejections and monitor workload.
- Weeks 3–6: standardize CRM disposition tags, team-lead coaching and quality scorecards.
- Week 8+: sponsor readout, postmortem, refreshed SOPs and handover to business owner.

## Adoption assets
- **Agent one-pager:** no auto-send; verify listing availability and price; reject uncertain drafts; preserve customer preference; log reason.
- **Team-lead audit:** 10 assisted messages/week/agent stratified by language, channel and tenure; review factuality, timeliness and consent.
- **Operating SOP:** incident escalation, model/provider fallback, approved template updates, audit review, agent access removal.
- **Training test:** pass all five scenarios—no consent, stale availability, unknown price, language mismatch, connector outage.

## Productization decision
Only propose a reusable platform capability if the same user need recurs across >=3 heterogeneous agencies, a standard schema/API can serve at least 80% of use cases without custom code, risk controls generalize, and incremental support cost is viable. Values are **candidate decision thresholds**, not observed measurements.

## Handover checklist
Product owner and client sponsor sign off on API owner, data dictionary, SOP, support RACI, dashboards, retraining triggers, billing/contract boundaries and 30/60/90-day adoption reviews.

## Portfolio reuse
The adaptable work product is an **AI engagement delivery kit**: executive discovery, measurable value hypothesis, two-engineer backlog, controlled experiment, training, governance, commercial-readout template. These templates can be reused across clients without representing that the client named in the vacancy used them.
