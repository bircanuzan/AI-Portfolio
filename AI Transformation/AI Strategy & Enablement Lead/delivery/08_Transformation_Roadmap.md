# Delivery roadmap | First 90 days through Year 1

## Days 1–30 — Discover and govern
- CEO mandate; steering group; RACI; stakeholder interviews across engineering, product, QA, commercial and compliance.
- Inventory software, processes, data classes, current AI use and baseline metrics.
- Establish AI use policy, vendor approval gates, permitted sandbox and evaluation scorecards.
- Select **2 internal process pilots + 1 product discovery concept**, not a company-wide rollout.

**Deliverables:** opportunity backlog, baseline scorecard, data/privacy risk map, approved experiment charters.

## Days 31–60 — Execute evidence-based pilots
- Run coding/QA tasks with reviewer-controlled design and representative synthetic or approved datasets.
- Validate enterprise knowledge/RFP drafting in permissioned sources.
- Prototype KYB document validation, exception handling and review workflow.
- Launch champions cohort, task-specific training and initial Innovation Day.

**Deliverables:** experiment results with control comparisons, decision logs, learning materials, technical / security findings.

## Days 61–90 — Decide and institutionalize
- Publish real usage, quality, security and financial measures.
- Expand only validated low-risk workflows; address teams with low access or uptake.
- Prioritize high-value product discovery and select consenting design partners where appropriate.
- Present CEO steering pack: approved toolkit, controls, cost, benefits, next-quarter investment.

**Exit gates:** no unmitigated material risk; documented owner; representative evidence; fallback/rollback; human accountability.

## Months 4–6
Scale successful developer / QA and commercial pilots; integrate with established SDLC and CRM workflows; build controlled product extraction evaluation set and evidence/provenance UX.

## Months 7–9
Limited opt-in KYB feature pilot only after legal/security approval; monitor model quality per document type and reviewer effectiveness; refine workflow and operational economics.

## Months 10–12
Decision on scaled release based on evaluation and business case; quarterly model/vendor renewal; new capabilities roadmap. **AI-enhanced feature release is an ambition, never a guaranteed outcome.**

## CEO monthly checkpoint
Spend vs budget | weekly active eligible use | accepted work quality | time-to-value | model/security incidents | compliance decisions | next tranche go/no-go.
