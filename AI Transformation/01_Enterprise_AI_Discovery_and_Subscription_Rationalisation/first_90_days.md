# Enterprise AI discovery → measurable first phase

| Stage | Purpose | Outputs | Decision owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Weeks 1–2 | Inventory and strategic discovery | Sponsor goals, workflow baseline, approved AI applications, cost and contract register, RACI, data owners | CEO/Finance/IT |
| Weeks 3–4 | Prioritise and design | Business use-case map, task-level risk matrix, workflow diagrams, security prerequisites, target metrics | Business owner, IT/security, HR/legal |
| Weeks 5–8 | Controlled prototype | Current-state vs proposed-state handoffs, minimal approved knowledge collection, permission tests, exception and review design, UAT scripts | Process owner and data owner |
| Weeks 9–12 | Pilot and decide | Adoption coaching, quality and burden sampling, variance analysis, risk log, finance review, go/no-go dossier | Sponsor steering group |

## Illustrative gating rubric
1. Business value and process owner documented? If no, do not build.
2. Data sensitivity, consent, licensing, residency and access permissions resolved? If no, block.
3. Known source owner, validation interval and citations? If no, restrict to non-sensitive work.
4. Review effort and failure mode measured? If no, only use an offline prototype.
5. Quality and user adoption evidence collected? If no, do not scale.
6. Security incident/permission regression? Pause and remediate regardless of expected ROI.

**Technology choice:** prefer existing approved platform capabilities; add connectors/API services only after security and total-cost analysis. Do not assume native Copilot, Power Automate, Zapier, Make or OpenAI API permissions exist.
