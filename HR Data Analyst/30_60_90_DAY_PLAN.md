# HR Data Analyst — 30/60/90-Day Delivery Plan

Proposed onboarding workplan for a hypothetical HR MIS function. It is not a commitment made by or to ADIB.

## Days 1–30 | Understand, reconcile, control
- Meet divisional HRBPs, HRMS/ITD, Talent Acquisition, Finance/Strategy and reporting governance owners.
- Inventory source definitions and monthly pack versions; record actual versus budgeted versus requested headcount meanings.
- Map staff ID, employment status, division code, effective dates, FTE, costs and Emiratisation grouping.
- Establish 3 reconciliation baselines: employee moves, funded positions/vacancies, and annualized cost.
- Define materiality thresholds, issue ownership, source-freeze and approval/sign-off procedures.
- **Evidence gate:** approved KPI dictionary, baseline extract control totals and data quality register.

## Days 31–60 | Build and validate MIS
- Automate routine joins and crosschecks with locked-down approved platforms.
- Build division-to-group aggregation, variance waterfall and movement bridge.
- Draft monthly headcount variance commentary, manpower request summary and staffing forecast scenarios.
- Develop a governance-approved national workforce metrics view; obtain Legal/Compliance approval for any official definitions.
- Pilot management dashboard; verify it against independent Finance and HR counts.
- **Evidence gate:** signed UAT cases, dashboard metric reconciliations, corrected exceptions and documented reviewer sign-offs.

## Days 61–90 | Stabilize and govern release
- Publish an owner-approved monthly reporting pack and data-quality scorecard.
- Review variance drivers and expected recruitments with workforce planning and Financial Control.
- Produce actionable division reviews and cost / vacancy sensitivities.
- Formalize annual budget cycle inputs, record retention, controlled changes and incident escalation.
- Agree measurable service improvements (timeliness, completeness, reconciled rows, time-to-resolve).
- **Evidence gate:** reproducible monthly close, audit-ready definitions, decision log and handover to authorized reporting owners.

## Example service measures (targets proposed, not observed)
| Proposed measure | Illustrative target | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Snapshot validation completion | Within agreed closing timetable | HRMS data owner |
| Unresolved duplicate employee IDs | 0 at publication | HR analyst / HRMS |
| Reconciliation gap | 0 unexplained material items | HR / Finance |
| Approved funding traceability | 100% of funded allocations | Financial Control |
| Signed group MIS release | 100% of published months | HR reporting manager |
| Data quality incident aging | Trend down vs baseline | HRIS / ITD |

**Dependency:** All actual regulatory report frequencies, templates and definitions require validation against current authoritative instructions and the institution's designated compliance owners.
